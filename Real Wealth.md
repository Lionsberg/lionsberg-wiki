# Real Wealth

**Real Wealth** is what remains when the illusions of money are set aside: the actual capacity to sustain and elevate Life.

It has two inexhaustible components. **[[Energy]]** — which by the first law of the Universe cannot be destroyed, only transformed, and which pours onto Earth daily as pure income. And **[[Wisdom]], [[Knowledge]], and [[Information]]** — which do not diminish when shared, and can only increase: every experiment, every lesson, every story adds to the stock forever. Wealth made of these two cannot be spent down; it can only be grown and better circulated.

By this true measure, scarcity is a design choice of the [[Old World]], not a fact of the Universe. The fiat instruments of the old economy are claims and counter-claims — debt denominated in fear — while the Real Wealth of humanity ([[Our Collective Inheritance]] joined to the daily income of the Sun) already suffices for every family and community on Earth to flourish. This is the sober arithmetic behind the saying that [[We Are All Billionaires]].

[[Time-Energy Accounting]] is how Real Wealth is measured; [[The Commons]] is where it is gathered and held; [[Centers of Distribution]] are how it circulates; and the [[39. The New Economy|New Economy]] is the system that produces progressively infinite Real Wealth for [[All]] — an economy whose bottom line is not profit extracted, but Life elevated.
