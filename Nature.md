# Nature

Nature carries two meanings in one word, and both are load-bearing.

**The Nature of a being** is its essential character — what it most deeply is. The [[Universal Law]] is a reflection of the Nature, [[Mind]], [[Intention]], and [[Spirit]] of the [[ØNE]]; to live in harmony with that Law is to live in harmony with the perfect Nature of the Source ([[Logos]]). Every being likewise has a Nature — the [[Essence]] beneath its habits — and the [[The Sacred Ascent|Sacred Ascent]] is, in one telling, the progressive [[ReMembering]] of one's true Nature and living from it.

**Nature as the living world** is that Essence made visible: the forests, waters, creatures, and cycles of the [[Living System]] — the ØNE's character written in ecology. The two senses meet exactly there: the natural world is not scenery around the spiritual life but its oldest scripture — the place where the patterns of the [[Creator]]'s Nature can be read directly ([[Biomimicry]] · [[Golden Thread]]). What the [[Old World]] treated as raw material, the [[New World]] receives as kin and teacher, with its own [[Voice of Nature|voice]] in the councils that shape its fate.

To act *according to nature*, in the deepest sense, is therefore one act: conforming to one's own true Essence, and to the Living System's ways, and to the Nature of the ØNE — for these are one Nature, expressed at three scales ([[Holarchy]]).
