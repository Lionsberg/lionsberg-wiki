# Connection
Connection is the reading that a live bond is carrying current between beings.
**Felt Sense.** Warmth spreading through the chest; the shoulders lowering; breath deepening without being asked. Eyes meet and hold without strain. The body stops performing and starts transmitting — presence meeting presence, two instruments finding the same key.
**What It Signals.** Connection reports that the channel is open and current is flowing: I am seen, and I am seeing; what moves in me can reach you, and what moves in you can reach me. It is the felt confirmation of [[Right Relationship]] at the scale of two — and, widened, the cell sensing the body it belongs to ([[Superorganism]]).
**Its Gradient.** [[Loneliness]] ← [[Disconnection]] … Connection → [[Belonging]]. Its instrument is [[Empathy]]; its warm horizon is [[Love]]; sustained across a [[Circle]], it matures into [[Belonging]].
**Its Gift.** The nutrient all social beings run on. Bodies heal faster, minds think truer, and courage comes easier inside live connection; every durable [[New World]] structure is built from it, bond by bond.
**Its Distortion.** Counterfeit connection — parasocial feeds, curated performance, a thousand contacts and no current: forms that simulate the signal without carrying it, leaving the [[Old World]] crowded and starving at once. The tell is the aftertaste — real connection nourishes; the counterfeit leaves you hungrier.
**The Move.** Give the one thing the counterfeit cannot: undivided presence. Put the device down, turn the body toward the other, let yourself be reached before managing how you land. Then keep the current flowing — connection lives on exchange, not display.
