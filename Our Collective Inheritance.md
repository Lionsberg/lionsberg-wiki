# Our Collective Inheritance

All the wisdom, knowledge, solutions, and technologies developed throughout history — by every people, in every land, across every generation — are **Our Collective Inheritance**: the accumulated gift of the whole human family, and beyond it, of the [[Living System]] and its Source.

No breakthrough was ever made alone. Every idea stands on ten thousand years of shoulders: language, mathematics, agriculture, medicine, the sciences funded by the people's own labor and taxes, the crafts carried hand to hand through the ages. Every wise, right, true, and good idea belongs, in the end, to Us All — an aspect of our divine origin and legacy.

Much of the inheritance has been captured — enclosed by [[The Giants]], locked behind patents born of public research, weaponized, or simply hidden. The [[New Civilization]] gathers it back: liberated into [[The Commons]], synergized into [[The Prototype]] as an interoperable [[Kit of Parts]], and made accessible to every family and community on Earth through the [[LIØNSBERG Instantiation Hubs]] — with wise gates kept around the technologies that carry existential risk.

Every person who takes up [[New Citizenship]] receives, as a birthright of membership in ØNE Body of Creation, **equal access to the Collective Inheritance** — and the responsibility every inheritance carries: to steward it, add to it, and pass it on greater than it was received.
