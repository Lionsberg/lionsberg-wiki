# Template For Weekly Turn Post

*The Circle's weekly Turn, posted where the body can see it — the heartbeat made audible.*

---

At the close of each weekly gathering ([[Play — Run the Weekly Turn]]), the Circle's tender posts the Turn to the **#turns** channel of [[The Communication Platform]] — and additionally to any other platform where your Circle also lives. The same lines go onto your [[Quest Card Template|Quest card]]'s `turns::` line, which remains the Board's memory of record. 

**The post:**

```
🔥 <Circle Name> — <Season> · Week of <date>

DID: <what we said we would do, and did — and the gap, honestly>
WILL: <what we commit to this coming week>
LEARNED: <one thing worth passing to the whole body>
NEEDS: <what we could use — anyone may answer>
OFFERS: <what we have to give>
```

Two minutes. Honest and done over polished. The gap between said and did is the field of play, never a shame. And when another Circle's NEEDS meets your OFFERS — answer in the thread: that is the body coordinating itself, no center required.
