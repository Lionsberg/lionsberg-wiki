# Paradox
Paradox is the reading at the edge where truth appears two-sided.
**Felt Sense.** The pleasant vertigo of a held contradiction. The mind runs one side true, then the other side true, then both — and something at the center of thinking goes still, the way eyes rest when a stereogram resolves. Lighter than [[Cognitive Dissonance]]'s grind: this strain has a strange spaciousness in it.
**What It Signals.** Paradox reports that the [[Map of Reality]]'s categories have run out before Reality has. Both readings survive every test; the contradiction lives not in the territory but in the map's grid. This is not an error to fix but a doorway to walk through — the precise place where the [[Boundary of Knowing]] marks its own edge. The deepest truths arrive two-sided: free and bound, one and many, dying and living, already here and not yet.
**Its Gradient.** The far end of the map-strain band: [[Confusion]] → [[Cognitive Dissonance]] → paradox. Dissonance demands that one side yield; paradox announces that neither will — the *frame* must. Its wry cousin is [[Irony]]; its resolution feels like [[Wonder]].
**Its Gift.** The invitation to ascend. Every true paradox points to a higher vantage from which both sides are held as one — the view toward [[ØNE]], where the map's opposites are revealed as two hands of a single body. Paradox is the staircase built into the wall.
**Its Distortion.** Paradox used as an excuse to stop thinking — "it's a mystery" deployed to end inquiry rather than deepen it, contradiction worn as sophistication. False paradoxes shelter there too: plain errors dressed in koan's clothing so no one checks the math.
**The Move.** First verify it is real: test each side honestly — many paradoxes are just mistakes. If both sides hold, stop forcing a winner. Hold the two in one attention and ask: *from what height would these be one thing?* Then climb.
