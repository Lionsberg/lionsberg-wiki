# Cognitive Dissonance
Cognitive Dissonance is the strain of holding two readings that cannot both be true.
**Felt Sense.** Grinding. A low internal friction, like gears cut to different pitches forced to mesh — restlessness without object, irritability that spikes strangely when the tender spot is touched. The body knows before the mind admits: something in here does not agree with itself.
**What It Signals.** Where [[Confusion]] reports the map failing to fit the territory, dissonance reports the [[Map of Reality]] at war with itself: a belief and an evidence, a value and an action, two loyalties — each held, and incompatible. The strain is structural, not moral. It means the map has grown honest enough to register its own contradiction, and the pressure will not release until something true gives.
**Its Gradient.** The map-strain family: [[Confusion]] → cognitive dissonance → [[Paradox]]. Confusion lacks a map that fits; dissonance holds two that clash; paradox stands where the categories themselves run out. Untended, dissonance sours toward [[Guilt]] (when the clash is value-versus-action) or [[Anxiety]].
**Its Gift.** The tension *is* the signal that growth is available. Every dissonance marks an exact coordinate where the map can become truer today — if the strain is resolved forward, toward evidence.
**Its Distortion.** Resolved backward: the evidence rejected, reinterpreted, or shot as messenger, so the comfortable belief survives untouched. This is the [[Old World]]'s engine of denial — whole civilizations easing the grind by breaking the gauge instead of fixing the machine. It works, briefly, and costs the [[Truth]].
**The Move.** Feel the grind and refuse the cheap release. Lay both readings on the table in plain words. Ask, as a Sovereign: *which one does Reality vote for?* Update the map in that direction — belief or behavior, whichever was false — and let the strain resolve into [[Coherence]].
