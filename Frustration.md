# Frustration
Frustration is the reading that will is meeting obstruction.
**Felt Sense.** Pressure against a wall. Energy surges toward an aim and cannot get through: hands want to move, breath comes harder, the same attempt repeats with more force and less precision. The body of someone pushing on a door that will not give.
**What It Signals.** Frustration is [[Anger]] at blockage rather than violation. Something alive in us — a purpose, a need, an act of creation — is flowing toward expression, and the channel is dammed: by circumstance, by another's choice, by a broken tool, by our own present limits. Its intensity measures how much the aim matters; its persistence measures how firmly the flow is blocked.
**Its Gradient.** Third band of the heat spine: [[Annoyance]] → [[Irritation]] → frustration → [[Anger]] → [[Fury]]. When the obstruction is judged permanent, frustration collapses toward [[Despair]]; when it is another's doing, it sharpens into anger proper.
**Its Gift.** Location. Frustration is a dowsing rod for dams: it tells you precisely *where* the flow stops, which is the first thing an engineer of any river needs to know. It also confirms the aim is still alive — the dead do not push.
**Its Distortion.** Two, in opposite directions: giving up — abandoning the aim to escape the feeling — and forcing — ramming the same approach harder until something breaks. Both mistake the wall for the whole territory.
**The Move.** Stop pushing; step back until the dam comes into view. Ask: *what exactly is blocked, and is this wall real, or just the only door I have tried?* Then choose: another route, another tool, help, patience — or the honest [[Sovereignty|sovereign]] decision that this aim is not the one.
