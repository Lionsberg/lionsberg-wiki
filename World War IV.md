# World War IV

**World War IV** is the war presently being waged for the future of Earth and its inhabitants.

The numbering tells the true history. [[World War III]] was not averted in 1945 — it began there: the long, undeclared world war that ran from the end of World War II until roughly 2017. It was fought under the name of peace — cold war and proxy war, financial and informational warfare, the capture of institutions, the corruption of food, medicine, and money, the war on [[Consciousness]] itself — a world war precisely because no continent, economy, or mind stood outside it.

Around 2017 it escalated into **World War IV**: the present, intensifying planetary conflict — kinetic theaters spreading and spilling over, joined to open economic, biological, informational, psychological, and spiritual fronts — the war of the [[Keepers of The Caves]] against the awakening of humanity, waged through the [[Collapse]] of the [[Existing Order]] *by design*. Its stakes were named in eighty-eight words in [[Unity Or Death]]: the death or enslavement of every human on Earth, or our uniting.

The Strategy's answer is not to win World War IV. It is to **render it obsolete**: forestalling the omnicidal escalation; [[Progressive Disarmament]] through [[Swords to Ploughshares]] — the phased redirection of the war's own resources into the infrastructure of life; retraining the world's forces to co-create rather than destroy; holding [[Minimum Necessary Force]] for genuine defense within a [[Properly Ordered]] world; and moving the whole population [[FROM the Old TO the New]] faster than the old war can consume it. Every day of the [[10 Year Grand Strategy]] is a day of this race, run within [[The Window]].
