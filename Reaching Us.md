# Reaching Us

*No one plays alone, and no one wanders lost. When you need a human, the path is short.*

---

1. **The one who invited you.** The Game travels hand to hand, so your first door is the hand that reached you. Ask them anything — and know that one day, you are that door for the two or three you invite. 

2. **AURELLIØN, via the Substack.** If you arrived without an inviter — welcome; [the Substack](https://cocreatingheaven.substack.com/) is your inviter. Subscribe, and reply to any letter: replies reach AURELLIØN. 

3. **The orientation gatherings.** Invitations arrive by email through the Substack. Come with your questions — that is precisely what orientations are for. 

4. **The Communication Platform** *(presently Discord)*. The link arrives through the doors above — the membrane opens by invitation, not broadcast. And wherever we gather, a visible **Help Button** lives: press it, and one of Us will find you and do our best to provide assistance.
