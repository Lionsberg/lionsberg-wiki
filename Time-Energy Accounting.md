# Time-Energy Accounting

**Time-Energy Accounting** is the accounting of the [[New Civilization]]: the measurement of [[Value]] in the true currencies — [[Time]] and [[Energy]] — relative to [[The Goal]], rather than in tokens of scarcity relative to profit.

The Universe already keeps these books. Energy enters Earth from the Sun as pure income; life receives it, stores it, circulates it, and builds ever more capable order with it — an eternally regenerative economy running for billions of years without a unit of currency or an hour of debt. Time-Energy Accounting is the conscious adoption of the Universe's own method: accounting comprehensively for the flows of time and energy through the [[Human System]], and measuring every activity by one question — *does this create [[Value]] toward [[The Goal]], or does it create [[Waste]]?*

Held against this measure, the old books invert:

- Vast portions of what the [[Old World]] counts as productive "earning a living" are revealed as **[[Waste]]** — time and energy spent exploiting one another and Creation, with sixty to eighty percent or more of human effort producing nothing that serves life.
- Much of what the old books ignore — raising children, tending soil, healing neighbors, keeping the [[Commons]] — is revealed as the primary **[[Value Creation]]** of the species.
- Every human being's time and energy are recognized as real wealth from the start: to each, an equal baseline share of the income the Universe freely gives.

Time-Energy Accounting is not a distant utopia; it is a progressive transition. It begins wherever a [[Cell]] measures its [[Seasonal Quests]] by throughput of The Goal rather than by revenue, and wherever a [[Commons]] keeps open books of flow rather than ledgers of possession. As it matures — joined to the conversion of our high technology [[FROM Weaponry TO Livingry]] and to abundance shared through [[Centers of Distribution]] — the notion of "money" itself is rendered progressively obsolete, completing the passage from scarcity-by-design to sufficiency-for-all.
