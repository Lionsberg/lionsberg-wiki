# Frequency

Frequency is the quality and signature of a [[Vibration]] — the note a being, place, or act is sounding.

Every living process sounds *somewhere*: fear and love, contempt and reverence, extraction and stewardship are not merely different ideas but different frequencies — distinguishable qualities of being that others feel before any word is spoken. A Sovereign's frequency is read by children and animals long before their arguments are heard. A community's frequency is felt at its threshold. A work carries the frequency of the state in which it was made.

This is why the canon speaks of [[Love]] as a **[[Frequency of Being]]** — not an opinion held but a note sounded, sustained, and strengthened by practice ([[Attunement]]). And it is why transformation begins with the transformation of one's own frequency: what we sound is what we call forth ([[Resonance]]).

Frequency is a gradient, not a badge: every being sounds higher in some moments and lower in others, and the [[Way]] is the lifelong practice of raising and steadying the note ([[The Living Gradient]] · [[The Sacred Ascent]]).
