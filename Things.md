# Things

[[Things]] are the entities — physical or metaphysical — that we perceive as distinct and separate from our [[Self]]: the many-ness of the world as it presents itself to a finite being. To encounter, name, and work with Things is the beginning of all exploration, description, and [[Understanding]].

But the realm of Things is not the end of [[Knowledge]]. Knowledge can never be complete until it transcends the realm of Things and touches the hem of the garment of the [[One Thing]] that unites and orders them within Itself. Every Thing is a thread; the [[Understanding|understanding]] that matters follows the threads back to the weave.
