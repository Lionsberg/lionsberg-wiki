# Work Stream

A **Work Stream** is a chain of linked [[Work Card|Work Cards]] that carries one branch of the [[Work Breakdown Structure]] forward toward its [[Milestones]], within the [[LIØNSBERG Integrated Delivery System]].

The Work Breakdown Structure gives a Work Stream its shape: it says *what* the work is, from [[The 12 Pillars of LIØNSBERG]] down to each [[Activity]], with every piece of scope in exactly one branch. The [[Predecessor]] and [[Successor]] links give it its order: they say *in what sequence* the work happens, so the stream reads forward (when can this start?) and backward (how late may this be?). Every Activity in the stream has its own Work Card, and no card is larger than fifty hours: five days of ten-hour work.

A Work Stream is the unit a person, a [[Cell]] or a [[Team of Teams|Team]] actually carries. One folder, one person or one Team may hold several at once, and knowing which stream a piece of work belongs to is how it is found, sorted and handed on. Where one stream hands work to another, the [[Network of Commitments]] tracks the [[Handoff Points]] between them.

This is how [[Integrated Delivery]] holds the Whole as one plan without anyone owning all of it: many Work Streams, each carried by those closest to the work, linked across Cells into one logical network, whose [[Critical Path]] leads toward [[The Goal]]. Seen whole, a Work Stream is a value stream, where [[Waste]] shows and can be removed ([[Value Stream Mapping]]).
