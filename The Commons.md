# The Commons

The Commons is what belongs to All — the shared infrastructure, resources, services, knowledge, and living systems held in trust for every member of the whole, and for [[Future Generations]].

Some wealth is rightly personal; some is rightly held in common, because everyone depends on it and no one should be able to enclose it: water and air, roads and grids, the accumulated knowledge of the species, the [[Living System]] itself. The canon states the knowledge-half plainly: *all True ideas belong to The Commons* — the body of thought and progress is a shared inheritance, given forward freely ([[Transmission]]).

The [[Old World]] told one story about shared things — the [[Tragedy of the Commons]] — and used it to justify enclosure: fence it, sell it, let owners save it from users. The [[New World]] answers with the older, truer pattern: commons flourish when they are *stewarded* — governed by the communities that depend on them, with clear agreements, active care, and consequence for abuse ([[2.5. The Pattern for Stewarding the Commons]] · [[Nurturing the Commons]] · [[Stewardship]]). The tragedy is not sharing; it is sharing without stewardship.

Continuously improving The Commons is core work of the whole endeavor: every gift into it — a tool, a teaching, a restored watershed, a kit part — compounds for everyone, forever ([[Kit of Parts]] · [[The Prototype]]). A civilization is finally measured less by its private fortunes than by the richness of what it holds together.
