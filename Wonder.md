# Wonder
Wonder is the reading that Reality has gladly exceeded the map.
**Felt Sense.** Opening. The mouth softens, the eyes widen, the breath drops in of its own accord. Time slows without stopping. The body's whole posture says *oh* — not grasping, not fleeing, simply receiving.
**What It Signals.** Wonder reports that the [[Map of Reality]] has been exceeded — and that the excess is good. Where [[Fear]] reads the [[Unknown]] as threat, wonder reads it as gift: the territory has just proven larger and stranger and more beautiful than the map allowed, and nothing precious was lost in the proving. It is the feeling of the [[Boundary of Knowing]] dissolving pleasurably, the child's native reading of a world not yet flattened by familiarity.
**Its Gradient.** The opening band: [[Interest]] → [[Curiosity]] → Wonder → [[Awe]]. Beneath it runs [[Delight]]; beyond it, where the marvelous turns vast, wonder touches [[Reverence]].
**Its Gift.** Wonder keeps the world from going flat. It reopens the child's eyes inside the adult, restores the sparrow, the rain, the other person to their true strangeness. A map held in wonder stays alive — humble, porous, still growing. It is [[Curiosity]]'s fuel and [[Truth]]'s welcome.
**Its Distortion.** Wonder outsourced to spectacle: needing ever-bigger marvels, ever-louder screens, while the sparrow goes unseen. The [[Old World]] sold wonder back to people it had first numbed. Manufactured awe escalates; real wonder needs only attention.
**The Move.** Stop. Let the *oh* complete itself before naming or photographing anything. Then look again at one ordinary thing — slower — until it gives up its strangeness. Wonder is less found than permitted.
