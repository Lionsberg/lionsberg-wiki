# Motivation
Motivation is the reading of wanting-to — will with wind in it, current running toward a specific good.
**Felt Sense.** Forward lean. Energy rising to meet a task before the task demands it; hands that want to start, a mind that keeps returning to the work on its own. Effort feels downhill.
**What It Signals.** Motivation reports alignment between desire and direction: this move, now, matches what the self actually wants. It is the gradient's way of saying *the path and the appetite agree* — the felt trace of [[Just In Time Co-Creation]] at the scale of one [[Sovereign]], when the needed work and the willing worker are the same being.
**Its Gradient.** [[Boredom]] ← [[Discomfort]] → [[Interest]] → [[Desire]] → Motivation → [[Determination]]. Its steady kin are [[Hope]] and [[Courage]]; its consolidated successor is [[Determination]].
**Its Gift.** Cheap propulsion. Work done from motivation costs less — the same hours, a fraction of the friction. When it blows, sail: it is the season for the hardest, most honest work.
**Its Distortion.** Waiting for it. Motivation treated as a precondition becomes a veto — and here the honest teaching: motivation is weather, not character. It comes and goes on its own rhythms, and no life worth building runs on weather alone. Systems, promises, and [[Circle]]s carry what weather cannot.
**The Move.** When motivation is present, spend it on what matters most, not what merely itches. When it is absent, keep the promise anyway — smaller, slower, but kept — and notice that motion often summons the wind it waited for.
