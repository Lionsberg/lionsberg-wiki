# Observable Universe

The [[Observable Universe]] is the subset of the Universe as such that is visible from our vantage — a sphere extending out in all directions, with the observer at its center, bounded not by any edge of Reality but by the finite reach of light, perception, and time relative to where we stand.

Its deepest lesson is [[Perspectival]]. If we were to move our observation point halfway out along the radius of that sphere, the entire observable sphere would shift with it: half of what we now see would vanish from view, and a new half would emerge. What we call "the universe" is, in the strict sense, *our* local universe — the territory as disclosed to one vantage. The Universe as such extends beyond every observable horizon, conceptually without limit, teeming with cultures, civilizations, and forms of life beyond our present [[Perceive|perception]].

The Observable Universe is thus the cosmic-scale demonstration of the humility every [[Map of Reality]] requires: even our largest view is a view *from somewhere* — vast, real, and partial all at once.
