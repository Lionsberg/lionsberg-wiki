# Creation

Creation is all that is created — the total living work of the [[Creator]], and the ongoing act by which it continues.

The word carries both at once, and both are meant. As the *work*: Creation is [[All]] — every being, world, and scale of the [[Living System]], the entire [[Holarchy]] within which we live and move ([[All Creation]]). As the *act*: Creation is not a finished event receding into the past but a continuing process in which conscious beings participate — we are co-creators, and every day's choices add to what is made ([[Co-Creation]] · [[The LIØNSBERG Cycle of Cosmic Co-Creation]]).

Right Relationship runs through the whole triangle: [[Creator]], Creation, and the creature — honoring the [[Source]], tending what flows from It, and taking up one's own creative part. The relative value of any individuated aspect of Creation is read on the gradients — its depth in the holarchy, its [[Conformance To The Ideal]], its [[Relative Potential]] ([[Total Nested Holarchy of Ideals]]) — while its *dignity* is not graded at all: everything that is, is a subarticulation within the nested wholeness of ØNE ([[The Holarchy of the ØNE]]).

To co-create well is to create as Creation itself creates — by pull, in response to real need and ripened opportunity, in the [[Way]] the ØNE creates through the apparently discrete parts of ItSelf ([[Just In Time Co-Creation]] · [[Biomimicry]] · [[The Creative Cascade]]). The [[New World]] is not an exception to Creation. It is Creation, continuing consciously.
