# Interest
Interest is the reading that something at the edge of the known is worth approaching.
**Felt Sense.** A lean. The body tilts almost imperceptibly toward — eyes settle, breath quiets, the background dims a shade. Nothing dramatic: just attention gathering itself around one point like water finding a channel.
**What It Signals.** Interest is the gentlest of the threshold readings. It reports that the [[Map of Reality]] has noticed an edge — a place where the [[Unknown]] shows just enough of itself to promise more — and that this edge, of all the edges available, may repay approach. It is the first stirring of [[Curiosity]], the seed of [[Wonder]]: the map's quiet vote for where it wants to grow next.
**Its Gradient.** One rising band of approach: Interest → [[Curiosity]] → [[Wonder]] → [[Awe]]; its absorbed deepening is [[Fascination]]. Its opposite pole is [[Boredom]] — the reading that no live edge is currently in view.
**Its Gift.** Allocation of the scarcest resource there is: attention. A life is finally made of what it attended to, and interest is the instrument that chooses. Followed honestly, it is a compass toward one's own growth.
**Its Distortion.** Interest can be captured. The [[Old World]] built an economy on harvesting exactly this signal — engineering endless artificial edges so the lean never resolves into learning. Captured interest feels like interest but leaves the map unchanged.
**The Move.** Notice the lean before something monetizes it. Ask: *is this edge mine — will approaching it grow my map?* If yes, follow it all the way to learning. If not, withdraw attention as a [[Sovereign]] spends gold.
