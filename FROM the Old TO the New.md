# FROM the Old TO the New

FROM the Old TO the New is the great crossing — the movement of humanity, within a single generation, out of the collapsing [[Old World]] and into the consciously co-created [[New World]].

It is a vector, not a place: the direction that orients every project, plan, and practice in the [[LIØNSBERG System]]. The [[Critical Chain|Critical Path]] runs along it; the [[10 Year Grand Strategy]] schedules it; the [[Meta Project]] is its total program of action — to move every family, community, and institution across, leaving no one behind by design.

The crossing is fractal. A Sovereign crosses it in dozens of small irreversible acts ([[The Boundary]]); a community crosses it as its systems are transformed, replaced, or built anew ([[Instantiation]]); a civilization crosses it as an Exodus — measured, provisioned, and led, not merely hoped for. At every scale the same three motions braid: building the [[New]] from the ground up, transforming what can be carried over, and releasing what cannot ([[Inbreaking And Displacing]]).

And at every scale the same law holds: the Old does not need to be defeated so much as *out-created*. Energy spent against the Old is mostly wasted; energy spent building the New — pockets of coherence that connect, demonstrate, and draw — moves the whole crossing forward ([[Islands of Coherence]] · [[Cultivating The Green Shoots]]).

FROM and TO name the whole of it: where we actually are, honestly read, and where we are actually going, clearly aimed — with the whole [[Way]] between them walked one step at a time ([[Navigate]]).
