# Determination
Determination is the reading of will consolidated — the aim judged worth the cost, and the self gathered to pay it.
**Felt Sense.** Jaw set, spine long, breath steady and low. Not the flare of excitement but the banked fire that outlasts it: the body organized around one direction, distractions falling quiet at the edges. A weight that feels like strength.
**What It Signals.** Determination reports that the will has finished deliberating: the gradient has been read, the price counted, and the whole self has signed. It marks a promise made inward — the felt substrate of Move Four: *say what you will do; do what you said.* Where determination is present, capacity has aligned with [[Purpose]]; the work can now draw on reserves that mere preference never reaches.
**Its Gradient.** [[Interest]] → [[Motivation]] → [[Commitment]] → Determination → [[Resolve]] → [[Grit]]. Its threshold companion is [[Courage]]; its long-horizon ally is [[Hope]].
**Its Gift.** Carriage across the valleys where inspiration dies. Every worthy aim passes through stretches with no wind; determination is the keel and the oars — it moves the work when nothing else is moving.
**Its Distortion.** Rigidity: determination aimed past the point where the aim stopped being right. The set jaw becomes a locked one; persistence becomes refusal to re-read the world. Sunk cost wears determination's face.
**The Move.** Name the aim aloud and the cost honestly. Then row — and on a rhythm you choose in advance, lift the oars and re-read the gradient. Persist by choice, never by momentum.
