# Share Your Stories

**Share Your Stories** is the fourth of the five moves of [[The Great Game of LIØNSBERG]]:

**A [[Superorganism]] is emerging. [[Accept The Invitation]]. [[Form A Cell]]. [[Choose A Quest]]. Share Your Stories. [[Pass The Flame]].**

A [[Quest]] completed in silence blesses one place once. A Quest completed and *told* blesses every place it reaches. Stories are not the record of the work — they are part of the work:

- **Stories are the proof.** Every [[Stories of Transformation|Story of Transformation]] is living evidence that the Way works — a doctor's free clinic, a farm feeding its neighbors, a Cell that crossed its first season together. One true story outweighs a hundred pages of promise.
- **Stories are the fuel.** People do not join arguments; they join stories they can see themselves inside. Each story shared draws the next players in, and the flywheel turns: play → transformation → story → new players → play.
- **Stories are the coordination.** The [[Superorganism]] has no headquarters. Cells find their next wise action by reading the traces other Cells leave in the shared field — what was tried, what worked, what is needed, what is possible. Shared stories *are* that field. This is how ants and forests coordinate: not by command, but by trace.
- **Stories are the memory.** Told into the [[Commons]] and woven into [[The Story of LIØNSBERG]], they become the living chronicle of the crossing — how this generation walked out of the Old and built the New, kept for every generation that follows.

The practice is simple. At every new moon and full moon, and at the close of every season: tell what happened. Plainly, honestly, in your own voice — the victory, the struggle, the lesson, the need. Where you were. What you did. What it made possible. Who you are becoming.

Your story is not decoration on the Great Game. It is your move.
