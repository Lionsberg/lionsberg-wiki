# MindWeaving

[[MindWeaving]] is the practice of weaving finite minds together into shared understanding — the living process by which what no single vantage can see becomes visible to the whole.

Recognizing the inherent finitude of each individual, the deepest acts of collective navigation — [[OmniSpection]], shared [[SenseMaking]], the continuous updating of our common [[Map of Reality]] — can only be done [[In Community]], and therefore require MindWeaving. [[The Great Game of LIØNSBERG]] calls for all Co-Creators to engage in it periodically: minds meeting in good faith, perceptions compared, maps corrected against one another, understanding woven strand by strand until it holds. It is how Reality is made intersubjective, and how We keep [[Navigating Towards Truth]] together from [[Where We Are]] to [[Where We Are Going]].

MindWeaving is also a literary craft: the careful weaving of great minds and works — the wisdom inheritances of humanity — into the living body of LIØNSBERG, honoring each source while binding its threads into the larger weave. The MindWeaving wiki books are worked in this way, thread by thread, as living studies rather than finished summaries.

In both registers the discipline is the same: no thread is erased, no voice is collapsed into another, and the weave is stronger than any strand alone.
