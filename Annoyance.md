# Annoyance
Annoyance is the reading that something small does not fit.
**Felt Sense.** A flicker of heat, quickly gone or quickly returning: a slight tightening at the brow, a small exhale through the nose, attention tugged sideways by the thing that grates. The body registers friction the way skin registers a splinter — minor, but real.
**What It Signals.** Annoyance is the first band of the [[Anger]] family: a [[Boundary|boundary]] brushed rather than breached, a value scuffed rather than trampled. Something in the environment — a noise, a habit, a small breach of form — is out of tune with how life wants to flow here. Often it also reports on the reader: a tired or overloaded instrument registers friction sooner.
**Its Gradient.** The band's opening note: Annoyance → [[Irritation]] → [[Frustration]] → [[Anger]] → [[Fury]]. Left unread and repeated, it does not stay small — it accumulates downstream.
**Its Gift.** Early detection. Annoyance finds misfit while it is still cheap to fix — a word tool for the smallest crossings, so they can be named before they compound. Most large ruptures were once annoyances no one voiced.
**Its Distortion.** Enthroned, it becomes chronic pettiness: a reader tuned only to flaws, spending sovereign attention on lint. Refused, it becomes the drip that fills the [[Resentment]] cistern.
**The Move.** Notice the flicker and ask two questions: *is this about the thing, or about my state?* If the thing: name it lightly, early, without ceremony. If the state: rest the instrument. Either way, spend it small — that is what the small band is for.
