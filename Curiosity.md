# Curiosity

The value of curiosity represents a commitment to learning and exploring. It means being open to new ideas and experiences, and having a desire to learn and understand more about the world around us.

## As a Felt Reading

**Felt Sense.** The lean-forward. Attention lights up and reaches; the [[Unknown]] shifts from threat to invitation; time disappears into the question. **What It Signals.** Curiosity reports an edge of the [[Map of Reality]] that is ready to grow — [[Interest]] ripened into pursuit. It is the appetite of understanding: the felt form of the drive that carries all learning, all science, all [[Navigate Towards Truth|navigation toward Truth]]. **Its Gradient.** [[Interest]] → Curiosity → [[Wonder]] → [[Awe]] — the opening spine of the threshold family. Its shadow neighbor is [[Boredom]], the signal that curiosity is starving. **Its Gift & Distortion.** The gift: the Map grows only where curiosity walks; a curious being cannot be made a prisoner of one story. Distorted, it becomes prying (curiosity without reverence for another's [[Sovereignty]]), or novelty-chasing that samples everything and digests nothing. **The Move.** Follow the genuine question — the one that keeps returning; ask it aloud in [[Circle]]; stay long enough to digest what is found ([[Emotion]]).
