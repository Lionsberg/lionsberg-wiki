# Information

Information is pattern made communicable — that which informs [[Energy]] how to manifest.

In the deepest register, Information flows from the [[Logos]]: the ordering pattern of the [[ØNE]] gives rise to Information, and Information instructs Energy into form — the DNA that animates a body, the word that shapes an act, the plan that becomes a dwelling. Creation runs on this current: Energy is the capacity; Information is the instruction; [[Life]] is their marriage.

Information has an altitude ladder, and confusing its rungs is costly: **data** (raw readings) → **Information** (data patterned into meaning) → [[Knowledge]] (information integrated by experience) → [[Wisdom]] (knowledge rightly aimed toward [[The Goal]]). The [[Old World]] drowned in the lower rungs while starving on the upper; the [[LIØNSBERG Intelligence System]] exists to climb the ladder deliberately — turning the flood into shared [[Understanding]] the whole body can act on.

And Information is a commons discipline: in Integrated Delivery, information is shared transparently and freely — including real-time issues and concerns — because a body whose cells hide their signals cannot coordinate ([[Trust]] · [[Minimum Standards of Interoperability]]). Hoarded information fragments the [[Superorganism]]; circulated, it becomes the nervous system of the [[New World]].
