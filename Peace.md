# Peace
Peace is [[Coherence]] at rest — [[Right Relationship]] holding, at any scale: within a heart, between beings, among peoples, across the Earth.

It is simultaneously a felt reading, a way of being, and a civilizational aim: the [[New World]] is characterized by Peace, [[Abundance]], [[Justice]], and [[Harmony]], and the reign of Peace and Justice on Earth is among the oldest articulations of [[The Goal]] itself ([[119. Building Constructive New Peace and Security Systems]]). The same pattern at every scale — the holofractal law: there is no peace between peoples that is not built of peace within and between persons.

## As a Felt Reading

Peace is the reading of coherence felt as stillness — not the absence of motion, but all motions in harmony.
**Felt Sense.** Ground. A stillness with weight and warmth, deeper than quiet — the body resting on something that does not move. Breath free, heart unguarded, face soft. Even in a full room, even in motion, a center that is not disturbed. The deepest still band the instrument can sound.
**What It Signals.** Peace reports [[Coherence]] at depth: the parts of a self, a relationship, a [[Circle]], a world, moving without contradiction — nothing at war, nothing suppressed to keep the quiet. It is the felt signature of [[Right Relationship]]: with oneself, with others, with what is. Where peace holds under contact, the gradient says *this is how it is meant to fit together* — a standing taste of [[Heaven Here and Nøw]].
**Its Gradient.** [[Calm]] → [[Tranquility]] → Peace. Its bright twin is [[Joy]] — the same coherence read as song rather than stillness; its floor of sufficiency is [[Contentment]]; between beings it appears as trust.
**Its Gift.** The reference tone. Peace is what everything else is tuned against — the felt criterion of resolution. Negotiations, decisions, reconciliations are finished not when argument stops but when peace arrives; until then, something is still unreconciled, whatever the signatures say.
**Its Distortion.** Counterfeited, peace becomes conflict avoided rather than transformed — the false peace of the unsaid, quiet purchased by suppression, "keeping the peace" while the war goes underground. The [[Old World]] signs many such treaties. Real peace can hold conflict in the open and metabolize it; the counterfeit shatters when truth enters the room.
**The Move.** Do not fake it and do not rush it — make peace, in the old strong sense: speak the unsaid, repair the relation, finish the reconciliation, then receive the stillness that confirms it. Guard it nowhere by silence. And where it is real, build there — peace marks ground that will hold.
