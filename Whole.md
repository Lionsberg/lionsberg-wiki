# Whole

A Whole is a living unity — something that is genuinely one, not merely a heap of parts.

Wholeness is perceived before it is analyzed: the [[Right Hemisphere]] receives the Whole directly, and the [[Left Hemisphere]] then differentiates it into parts for handling ([[Categorization]]). The discipline is the return: having used the parts to [[Map]], we come back to the Whole with better [[Understanding]] — never mistaking the parts-list for the living unity it describes ([[The Living Gradient]]).

Every Whole is also a part ([[Holon]]): the cell whole within the organ, the Sovereign whole within the [[Circle]], every Whole flourishing *as itself* precisely by participating in the greater Whole — up the entire [[Holarchy]] to the ØNE, within Whom [[All]] inter-exist. Even sacrifice serves this pattern: as cells sometimes yield at the DNA level for the wellbeing of the greater Whole, so beings freely give for what exceeds them — freely, never by command ([[Higher Order Functional Unity]]).

And wholeness is a gradient, not a status. A [[Personality]] becomes whole as its competing drives integrate into functional unity moving toward one worthy aim; a community becomes whole as its members cohere without ceasing to be themselves ([[Coherence]]); a work becomes whole when nothing essential is missing and nothing foreign remains ([[Process For Filling Up What Is Lacking]] · [[Process For Removing What Does Not Belong]]). To heal — at every scale — is to make whole again; the words are one word.
