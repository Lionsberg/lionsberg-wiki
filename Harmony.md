# Harmony

Harmony is diverse Unity sounding together — many distinct notes, one music.

It is never uniformity: a single note repeated is not harmony but monotone, and the [[Old World]]'s enforced sameness was harmony's counterfeit, not its achievement. True Harmony requires difference — voices genuinely distinct, sovereignly themselves — brought into relationship so right that their differences enrich rather than clash ([[Coherence]] is the state; Harmony is its music and its beauty).

The canon names Harmony among the marks of the [[New World]] — characterized by [[Peace]], [[Abundance]], [[Justice]], and Harmony — and among the deepest attributes of the [[ØNE]]: the perfect diverse [[Unity]] and Harmony that resides at the end of the [[Logic of Love]]. It is what the [[Total Nested Holarchy]] sounds like when it is well: every scale in tune with every other, [[Individuals]] with [[Circle|Circles]], Circles with [[Community|Communities]], Communities with the [[Living System]], All with ØNE.

Its laws can be learned ([[10. The Laws of Harmony]]): harmony arises around a clear [[Fundamental Tone]] — the truer the center, the more freely the voices can differ without fracture; it is restored not by silencing the dissonant voice but by deepening the shared root ([[Right Relationship]]); and it is practiced before it is achieved — in the body's equilibrium, in the Circle's dialogue, in the daily craft of moving with rather than against ([[1.13 Harmony, Equilibrium, Right Relationship, Flow, Oneness]] · [[Attunement]]).
