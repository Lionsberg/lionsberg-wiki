# Driver

A [[Driver]] is the compelling need, condition, or calling that will not let us rest — the force to which a purposeful response must be born. Every genuine undertaking traces back to one: a system is built, a [[Quest]] is chosen, a work is written *in response to* something real that demanded it.

In the craft of purposeful organization, the Driver is articulated as the one sentence everything else hangs from: *what in our place will not let us rest — and what do we therefore exist to bring into being?* From the Driver flows purpose; from purpose flow the seasonal [[Quest|Quests]]; from Quests flows the work of Tuesday morning. The gap that kills most good intentions is the one between a beautiful purpose and daily action — and a clearly named Driver is what closes it.
