# The Decision Log

*The Game's record of decisions as they are made — so nothing is re-litigated without new understanding, and anyone can always ask why.*

---

A decision unrecorded will be re-litigated. So the Game keeps this log: one line per decision, dated, with the why. Held as current best understanding — a logged decision can be reopened, but only *forward*: with new understanding, through [[Reaching Us]] or [[The LIØNSBERG Workshop|the Workshop]], never by forgetting. Every Circle and body is invited to keep its own decision log the same way *(templates in [[The LIØNSBERG Toolkit|the Toolkit]]'s Forge)*.
