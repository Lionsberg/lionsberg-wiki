# Blue Belt

*Integrated embodiment — the Body recognizes a fully participating member.*

---

**Blue Belt in [[The Way Of The ØNE Body]]** is the recognition that a Sovereign has become a fully participating, integrated member of the Body across the scales they play in — roughly three years, a dozen seasons, of witnessed questing, commitments kept, harvests told, and flames passed ([[The Arc of Play]]). 

**Why Blue Belt comes before deeper maturity in the system:** words can be performed, and even sincerity can be mistaken — for a few years. Embodiment witnessed across seasons and flames cannot. The Blue Belt is [[Structural Immunity]] in temporal form: maturity in the Body is never claimed, bought, or self-declared — it is *seen*, season after season, by the Circle, the Elders, and the Body, and then confirmed. [[The LIØNSBERG Belt System]] holds the full logic in one place: belts are granted long after observation; false belts cannot hold. 

Until Blue Belt, a Player plays fully — questing, taking Turns, sponsoring the two or three they invite — while the deeper trusts of the Body ripen with the belt.
