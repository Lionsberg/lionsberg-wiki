# Terror
Terror is fear at full flood — the instrument overwhelmed.
**Felt Sense.** Whiteout. Heart slamming or stopped, time dilated or gone, the body seized by one of its oldest programs: flee, fight, freeze. Thought is not consulted; it is bypassed. In terror the animal takes the wheel, because milliseconds matter.
**What It Signals.** Terror reports mortal-scale threat — to life, or to what the soul holds as life: a child in danger, the self facing annihilation, the ground of the world giving way. It is the band's extreme: [[Fear]] escalated past the point where reading is possible, where signal becomes seizure.
**Its Gradient.** The far end: [[Fear]] → [[Dread]] → Terror. Beyond it lies not a stronger emotion but shutdown — freeze and dissociation, the fuse blowing to protect the circuit.
**Its Gift.** Raw survival. Terror's reflexes have carried life through fire for millions of years, summoning speed and strength the deliberating mind could never assemble in time.
**Its Distortion.** Terror is honest in its moment; its distortion lives in the aftermath. Unmet, the flood leaves a watermark — trauma — and the alarm re-fires at echoes of the hour, reading past danger into a present that is safe. And terror deliberately inflicted is the [[Old World]]'s oldest instrument of rule.
**The Move.** In the flood: survive — the practices come later. Afterward: come back slowly, in safety and in company. Terror is not metabolized alone; it asks for a witness, a [[Circle]], sometimes a skilled guide. Let the body finish the shaking it began, and teach the alarm, gently and often, that the hour has passed.
