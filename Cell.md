# Cell

A **Cell** is the smallest complete unit of a living body — the unit that carries the whole.

In every living thing, the cell sustains itself, does its work, and brings forth new cells — not in isolation, but within the body of which it is a part. It draws from the whole and gives back to the whole. And within its nucleus it carries the complete code of the entire organism: though nearly all the body be lost, a single living cell retains everything needed to regenerate the whole.

- **It replicates.** The mark of a mature Cell is not its size but the new Cells it has helped birth. ([[Self-Replicate Your Circles]])
- **It interexists.** Cells federate into communities, forge [[The 300]] of their localities, and weave the emerging Superorganism — many cells, ØNE Body.
