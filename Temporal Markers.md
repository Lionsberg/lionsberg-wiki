# Temporal Markers

**Temporal Markers** are the dates, milestones, and schedules we consciously set within the illusion of [[SpaceTime]] to orient and manage our work — the surveyor's stakes of [[The Transformation]].

They are instruments, and instruments only. A marker exists so that a body of people can pull a plan backward from [[The Goal]], walk the [[Critical Path]] toward it, measure variance honestly, and consume contingency knowingly rather than drift blindly. The [[10 Year Grand Strategy]], the [[20 Year Generational Contingency]], the seasonal turnings of the [[LIØNSBERG Calendar]], the [[LIØNSBERG Cycles of Growth]] — all are Temporal Markers: chosen, revisable, and in service.

Three laws govern their use:

1. **The markers serve the work; the work does not serve the markers.** When a marker is missed, the marker did its job — it made the lateness visible. The response is ignition and honest accounting, never despair, never quiet re-baselining, and never the sacrifice of the wise right path to hit a date.
2. **Markers are not The Window.** [[The Window]] is set by reality and may close at an unknown time; markers are set by us. Managing to markers while discerning The Window — through continual sense-making and [[OmniSpection]] — is the double vision the work requires.
3. **Markers bind bodies, not Spirit.** The deepest currents of the Transformation move through consciousness and grace, beyond [[SpaceTime|the Illusion of SpaceTime]] in which the markers are staked. No one knows how non-local Spirit accelerates what no schedule could — which is why markers are held firmly enough to steer by and lightly enough to be outrun.
