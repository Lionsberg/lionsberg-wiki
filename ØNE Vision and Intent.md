# ØNE Vision and Intent

*The invariant above every plan — the one thing that never changes, which everything below it serves.*

---

**[[The Goal]]: ØNE · Creator's Intent · Heaven — here, now, everywhere, for everyone, forever.** 

This is the Game's Commander's Intent: the **why** is invariant; every **how** is current best understanding, revisable the moment a better way serves the Intent. Plans, decisions, tools, seasons, structures — all of it below this line may change, and should, as understanding deepens. This line does not. 

The compass that carries the Intent into every move: ***Does this make life a little more like Heaven?*** 

And the Vision stays in the middle of the Board, only growing — no one's dream is ever removed; we simply weave a bigger bouquet. Every body articulates its own vision and purpose *within* the ØNE Intent ([[Template For Vision and Purpose]]) — holofractal: one Intent, ten thousand faithful expressions.
