# Navigate

To Navigate is to move consciously FROM where we are TO where we are going — by a map, toward an aim, through real territory.

Navigation is the second half of every quest; the first is fixing the [[Aim]]. Having chosen [[The Goal]], the whole work of [[Governance]], of a life, of the [[Meta Project]], is to Navigate FROM [[Where We Are]] TO [[Where We Are Going]] — honestly locating the present position, clearly holding the destination, and choosing the [[Discern the Wise Right Next Steps|wise right next steps]] between them, again and again.

Every navigator steers by a [[Map of Reality]] — and the map is not the territory ([[Perception]]). So navigation is a double discipline: moving through the Territory, and continuously correcting the map as the Territory answers ([[Updating Our Map]]). The falls and surprises are not interruptions of navigation; they are its best data.

The deepest compass points are already given: away from [[Pain]] and [[Suffering]], toward [[Potential]] and flourishing; away from Hell, toward Heaven — read anew in every moment ([[The Living Gradient]]). And the truest heading of all is [[Navigate Towards Truth]]: following the [[Golden Thread]] wherever it leads, no matter what it costs the current map.
