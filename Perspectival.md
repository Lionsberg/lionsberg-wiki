# Perspectival

[[Perspectival]] describes what is true of all finite knowing: it is a view *from somewhere*. Every act of perception, description, and understanding occurs from a particular vantage — a location in space, time, culture, language, development, and [[Mastery]] — and what is disclosed is always the territory *as seen from there*.

That knowledge is perspectival does not make it false; it makes it partial. The [[Observable Universe]] is the cosmic demonstration: move the observation point, and the entire visible sphere moves with it. The same holds for every [[Map of Reality]], every canon, every guide. The response is not despair of truth but the twin disciplines of humility and weaving — owning one's vantage honestly ([[Subjective]]), and joining it with the vantages of others so the whole may see what no single view can.
