# Shame
Shame is the reading that the self, as seen, has fallen below the circle's standard — and that belonging itself may be forfeit.
**Felt Sense.** Heat rising to the face, then a collapse inward: eyes down, shoulders curled, the whole body trying to be smaller. The impulse is to hide — to leave the room, the conversation, the gaze. Shame wants, above all, to be unwitnessed — which is precisely why, unwitnessed, it festers.
**What It Signals.** Shame reports a gap between who the circle needs its members to be and who I now appear to be — to them, or to my own internalized eyes. It is the social instrument reading *exposure below the line*. Read precisely, it says: something here matters to [[Belonging]].
**Its Gradient.** [[Self-Consciousness]] → [[Embarrassment]] → Shame → [[Humiliation]]. Its repairable cousin is [[Guilt]]; its frozen extreme, [[Mortification]].
**Its Gift.** In right size, shame guards the standards that make community possible. A circle whose members can feel it can hold trust without surveillance; conscience does the work of walls.
**Its Distortion.** The distortion is the shift from verb to noun: guilt says *I did a harm* — repairable; shame distorted says *I am the harm* — an identity, not a reading. Refused, it hardens into defiance or contempt; enthroned, it crushes the self it was meant to calibrate, breaking [[Right Relationship]] from within.
**The Move.** Shame cannot be reasoned away in hiding; it dissolves only in the thing it fears — being seen and still received. Bring it into a [[Circle]] that can hold it. Name the deed; refuse the identity. Repair what can be repaired, and let the eyes that receive you reset the reading.
