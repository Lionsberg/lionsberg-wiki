# Anxiety
Anxiety is fear without a locatable object — the alarm sounding on standby.
**Felt Sense.** A hum under everything: chest tight, breath high and shallow, stomach unsettled, attention scanning without landing. The body is braced for a threat it cannot point to, so it braces at everything.
**What It Signals.** Anxiety reports that the system has detected more uncertainty than it can currently map — too many open loops, too much unnamed, too little ground. Because it has no single object, it is usually carrying many small ones: unfelt fears, unmade decisions, unspoken truths, pooled below the waterline. Anxiety is the sum of what has not yet been read.
**Its Gradient.** [[Unease]] → [[Apprehension]] → [[Worry]] → Anxiety → [[Fear]] → [[Dread]] → [[Terror]]. Where [[Worry]] names its tomorrow, anxiety cannot; that blur is its signature. Its lighter, threshold cousin is [[Nervousness]].
**The Diagnostic That Frees.** Anxiety is not a personal failing, a moral defect, or a broken self ([[Anxiety as Default-On]]). It is the **default state of the human nervous system** — vigilance calibrated for an ancient threat-environment, running inside the [[Old World]]'s engineered fear and amplified scarcity. The system is not malfunctioning; it is reading its conditions. The work is neither suppression nor silencing, but **recalibration**: body-first practices that signal safety from the inside (the breath is the handle), and honest movement toward a Worthy Aim — a system in motion toward a real [[The Quest|Quest]] recalibrates.

**Its Gift.** A summons to inventory. Anxiety says: *something in the field needs naming.* It keeps vigilance funded until the real objects are found.
**Its Distortion.** Left unread, anxiety becomes the weather — a chronic climate mistaken for the self, managed with numbing and busyness, narrowing life to what feels controllable. The alarm was built to summon attention, not to govern.
**The Move.** Ground the body first — long exhale, feet on the floor, the real room — because a braced instrument cannot read. Then take inventory on paper: *what, specifically, is unresolved?* Each item named moves from anxiety into [[Worry]] or [[Fear]], which can be met. What cannot yet be named, hand to [[Trust]] for the night.
