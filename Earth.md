# Earth

[[Earth]] is our beloved planetary home — among the countless orbiting bodies of the cosmos, the one whose humans named it Earth, and the local theater in which the present chapter of [[The Great Story]] is being written.

Earth holds uniquely perfect conditions for [[Life]]: sacred water flooding seas and lakes and rivers below, the protecting sea of gases above, the living weave of creatures and ecologies that has unfolded across deep time. It is not a warehouse of resources but a living whole — to be met in Sacred Reciprocity, stewarded across [[All Generations of Life]], and cherished as the ground of every local place where the Game is played.

Aboard Earth, humanity now stands at its crossroads — and the purpose of [[The Great Game of LIØNSBERG]] is played out here: the co-creation of [[Heaven On Earth]], every place made a little more like Heaven, with no one involuntarily left behind. What is learned and embodied here belongs to the wider cosmic weave; Earth's awakening is a local event of a cosmic Reality.
