# Fractal Commonsing

Wherever sovereigns freely associate, a commons naturally emerges — and this is true at every scale. **Fractal Commonsing** is the conscious practice of that truth: at every table where members of the [[New Civilization]] sit — [[Cell]], neighborhood, [[Community]], locality, bioregion, world — a [[Commons]] is forged, governed, and nurtured *by and for those who sit there*.

Each fractal commons follows the same living pattern:

- **Resourced by voluntary contribution** — the first tenth of available flow ([[Voluntary Tithing]]) offered inward to the center of whatever table you sit at, never extracted by any distant center. There is no central organization to tithe to; there are ten thousand tables, each building shared abundance its members could never reach in isolation.
- **Governed by its own [[Field of Agreements]]**, stewarded by its own members, transparent to all of them.
- **Protected by its own [[Selectively Permeable Membrane]]** — so that what is pooled can bless without being captured.
- **Provisioning real capacity** — the tools, spaces, and services that every Cell and family needs but none can afford alone: energy and clean water, workshops and food forests, learning and healing places, the instruments of making and repairing — held in the commons of each locality with equal access for all. ([[LIØNSBERG Instantiation Hubs]])
- **Interexistent with the whole** — each commons sovereign, yet sharing its best wisdom, solutions, and patterns upward and outward, so that every table inherits the learning of all tables. ([[The Commons]] · [[Kit of Parts]])

The faster value circulates within and among these commons, the faster All rise together. Fractal Commonsing is how the [[Superorganism]] provisions its cells — from the bottom up, from the inside out, with no center to capture and no periphery left behind.
