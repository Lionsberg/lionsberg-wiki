# Resignation
Resignation is the reading of will surrendered — "nothing I do matters here" settled from conclusion into stance.
**Felt Sense.** Slump. Shoulders rounded, gaze lowered, voice flattened; the reach that no longer extends because the hand has learned to expect nothing. Not the sharp ache of defeat — the grey quiet after the ache was swallowed. Effort feels absurd before it begins.
**What It Signals.** Resignation reports that the will has stopped bidding on some territory of life: past attempts read as evidence, evidence hardened into verdict. It must be distinguished from its noble cousin — [[Acceptance]] is peace with what *is*; resignation is defeat about what *could be*. Acceptance releases the unchangeable; resignation abandons the changeable too, and the difference is the whole of [[Hope]].
**Its Gradient.** [[Discouragement]] → Resignation → [[Despair]]. Upstream lie [[Stuck]] and chronic [[Stress]]; its counterfeit twin is [[Acceptance]].
**Its Gift.** An honest core: real powerlessness *somewhere*. Resignation almost always contains a true reading — some door genuinely is closed, some lever genuinely is not yours. Honored precisely, it stops the waste of force on the immovable.
**Its Distortion.** Overgeneralization: powerlessness *somewhere* declared powerlessness *everywhere*. This is the [[Old World]]'s quietest victory — beings who could move, taught by a few walls that all the world is wall.
**The Move.** Find the actual edge of your agency — draw it honestly, however small the territory inside. Then act there, visibly, once. [[Sovereignty]] is not power over everything; it is full authorship of what is truly yours. One real act inside the edge begins to move the edge.
