# Energy

Energy, as a felt reading, is the sensed level of available life — the capacity to effect change, experienced from inside.

**Felt Sense.** Current. The body bright and springloaded, ideas arriving faster than they can be spoken, the work reaching for the hands — or, at the low band, the limbs heavy, the will willing but the battery flat.

**What It Signals.** Energy reports the state of the whole instrument's reserves: rest, nourishment, meaning, connection, and hope all feed it; depletion in any of them drains it. It is among the most honest readings there is — energy rises reliably around the people, places, and work that are *life-giving*, and drains around what is not. Read over weeks, the energy-gradient is a map of where one's life actually lives ([[The Living Gradient]]).

**Its Gradient.** Depletion → fatigue → steadiness → vitality → [[Excitement]] → overflow. Its deep kin is the canon's wider meaning: the creative field of capacity through which all change moves ([[Vibration]] · [[Frequency]]).

**Its Gift.** Truth about sources. What energizes is pointing toward [[Purpose]]; what chronically drains is asking to be changed, shared, or released.

**Its Distortion.** Overridden — the [[Old World]]'s habit of spending past empty on borrowed stimulation — the reading is silenced until the body stops the whole game itself. Hoarded, energy stagnates: it is a current, kept alive by flowing into the work and the ones we love.

**The Move.** Read the level without judgment. Tend the sources before the tasks. Spend generously from fullness, rest without guilt at the ebb — and let what reliably gives life shape where the life is given ([[Emotion]]).
