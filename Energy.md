# Energy

Energy is the creative field of capacity to effect change — in accordance with pattern, will, or design; informed by the [[Logos]].

At the highest level of resolution we can [[Perceive]], the nature of the [[Creative Field]] itself seems to be (something like) Energy. The vast majority of it remains [[Dark]] — not yet illuminated by science — and it is a [[Reality]] that can be *worked with* before it can be *understood*. It is therefore important not to limit one's conception of Energy to society's present definitions, but to hold it open toward the still-[[Unknown]] elements ([[Current Best Understanding]]).

## The Two Directions

Within the field, Energy moves in two fundamental directions ([[Associative And Dissociative Energy]]):

**Associative** — drawing together: integrating parts into wholes, forming cells into bodies, beings into [[Circle|Circles]], Circles into [[Community|Communities]], Communities toward the [[ØNE Body]]. The vector of [[Love]], [[Creation]], and [[Healing]].

**Dissociative** — pulling apart: decomposing form back into substrate. Not evil in itself — the dissolution of what has completed its season belongs to the cycle — but dissociative Energy *directed against [[Right Relationship]]* is the operational signature of the inverted Pattern. The trained being reads the actual signature of every encounter, in the body, before analysis: associative Energy enlivens, coheres, and leaves beings free; predatory dissociation diminishes, fragments, and binds.

## The Scales

**As metaphysical field** — all manifestation is association and dissociation of Energy, informed by the Logos in an eternally regenerative process ([[Vibration]] · [[The Living Gradient]]).

**As physical capacity** — the measurable ability to do work: the sunlight, food, fuel, and flow every organism and civilization runs on; its regenerative sourcing and just distribution are core work of the [[New World]] ([[Total Nested Holarchy of Goals]]).

**As personal resource** — each Sovereign allocates a daily flow of [[Time]], [[Attention]], and Energy — the true currency of a life; their [[Stewardship]] is among the first disciplines of the [[Way]].

## As a Felt Reading

From inside, Energy is sensed as the level of available life ([[Emotion]]): the body bright and springloaded, the work reaching for the hands — or the will willing and the battery flat. The reading reports the state of the whole instrument's reserves; read over weeks, the energy-gradient maps where one's life actually lives — what reliably energizes points toward [[Purpose]]; what chronically drains is asking to be changed, shared, or released. And the same faculty, trained, reads the field beyond the self: the felt signature of a room, an institution, an encounter — associative or dissociative — is Energy read directly ([[Sensing The Field]]). Its distortions: overriding the reading — the [[Old World]]'s habit of spending past empty on borrowed stimulation; or hoarding, where Energy stagnates — it is a current, kept alive by flowing into the work and the ones we love. The move: read the level without judgment; tend the sources before the tasks; spend generously from fullness; rest without guilt at the ebb.
