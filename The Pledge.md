# The Pledge

*The first Commitment — made and kept by every Player.*

---

To step into the Game is to make the first Commitment: **the Pledge**. Before the first Quest, before the first gathering even, each Player accepts [[The Invitation]] and states what they will contribute — for the Game is a **commitment-based system**, and Heaven on Earth is brought forth by commitments made and kept. The Pledge is the first one: the one all the others stand on. 

**A Pledge names, for the season ahead** *(each 90-day cycle, turning at the solstice or equinox)*: 

- **Your TEA** — the Time, Energy, and Attention you commit each week *(the baseline to play: 10%, 4–5 hours)* 
- **Your reciprocal resource contribution** — treasure of any amount, flowing to [[Nurturing the Commons|the commons]] 
- **Your gifts** — the capacities, crafts, and offers you bring to the body 

**A Pledge is living.** Update it at any time, as your season of life changes. Renew it at every seasonal turning — freshly chosen beats silently assumed. And like every commitment in the Game: *say what you will give; give what you said; learn from the difference.* 

**Why the Pledge matters to the Whole:** visibility. The gathered pledges let the body see what is committed for every cycle — and so plan, provision, and play *into the future* together, rather than hoping in the dark. The growing pledge board lives on [[The Gameboard]], beside the Score: the Score tells what has flowed; the Pledges tell what is coming. This is a forcing function to bring shared [[Governance]], decision making, and resource allocation online. 

---

*How, this season: the Pledge channel is being established — pledge links arrive by email alongside the orientation invitations, via [the Substack](https://cocreatingheaven.substack.com/), and travel only through the doors — never posted raw on the open web, so the membrane keeps the bots out without troubling the humans. Its form may grow into a season-by-season crowdfunding of the commons — visible, voluntary, alive. Until the channel opens, your Pledge lives with your Circle: speak it at your gathering, and record it on your [[Circle Card Template|Circle card]].*
