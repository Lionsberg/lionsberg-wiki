# The Seasonal Cycle

The Movement moves in **Seasonal Cycles iterating toward [[The Goal]]** — Equinox to Solstice to Equinox — the same breath [[The LIØNSBERG Calendar]], the [[Quest|Quests]], and the treasury keep. Not sprints, not quarters: the rhythm of the sky itself, shared by every [[Cell]] on Earth without any center keeping time.

Each season, every Cell and the whole Body breathe together:

- **At the turning** — [[OmniSpection]]: the whole field is read honestly (The Record, the Stories, the signs of the times, the width of [[The Window]]), and each Cell chooses its [[Seasonal Quests|Seasonal Quest]] for the season ahead.
- **Each week** — the Cell gathers: commitments spoken, commitments kept, the gap learned from.
- **At every new moon and full moon** — resources divide across the fellows of the [[LIØNSBERG Ecclesia]], and Stories are shared into the [[Commons]].
- **Before the season turns** — every mature Cell passes the Flame: a new Cell seeded, carrying the whole code. ([[Self-Replicate Your Cells]])

Growth across the seasons is measured in the [[LIØNSBERG Cycles of Growth]] — the Fibonacci spiral, whose recurrence encodes the truth that only a matured Cell replicates — and the managed quantity is [[LIØNSBERG Cycle Time]]: how many days each cycle takes, reported at every turning in The Record, without adornment.

A sprint ends; seasons return. This work is not a push toward a finish line — it is a living rhythm, iterated toward The Goal for as many turnings as the crossing takes.
