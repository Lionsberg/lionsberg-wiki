# Disgust
Disgust is the reading that something toxic or degrading must not be taken in.
**Felt Sense.** Recoil and expulsion. The nose wrinkles, the throat closes, the stomach turns; the whole body says *out* — do not swallow, do not touch, do not absorb. It is the only emotion whose native gesture is rejection at the border of the self.
**What It Signals.** Disgust guards purity at two scales with one instrument. At the body's scale it screens what enters — the spoiled, the septic, the contaminating. At the moral scale it fires at conduct that degrades life: cruelty, corruption, treachery, the profaning of the sacred. Moral disgust is a real and ancient reading — the [[Boundary]] of a community's health, felt in one member's gut.
**Its Gradient.** A distinct current that braids with the [[Anger]] family: milder as [[Distaste]] and [[Aversion]], and — fused with anger and aimed at persons — hardening toward [[Contempt]] and [[Hate]].
**Its Gift.** Protection of integrity: it keeps poison out of the body and degradation out of the [[Circle]]. A community that can feel no disgust at cruelty has lost an immune system.
**Its Distortion.** Mistargeting. Disgust is calibrated for *harms* and *substances*; aimed at *beings*, it becomes the most dangerous reading we carry — the felt engine of dehumanization. The [[Old World]]'s cruelest chapters began by teaching populations to feel disgust at kinds of people.
**The Move.** Honor the recoil, then check its aim: *is this a harm, or a human?* Reject toxins and refuse degrading conduct without apology — and wherever the revulsion has attached to a being or a kind of being, stop, and deliberately re-aim it at the harm alone. Beings stay inside the circle.
