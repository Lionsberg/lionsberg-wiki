# Excitement
Excitement is the reading of aroused aliveness toward an approaching good — the body leaning into what is coming.
**Felt Sense.** Current. Quickened heart, bright eyes, energy gathering in the limbs, a fizz through the chest that wants motion. Sleep thins; attention sharpens. The same voltage as fear — but the body opens toward it instead of bracing.
**What It Signals.** Excitement reports a threshold: something alive is approaching, and the organism is mobilizing to meet it. It is anticipation charged with welcome — the announcement that *what comes next matters; be ready*. Read with openness, it is fear's twin current wearing its true face.
**Its Gradient.** A stir of it is [[Interest]]; fuller, it runs [[Energy]] high; at the crest it borders [[Joy]] and spills into [[Delight]] when the good arrives. Braced against, much the same arousal reads as [[Anxiety]] — often the only difference is the openness of the reading.
**Its Gift.** Fuel at thresholds. Excitement supplies exactly the energy a beginning demands — the launch, the meeting, the leap. It marks where life is asking us to show up at full voltage, and pays the bill in advance.
**Its Distortion.** Chased for its own charge, excitement becomes addiction to intensity — the [[Old World]]'s stimulation loop, mistaking arousal for meaning and novelty for life. The counterfeit needs an ever-louder next thing; the real thing is attached to a good, not to the buzz.
**The Move.** Let it charge you without discharging it in fidgeting or talk. Ask what threshold this fuel is for. Aim the voltage at the actual beginning. Then cross — excitement is spent well only in motion toward the thing it announced.
