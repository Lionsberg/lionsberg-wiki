# The LIØNSBERG Book of Emotions

This Book helps us answer, and truly hear, the most navigationally important question two embodied beings can exchange:

***How / What do you feel?***

## What an Emotion Is

An [[Emotion]] is a felt resonance-reading — the way one of the ones of the [[ØNE]] senses where they stand on [[The Living Gradient]] as they experience and explore [[Reality]] in embodied form. Emotions are not interruptions of navigation; they *are* the navigation: **information, not commands · gradients, not boxes · no enemies among them** — every emotion carries a gift, and every emotion has a distortion that arises only when the flow is dammed.

The names below are [[Word Tool|Word Tools]] — named bands in one continuous living spectrum, listed so that feelings can be communicated precisely enough to be met. The name is for communication; the reality is flow.

## The Practice

The core move, on any band, at any intensity — the practice this whole Book serves:

**Feel → Allow → Read → Name → Ask → Move.**

Let the reading register; let it flow; read what it signals about what is precious, threatened, opening, or lost; choose the Word Tool that lets it be met; ask what would move you and what you love toward [[Coherence]] — a little more like Heaven; then act as a Sovereign, in [[Right Relationship]]. Practiced in [[Circle]], this becomes the interfeeling of a community that navigates together. (Full pattern: [[Emotion]].)

## The Pattern for Each Entry

Each feeling below grows, over time, into a full page carrying the same living pattern — exemplars: [[Fear]] · [[Joy]] · [[Grief]]:

**Felt Sense** (the vibration as the body knows it) · **What It Signals** (the gradient reading) · **Its Gradient** (neighbors and intensities on the spectrum) · **Its Gift** (the information it evolved to bring) · **Its Distortion** (what it becomes when refused or enthroned) · **The Move** (the practice that restores flow) · **Related**.
