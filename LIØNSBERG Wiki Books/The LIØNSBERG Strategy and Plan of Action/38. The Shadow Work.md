# 38. The Shadow Work

Heaven is not built by pretending Hell is not operating. The Strategy does not avert its eyes: the honest confrontation of the shadow — in the world, in the systems, and in ourselves — is not a digression from the [[10 Year Grand Strategy]]; it is one of its load-bearing walls.

**We first do no harm.** Intervening in natural and social systems beyond our full comprehension, we act with wisdom and integrity — identifying harm, toxicity, and pollution at their sources, prioritizing by Pareto analysis (the ten to twenty percent of sources causing eighty to ninety percent of the harm), stopping them forthrightly, and withdrawing our own energy, attention, and resources from every system that wounds.

**We name the threats.** The Movement keeps an honest, prioritized holarchy of the [[Existential Threats]] — [[Collapse]] in its systemic, ecological, and economic forms; [[Pollution]] of air, water, soil, and body; war and the [[Progressive Ephemeralization]] of superweapons; engineered pandemic; the capture of AI and compute; weaponized propaganda and [[Anti-Truth]]; the strangling of food, energy, and supply — reviewed at every [[OmniSpection]], because a threat unnamed is a threat unmet. (The founding enumeration is preserved in [[The 100 Steps Towards The Promised Land (J, Spring 2024)]].)

**We face the parasitic and the pathogenic.** The [[Living System]] carries [[Parasites and Pathogens]] — biologically, where infections measurably alter minds and behavior and cleansing belongs to the healing journey [[FROM the Old TO the New]]; and socially, where a small percentage of the population — on the order of three to five percent — preys upon others for narrow gain, and is disproportionately drawn to religious and political power. We build our institutions so they no longer select for such patterns; we develop forthright protocols that preserve the [[Proper Order]] of society; and we hold the deeper recognition: dealt with wisely, the most predatory few lose the next ten percent who would have followed them, and those are pulled back into the Light.

**We reckon with [[The Giants]].** The mapping of their harm through the [[Living Systems Data Model]] creates irrefutable evidence; their [[Individual Fiduciaries and Board Members]] are formally informed of the harm and of their personal liability for what is knowingly continued; [[Safe Off Ramps]] and an [[Amnesty]] process open the honorable way out — for we have all been complicit in the [[Meta Crisis]], and the Future needs the skills of those who once served the Old. Where a true accounting of damage exceeds any capacity to repay, the path is receivership: harmful operations cease, captives are liberated, the captured [[High Science Technology]] passes into [[The Commons]], and the giant's might is turned toward the regeneration and [[At-One-Ment]] of the world.

**We forge the LIØNSBERG Alliance.** As the old security order completes its failure, a [[LIØNSBERG Alliance]] is forged to succeed it — ensuring universal strength and security, overseeing the responsible, balanced, and simultaneous redirection of military flows [[FROM Weaponry TO Livingry]], and holding the peace of the crossing so that no people is left naked before the storm while the swords are being reforged.

**We disempower corruption structurally.** Radical truth, transparency, and accountability — every ledger open, every flow traceable — make theft, capture, and exploitation costly beyond appetite. This is the same law the Ecclesia's own treasury keeps: what never pools and never hides cannot corrupt.

**We keep the balance of the Sacred Warrior.** [[Minimum Necessary Force]] for defense; the bullies on the playground stood up to and disarmed; the captives — including those inside the dark systems — loved toward the door; [[Restorative Justice]] over vengeance; protective, meaningful pathways for the few who cannot yet play by the rules that let All flourish. An excess of chaos or of order each leads toward Hell; the shadow work holds the [[Proper Balance]] — and leaves no one involuntarily behind, even here.
