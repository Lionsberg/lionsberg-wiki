# 23. How Much Time Do We Have?

Only [[ØNE]] knows. Our [[Current Best Understanding]] is that this decade — and perhaps the progress made in the next twenty-four months — will determine whether we succeed or fail on our quest.

Because any system divided against itself cannot remain, from the view beyond the [[SpaceTime|Illusion of SpaceTime]] the [[Existing Order]] has already failed. [[The Building Is On Fire]]; [[Collapse]] is a function of [[Time]].

Because [[We Do Not Know]] how long we have, we use [[Probability Based Thinking]]: the [[Non-Zero Probability of Systems Collapse]] is assessed honestly, and every individual and family decides how long they are willing to remain asleep in [[The Caves]] before awakening, arising, and joining [[The Movement]] toward a safe and flourishing future [[Beyond The Boundary]].

The Strategy therefore holds a double vision of time ([[Temporal Markers]] · [[The Window]]):

- **The markers we set** — the milestones of the [[10 Year Grand Strategy]], managed on the [[Critical Path]], their variance recorded honestly in [[34. The Record]] and consumed knowingly from the [[20 Year Generational Contingency]].
- **The Window reality sets** — the [[The Window|Critically Effective Time Limit]], which no one can calculate and which may close at an unknown time. Contingency can only be spent inside an open Window; how wide it remains is a continual matter of sense-making, discernment, and [[OmniSpection]], season after season.

The wise stance within an unknowable Window is to [[Act As If]] it could close at any time — moving as rapidly as possible toward [[Proper Order]], [[Without Harm or Disadvantage to Any]] — while never despairing, for the same unknowability that could close The Window early also means grace may hold it open longer than any calculation allows.

The epochs behind us and the milestones ahead are marked across the whole arc: all prehistory · the long formations · [[The 7 Year Window of Preparatory Work and Learning]] (2017–2023) · and the [[10 Year Grand Strategy]] — [[The 7 Year Window of Crisis and Opportunity]] with its calling, tipping, and building seasons, then [[The 3 Year Lag]] of formation for the final cohorts — toward [[The Greatest Celebration and Jubilee In History]], the [[20 Year Generational Contingency]] standing behind, and the millennia beyond, eternally into the Unknown, where the Quest continues.
