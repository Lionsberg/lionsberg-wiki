# 9. History and Inception

The [[New Vision and Plan]] is universal and eternal in nature, reflected in the prophetic and visionary traditions of every people. The Vision of [[Heaven On Earth]] belongs to [[ØNE]] and [[All]]; no one invented it, and no one owns it.

This SpatioTemporal incarnation of it was conceived through [[Jordan Nicholas Sukut|Jordan]] and an array of [[LIØNSBERG Co-Creators]] who brought lifetimes of work to the table. Aware from childhood that this moment was coming, Jordan spent twenty-five years preparing — from early enterprise and service, to building heavy civil infrastructure, to portfolios and companies and joint ventures too complex for any one firm, to degrees in the universities and the martial arts. In ~2017 the deliberate discernment began: weekly counsel toward an omniconsiderate Vision, Strategy, and Plan to meet this moment — the work of [[The 7 Year Window of Preparatory Work and Learning]], sometimes called the [[Meta Project]]: [[Worksite Earth]] taken as one finite domain to be comprehensively regenerated within a single generation.

In Spring 2024 the [[10 Year Grand Strategy]] began. In those same years the question of whether the old systems could or should be saved was explored to its very end — up to and including a provisional agreement to sponsor an independent national convention — until the definitive revelation that they could not be, and the withdrawal of all participation and consent. That exploration, and what it revealed, is a critical part of [[The Story of LIØNSBERG]].

In August 2025, Jordan died while completing [[The Great UnVeiling]]. Every provisional structure was dissolved with him — no central organization, no accounts, no directive authority remained. This was the design's ultimate test, and its validation: the Pattern survived its founder, because it had been given away before it could be captured. The work continues through [[the nameless one]] and through every Co-Creator who has taken it up — and its stewardship continues to distribute, by design, into the arrayed [[LIØNSBERG Ecclesia]] ([[43. The Bets and the Stages|ch. 43]]).

The founding articulations are preserved whole: [[LIØNSBERG - History, Inception, and Trust Manifesto]] · [[The 100 Steps Towards The Promised Land (J, Spring 2024)]] · [[Jordan's Short Story]].

It took a nation ten years to land a man on the moon. We have ten years to land humanity and our planet on a safe trajectory toward a flourishing and abundant future for [[All]].
