### B. The five-why on the stalled ignition

1. Why did spread not ignite? — Those who arrived waited for the center to act.
2. Why did they wait? — The invitation was to *join something*, and joining implies a host who serves.
3. Why was it framed that way? — Every prior instrument (site, book, board, guide) was built *by* the center *for* players, teaching by its very form that the center builds.
4. Why did that contradict the design? — The design requires sovereign cells that sustain and replicate themselves ([[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]]); a served guest is not a sovereign cell.
5. Root: **the calling was missing.** No one had been asked, by name, by people who know them, to *lead*. The [[LIØNSBERG Ecclesia|ecclesia]] — those called out of their homes into the public square to govern and to work — had no doorway through which to be called.

This is not a failure of the Pattern. It is the discovery, at seed scale and at low cost, of exactly what the Pattern demands next. Every defect is a gift.

## II. The Strategic Adjustment: The LIØNSBERG Ecclesia

### A. Both / and — the two doors

**The open door (self-selection):** Everyone on Earth is invited — to resonate, to contribute any amount, and thereby to take up a **dynamic voice in governance**. No one is turned away; no one is involuntarily left behind. This door leads in time to [[New Citizenship]] for all.

**The narrow door (nomination):** The first act of every voice is to **nominate** — out of the hundred people they know and trust most, the two or three they would most trust to help lead their tribe and community through [[The Collapse-Ascension Nexus]]. Not family or romantic partners; not celebrities or strangers; not the self-promoting; people they have personally watched navigate hardship faithfully. When a person is surfaced by three trusted nominations, they receive **The Invitation**. Those who accept enter the [[LIØNSBERG Ecclesia]] as fellows: they [[Form A Cell|form a Cell]], choose a [[Quest]], share their [[Stories of Transformation|Stories]], and [[Pass The Flame]].

The nomination web is itself the first harvest: a **transparent trust graph** across the awakening population — and, when the grid falters, a living contact-tree in which every member knows their three and is known by theirs.

### B. Resourcing — the metabolism arrives

The Q1 Update named the working-capital gap but no concrete mechanism for the first dollar. The mechanism now exists:

- **Contributions flow through a temporary, radically transparent structure** — a public ledger any person on Earth can read. The structure is scaffolding, not the building: the Ecclesia matures its own commons vehicle behind it, and depends on no platform.
- **About one tenth of all flows nurtures the shared Commons** — the infrastructure, the instruments, the world-site. The rest is **distributed across the fellows** doing the work. From those resources they can cover their own expenses and survival, and become [[Centers of Distribution]] of any excess funds to the [[Quest|Quests]] they value and want to support.
- **At every new moon and full moon, all resources in the pool divide across the fellows.** Money never pools at the center; no more than two weeks can ever be lost; the treasury itself keeps [[The LIØNSBERG Calendar]].
- **Each Cell forges and governs its own commons** — holofractal, interoperable, sovereign — exactly as the Q1 Update declared: *there is no central organization to tithe to.*
- Matching funds have already been offered by a friend of the mission, unconditioned — the first evidence that the 97% will resource the 3%.
- The crowdfund is the **spark, not the engine.** The mature economics remain what the Plan has always held: seasonal [[Quest]] resource flows directed by participants themselves, and commons tithes at every holofractal scale.

### C. The world — lionsberg.world

The Movement's single front door comes online at **lionsberg.world**: a world to fall into, a story to enter — where the invitation, the nomination, the transparent ledger, the Gameboard, and the Commons View live as one place. Behind it, the wiki remains the deep library. Two design laws govern it:

- **Nothing in the center to attack.** The world is generated from plain text; the whole of it can be downloaded and carried by any [[Cell]]. Though the site vanish, ten thousand copies remain, each able to regenerate the whole ([[The Golden Seed]] made digital).
- **Graded registers.** The Golden Seed is being rendered readable at every gate — beginning with a form any family in any village can read aloud together — so that the 90%+ resonance the Strategy requires can be real across the whole population, not only among readers of dense prose.

### D. What a fellow and a Cell are equipped with

Each fellow: the Book (the whole code, readable by a mother and a fourth-grader — the Story, the compass, the five moves, the seven provisions, the protocols including grid-down) · the Cell practice (a weekly rhythm any kitchen table can run) · their share of the moon distributions · their Cell's hearth in the world · their nominator chain as living sponsors · and, for those who choose it, a [[LIØNSBERG Personal Agents|LIØNSBERG Personal Agent]] — always optional, never required.

Each [[Cell]]: sovereignty (its own commons, its own agreements, its own legal skin or none) · a Quest chosen from the work of the [[The 12 Pillars of LIØNSBERG|12 Pillars]] · the seasonal rhythm · a [[LIØNSBERG Cell Agents|LIØNSBERG Cell Agent]] · and the standing quest of every mature Cell: **seed a new Cell** ([[Self-Replicate Your Circles]]).

And one truth stands above all the markers: **[[The Window]] may close at an unknown time.** Contingency can only be spent inside an open Window — a late project may find the opportunity it counted on no longer exists.
