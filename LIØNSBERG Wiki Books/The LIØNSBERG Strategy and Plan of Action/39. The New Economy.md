# 39. The New Economy

The economy of the [[New Civilization]] rests on a redefinition already made ([[33. Resourcing the Transformation]]): [[Value]] and [[Waste]] measured relative to [[The Goal]], and the books kept in the Universe's own currencies — [[Time-Energy Accounting]]. On that foundation, the whole economic order inverts.

**Real Wealth is recognized.** [[Real Wealth]] is [[Energy]] — which cannot be destroyed — joined to [[Wisdom]], [[Knowledge]], and [[Information]] — which always increase. By that true measure humanity is not poor and never was: gathered into [[The Commons]] as [[Our Collective Inheritance]], the wealth already at hand is sufficient for every family and community on Earth. In the deepest sense, [[We Are All Billionaires]] — heirs who were taught to believe themselves paupers.

**The liberation arc.** The New Economy progressively renders obsolete the need to "[[Earn A Living]]" — dissolving debt-based [[Employee Consumer Debt Slavery]], eliminating the meaningless and destructive [[Jobs]] that waste human [[Potential]], welcoming the automation of the old work while ensuring [[We The People]] own the AI, the robotics, and the [[High Science Technology]]. In its place: [[Meaningful Work]], [[Meaningful Relationships]], and meaningful income for every individual and locality willing to co-create — with [[Safety Nets]] for any we cannot yet connect, an [[Energy and Commitment Based UBI]] as the floor, and poverty rendered obsolete.

**Incentives are realigned with outcomes** — so that peace is more profitable than war, wellbeing than sickness, long-term regeneration than short-term exploitation, and co-operation toward mutual success than competition toward mutual destruction. [[Regenerative Underwriting, Resourcing and Insurance]] reorients actuarial data toward the long-term flourishing of living systems; a [[Regenerative Banking Network]] grows with the [[LIØNSBERG Instantiation Hubs]]; off-ramps carry capital out of [[Degenerative Systems]] and into the [[Critical Path]].

**Currencies are born from commitments, not debt.** As the fiat currencies of the [[Old World]] fail — or harden into instruments of surveillance — the New keeps [[Freedom of Exchange]] alive through an interoperable network of [[Local Currencies]], originated by **commitments made and kept**: a promise kept toward a [[Local Quests|Local Quest]] creates [[Throughput]] of The Goal, which is *by definition* Value — money as crystallized trustworthiness, tied to each locality's [[Network of Commitments]] and stacking up through the [[System Wide Master Planning Process]] to [[The Quest]]. The rollout must outrun the collapse: continuity of exchange, everywhere, before the old rails fail.

**The economy circulates rather than accumulates.** Landfills and waste streams become the primary mines ([[Circular Economy]]); every photon received is treasured toward Life; supply chains relocalize with the hubs and are traced end to end; the fractal [[Offers and Requests Marketplaces]] let every scale exchange freely; and standing over all of it, the anti-pooling law: wealth that flows blesses, wealth that pools corrupts — the economy of [[Centers of Distribution]], at every scale, for All.
