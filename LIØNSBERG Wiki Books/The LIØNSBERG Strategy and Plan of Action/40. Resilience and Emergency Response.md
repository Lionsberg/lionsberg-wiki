# 40. Resilience and Emergency Response

Reality will shake — in unknown ways, at unknown places and times, throughout [[The 7 Year Window of Crisis and Opportunity]] and beyond. The Strategy does not merely hope otherwise; it builds for it. Resilience — and where possible [[Anti-Fragility]] — is designed into everything the Movement co-creates.

**Every locality becomes able to stand.** Communities and bioregions rapidly relocalize the [[Core Functions of Life and Society]] — food, water, energy, shelter, wellness, exchange — so that each can provision itself indefinitely, while retaining [[Interoperability]] and [[Flow]] between domains. Every community holds its own resilience and recovery plan, woven through the [[Local Planning Process]] into a poly-centric planetary fabric that still holds if any thread — or any center — fails.

**Response is standardized so help is instant.** A common, interoperable system of response, recovery, and restoration — for flood, fire, drought, earthquake, storm, eruption, and social upheaval — with global first-responder standards and training, so that any expert can travel anywhere on Earth and step directly into coordinated action; modular and mobile facilities (medical, shelter, mission control) ready to deploy through the [[LIØNSBERG Instantiation Hubs]]; and [[Precision Measurement]] with [[Living Systems Data]] forecasting what can be foreseen, buying humanity time before catastrophe.

**Migration is prepared for, not feared.** The coming transformations will move the living — people, animals, plants, forests. The Movement targets its resources toward the most vulnerable first, renders refugee camps obsolete by ensuring every person a place to heal and flourish, keeps every people free to reunite and resurrect their culture in a homeland of their own, and reopens the ancestral migratory paths of every species — assisting the migrations the changing world requires.

**The Ecclesia is the emergency backbone.** As the old systems fail and disaster comes, it is the arrayed [[LIØNSBERG Ecclesia]] — its [[Cell|Cells]] trained, its hubs provisioned, its trust-tree intact when the grid is not — that feeds the local populations, keeps the peace, and provides a [[Way]] forward. Emergency response is not a department of the New Civilization; it is one of the first fruits by which the New proves itself to every community it serves.
