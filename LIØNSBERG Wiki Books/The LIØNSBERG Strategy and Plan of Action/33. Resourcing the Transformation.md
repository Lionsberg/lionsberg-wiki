# 33. Resourcing the Transformation

*Added August 2026, with the 2026 Q2 Update.*

A body without metabolism is a diagram. For the [[LIØNSBERG Ecclesia]] to do real work, resources must actually flow — from the many to the ones doing the work, and outward again to the [[Quest|Quests]] that regenerate the world. This chapter names the mechanism, from the first dollar to planetary scale.

## The ignition — the 97% resource the 3%

- **Contributions flow through a temporary, radically transparent structure** — a public ledger any person on Earth can read. The structure is scaffolding, not the building: the Ecclesia matures its own commons vehicle behind it and depends on no platform.
- **About one tenth of all flows nurtures the shared [[Commons]]** — the instruments, the infrastructure, the world-site. The rest is **distributed across the fellows** doing the work.
- **At every new moon and full moon, all resources in the pool divide across the fellows.** Money never pools at the center. No more than two weeks of flows can ever be captured, frozen, or lost — the treasury itself keeps [[The LIØNSBERG Calendar]].
- **Fellows are [[Centers of Distribution]].** From their share, each fellow covers their own transparent expenses and survival, and directs any excess to the Quests they value. How resources move through the whole becomes the combined intelligence of all the fellows — never the decree of a center.
- **Every [[Cell]] forges and governs its own commons** — holofractal, interoperable, sovereign. *There is no central organization to tithe to.*

## The engine — seasonal Quest flows

The crowdfunded pool is the spark, never the engine. The mature economics are the participants' own:

- At [[The First Three Percent]] (~3% of ~8.1 billion ≈ 250 million fellows, in roughly 50 million Cells of 3–7), Cells directing an average of even $10,000 per seasonal [[Quest]] would move on the order of **$500 billion per season** toward regenerative work — flowing peer to peer and Cell to Quest, without passing through any center at all.
- Each Cell and community tithes to its own local commons at every holofractal scale, provisioning [[The Commons]] from the bottom up.
- As the Movement becomes visible, philanthropic and institutional capital redirects from waste to value — and in later phases, the [[Progressive Disarmament]] of the old order reallocates trillions [[FROM Weaponry TO Livingry]].

## The laws that govern all of it

1. **Transparency is the legitimacy.** Every ledger open, every flow visible, from the first dollar. The Movement's claim to trust is not asserted; it is auditable.
2. **Anti-pooling is the security.** Wealth that never pools cannot be seized, cannot corrupt, and cannot become a target. Distribution on the moons is not an accounting convenience — it is armor.
3. **No platform dependence.** Every structure used to move money is temporary and replaceable; the trust graph, the ledgers, and the commons survive any platform's death.
4. **The center may equip; it must never operate.** Resources equip fellows and Cells to act sovereignly. Any flow that requires the center's ongoing hand is a defect.
