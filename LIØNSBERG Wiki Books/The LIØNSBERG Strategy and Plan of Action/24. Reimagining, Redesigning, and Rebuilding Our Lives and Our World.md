# 24. Reimagining, Redesigning, and Rebuilding Our Lives and Our World

The Transformation is not an idea to hold; it is a world to rebuild — and it begins in the only place it ever truly can: our own lives.

**First the life, then the world.** Because unenlightened beings cannot co-create enlightened systems ([[35. The Anatomy of the Body|ch. 35]]), the rebuild proceeds from the inside out: each person reimagining their own way of living — how they drink, eat, sleep, breathe, ground, work, play, and flow — then their family, their [[Cell]], their community, their bioregion, outward to the Whole. Every scale rebuilt is a proof and a school for the next.

**Then every aspect of society.** Nothing is exempt: learning and healing, food and water, energy and shelter, exchange and governance, story and celebration — every one of [[The 12 Pillars of LIØNSBERG]] is [[Reimagine, Redesign, and Rebuild|reimagined, redesigned, and rebuilt]] from first principles, using [[Comprehensive Anticipatory Design Science]], the wisdom of Nature, and the best of [[Our Collective Inheritance]] — with the [[New Norms and Values]] articulated as we go, and the physical and metaphorical bridges built so that billions can actually cross ([[21. Pathways, Bridges, and Infrastructure|ch. 21]]).

**On an honest horizon.** The [[10 Year Grand Strategy]] turns the trajectory; the rebuilding itself runs generations — on the order of one to two centuries for the [[Built Environment]] to be wholly remade regeneratively and most of humanity rehoused in dwellings worthy of them ([[42. The Great Stewardship|ch. 42]]). We build to last, not to planned obsolescence; we begin everywhere at once, fractally; and we let each generation inherit the work further along than it was received ([[Multi-Generational Co-Operation]]).
