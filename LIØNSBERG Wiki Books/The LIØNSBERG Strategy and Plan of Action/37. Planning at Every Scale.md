# 37. Planning at Every Scale

The vision, values, strategy, and plans for any domain must arise **from within** that domain. No center can plan a neighborhood it has never walked. Yet in the face of the [[Meta Crisis]], every domain's plans must ultimately interoperate — so that if the [[Existing Order]] partially or wholly [[Collapse|collapses]], the plans of every family, neighborhood, community, and bioregion still hold together as one fabric.

**The reciprocating engine.** Planning in the New runs as a living reciprocation between two motions: the [[Local Planning Process]] — each domain surfacing its own [[Local Plans]] in response to [[The Right Questions]], which illuminate the spirit and values of a person, group, or place — and the [[All In One]] [[System Wide Master Planning Process]], which weaves the local plans across all [[Levels of Analysis]] into a coherent whole. [[Local Plans]] become the basis for the [[Local Quests]] that fuel [[The Movement]] toward [[The Goal]]; the master plan ensures no domain plans in ignorance of its neighbors.

**Balance emergence with vision.** In each domain the approach balances [[Consensus]] emerging from the [[Bottom Up]] with the visionary [[Leadership]] needed to forge coherence across scales. The balance is struck the same way everywhere: identify, connect, and empower [[The First Three Percent]] of the wise right leaders in that domain — surfaced by the trust of their own people ([[32. The Calling of the Ecclesia]]) — and train and equip them to succeed. The [[Neighborhood]] may prove the wisest level to begin: local leaders empowering individuals and families below, communities and bioregions above.

**Training spreads by each one teaching one.** The work of envisioning, designing, and building the [[Best Possible Future]] in every domain requires broadly distributed facilitation. The Strategy therefore trains an initial fifteen thousand [[Facilitators]] as the seed of a [[Train The Trainer]] / [[Each One Teach One]] cascade spreading through The First Three Percent — and through them, to every table on Earth.

**Guilds carry the crafts.** Every Cell, community, and bioregion requires the same diversified array of skills to fulfill the core functions of [[The Body]] in its own time and place. [[Guild|Guilds]] are therefore established around the twelve or so core functions — each training on the order of twelve to fifteen thousand practitioners out of [[The First Three Percent]] — cultivating and cross-pollinating each craft's knowledge around the world.

**Stories fuel the flywheel.** As [[Local Quests]] are undertaken and begin to succeed, the [[Share Your Stories|Stories]] told of them fuel the resourcing-and-engagement flywheel — and [[Every Story Is A Prophesy]] of even greater things to come. Around the Quests' common needs, [[Guild|Guilds]], services, and support emerge in [[The Commons]] to help every [[Cell]] meet what it faces.

**Mission Control serves the arrayed Ecclesia.** Through the [[10 Year Grand Strategy]], a [[Mission Control]] function stands in service of the whole — never commanding the sovereign [[Cell|Cells]], but stewarding what only a shared function can hold: the submittal and continuous-improvement process of [[The Prototype]], so that every locality's best solutions flow into the common [[Kit of Parts]] and every locality inherits the learning of all; and a **two-way request-for-guidance system** — any Cell or domain may request guidance from Mission Control, and Mission Control may request guidance from the arrayed [[LIØNSBERG Ecclesia]] in turn. The center equips and serves; the called-out decide and do.

**Self-governance is trained at every scale.** Each domain is trained in the new way of governance and decision-making — [[Ecclesia Led Self-Governance]] that families, neighborhoods, communities, bioregions, georegions, and beyond can co-operate indefinitely. And within the existing systems of governance — the two hundred nation-states, the thousands of sub-states, the millions of counties, cities, and boards — the Movement forges domain-by-domain plans for progressive elevation and transformation: monitoring the [[LIØNSBERG Cycles of Growth]] in each domain alongside its local election cycles, and, as adoption of the [[New Paradigm]] crosses seventy to eighty percent in a given domain, reaching the deeper levers of constitutional renewal.
