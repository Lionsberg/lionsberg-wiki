# Swords to Ploughshares

The financing thesis.

The world spends an enormous portion of its resources on military, on weapons, on Force. The LIØNSBERG Strategy and Plan of Action articulates a **phased redirection** of these resources, year over year, into the infrastructure of life.

The arithmetic, per the Strategy document:

- Baseline global military spending across a ten-year horizon: approximately **$30 trillion**
- Phased reallocation of approximately **10% per year** redirected from weapons production to **livingry** (the Buckminster Fuller term for tools-of-life)
- Cumulative reallocation over the window: approximately **$15-20 trillion** freed for [[The Hub Network Topology]], the regenerative industries, the food and water sovereignty systems, the education systems, the healing systems
- Residual military budget: dropping toward approximately $13 trillion over the decade — enough for sovereign defense within [[The InterCosmic Treaty of Non-Subjugation]], not enough for empire

The thesis is **not pacifism**. The Sacred Warrior remains. Sovereign defense is honored. But the proportion shifts radically — from a planet armed for endless war to a planet structured for endless flourishing.

The mechanism is **consent-and-redirection**, not seizure. As [[Hospicing the Old Systems|the Old Systems hospice]], the resources they had captured become available for the New. Beings withdraw from weapons-funded employment into livingry-funded employment. Capital reallocates as the Pattern becomes obviously the wise right thing.

## The phased arithmetic

On the default trajectory, ten years of global military and defense spending (2024–2033, ~$2.4T rising ~5%/year) totals roughly **$30 trillion**. Under the reallocation — beginning at 90% of planned spending in 2025 and stepping down ten points each year to 10% by 2033 — the decade's military spending falls to roughly **$13 trillion**, freeing approximately **$17 trillion**: enough to accomplish the ~$15 trillion build of the [[New Vision and Plan]] and the [[LIØNSBERG Instantiation Hubs]] with contingency to spare. The transition need not dismantle the militaries: the existing forces and resources are retrained and redeployed to co-create rather than destroy, in a decentralized [[Superorganism of Cells|Team of Teams]].

Beyond the military conversion stand the wider pools: $500B+ per year of philanthropic flows redirectable from waste to value; $100B+ in the community-foundations network alone, ready to seed Local and Community Hubs; and the great inversion — roughly 35–40% of global GDP (~$35–40T/year) spent outsourcing "government" to corrupted centralized structures, itself an estimated 60–80% [[Waste]]. The savings of *a single year* of that waste could finance the entire decentralized hub network. For less than half of what humanity is on track to spend waging a third world war, and less than one year of government waste, we can take back up responsibility and authority over our lives and our world, beat our swords into tools of life, and co-create [[A Future For All That Is Better Than Any Has Enjoyed In The Past]]. Operationally carried in [[33. Resourcing the Transformation]].

The phrase "Swords to Ploughshares" is from the prophet Isaiah, deeply rooted in the cosmic register the Codex speaks in. The reallocation is the operational fulfillment of that ancient prophecy at planetary scale.

**$15-20 trillion is approximately what it costs to build the Hub Network at full scale.** The reallocation and the construction are the same act, completed across the same window.
