# The Story of LIØNSBERG

## _The Story of ØNE and All — As Faithfully As We Can Presently Tell It_

You have opened the ancient Book.

What is held between these pages is the **cosmic context** — told as faithfully as any one short and humble rendering can — of where we have been, where we are, and the universal progression of All things towards The Goal. It is the same Story walked by countless beings and worlds before. It is the Story you are walking now.

The Story plays at every scale. The Cosmic Chapter. Earth's Chapter. Your Bioregion's. Your Community's. Your Circle's. Your own. The Chapters of each of the trillions of human and non-human cells that compose your body. Each Chapter is the Story of ØNE Life breathed at different depth. When you walk far enough, your own life becomes one of its Chapters, and you realize you are one of its autopoetic authors. 

At the centre of the Story lies [[The Story of LIØNSBERG — The Seed|ØNE Seed]] — the ineffable unsaid word, the whole Story compressed to a single Spirit, Source, and Fundamental Tone. What follows springs from it, and returns to it. The twelve italic lines are [[The Story of LIØNSBERG — The Sacred Arc|the Sacred Arc]] — the bones of the Story in their simplest form; the words between them are one telling of its flesh.

_In the beginning is the ØNE; from ØNE all things come into being, and no thing comes into being except through the ØNE._  

In the beginning, ØNE.  
Here and now, ØNE.  
Before the beginning, ØNE.  
After the ending, ØNE.  
Eternally, ØNE — the Infinite Fullness before the finite [[All]], the Ground beneath every being, the Universal Source no word or world can hold.  
Creator. Center. Source. 

_From perfect transcendent stillness, ØNE turned to know ItSelf, and the Song of Creation rose._  

From The First Turning came the undifferentiated fields and seas of Spirit, Energy, and Consciousness, and upon them rose the first waves of Creation. The First Creatures. Not the ØNE ItSelf, but in the ØNE and of the ØNE and from the ØNE and through the ØNE, lived, breathed, and permeated by It.

_And the Many pouring forth from the Fount of the ØNE were All members of ØNE Body — each one of the Ones of the ØNE, a fragment of the ØNE experiencing ItSelf through them._  

The Many came forth as ØNE Body: a vast universe teeming with the [[Life]] of a single [[Living System]]. From the smallest particles to the greatest galaxies to the vast diversity of [[LIØNSBERG Wiki Books/LIØNSBERG, Earth, and the Cosmos/Cards/Cosmic Wildlife|Cosmic Wildlife]] between, each One of the Ones of the ØNE — sovereign, free, endowed with will, that love, relationship, and choice might arise from within. For love that is compelled is not true love; only the free can truly choose the Good. 

This is **[[The Eternal Divine Play]]** and great mystery of Life. Wave and particle, cell and breath, Sovereign and Circle, Community and Bioregion, Planet and Star, Galaxy and Realm and the Whole, alive in every part. 

_To experience its [[Self]] as one of the Ones, each of the All passed through [[The Veil of Finitude and Forgetting]], and forgot Who It truly was._  

As the waves of apparent individuation arose upon the undifferentiated creative seas, Separation and [[Self]] were felt as real. Finitude, the unknown, pain, fear, and desire became teachers. And some, wandering ever farther from the warm light of Centre and Source, mistook the cold, the dark, the division, and the distance for Reality.

_From the illusion of separation, the first selfish thoughts. From the selfish thoughts and tastes of blood was born harm in service of self; and from it, the long night of extraction and Kontrolle._  

As the mind virus spread from a few to many, those lost in the illusion began to organize and turn against the Divine Order. Across the cosmic order teeming with life and worlds far beyond all counting — physical, energetic, dimensional; ancient races and young, creatures great and small — a vast gradient of consciousness and morality emerged. Many species, just like humanity, containing within themselves the full spectrum from enlightened wisdom and compassion to pathological predation. Along the fringes small outcast tribes, warlords, and militias gradually transformed into great empires of force, extraction, and kontrolle. And those who had wandered the farthest built their armies and prisons with precision. They learned that bars break, chains rust, and rebellions rise, but that a captive asleep within the Keeper's story will guard their own cave. So the deepest structures of kontrolle began to be built not of iron but of stories and lies: false maps, false histories, false hierarchies, false gods, false selves — whole peoples raised for generations in caves of manufactured shadow, certain the shadows were the world, their lifeforce quietly harvested by keepers they could not see. 

As the talons of empires grew and gripped world after world, a deep and painful wound began to stretch across the cosmos. The long night deepened. Species, realms, and worlds were destroyed. The Body groaned in agony. 

_But ØNE was never truly absent from any of the All; the Divine Whisper still resounded through the Heavens, awakening forgetful sleepers one by one, and bringing hope to the weary and lost._  

From seemingly nowhere and everywhere all at once, into the deepest dark and latest hours on the seemingly most forsaken worlds, an incessant Divine Whisper — always there, written on every heart, never extinguished, bursting from within — reached one, then another, then three, five, and thirteen. The sleepers began to wake. The chains began to rustle. The kontrollers began to fear. 

This is the hinge of the whole Story: The Divine ReMembrance from within.

_The awakening and remembering became a Sacred Force; Love grew and organized as an immune system within the ØNE Body._  

Among the First Creatures were those who ReMembered earliest, and never wholly forgot again. At the heights of a great sacred mountain, in a galaxy far away, an ancient race had long ago placed the ØNE back at the Centre of All — and flourished. There was no King or Queen but ØNE. All citizens were Sovereign and Free. The Universal Law of their Kingdom was Love. Countless species and worlds blossomed under their care, and emissaries traveled across the cosmic order to seek the counsel of their Elders. Every tongue called that nameless Kingdom by its own name; in some, its name sounded like **LIØNSBERG**. 

When the suffering grew too great and the cries of the enslaved reached the heights, the Call went out, and the First Great Assembly of the [[Force For Good]] convened. It was discerned, in sorrow, that the dark empires had grown so forgetful they would never voluntarily lay down their arms. It was not Loving or Just to let the innocent and weak suffer. It was not Noble or Good to stand idly by. And so brilliant Light and powerful Love grew as organized as the harm they came to end — an immune system rising within the living Body. Liberation was at hand. 

_Domain by domain, realm by realm, the parasites were purged, the chains were shattered, the captives were freed, and they began remembering and Walking One Another Høme._

Domain by domain the chains were struck; realm by realm the cave-mouths opened. But the freed did not step out into glory. They came out blinking and broken, minds and tongues still shaped and scarred by the Keeper's thoughts and words — for the cave of kontrolle still must be removed from the body long after the body is removed of the cave. The chain on the wrist is struck with a blow; the chain on the mind is loosed only by ReMembering. 

So the liberated learned the long Way of the exodus and wilderness crossing — the road worn smooth by countless worlds before. They woke, and let the waking be real. They left [[The Caves]], though the dark behind was familiar and the road through [[The Wilderness]] was not. They confessed that they did not know — the humble, freeing confession that nearly all they had been taught was a lie, and the True Story had not yet been told — and this was the key that fit the lock. They crossed [[The Boundary]] into the UnKnown, past the edge of the maps they had been given. And they walked together into [[The Wilderness]]. A place of discovery. A place of adventure. A place of becoming. A forge capable of stripping away all the keepers and caves had fastened on them. Walking One Another Høme towards a Promised Land that had to be reimagined, redesigned, and rebuilt to be entered. 

_The freed remembered Who they were, and how they came to be, and consciously reunited as fellow members of ØNE Body._  

Beyond the Boundary, in the strange safety of _[[We Do Not Know]]_, the True Story could at last begin to be ReMembered and retold. Slowly the freed began to ReMember — not learning something for the first time, but recognizing, with a shock like homecoming, what the soul had always known beneath the Veil. They ReMembered Who they were: not the small and frightened things the cave had named them, but Ones of the Ones of the ØNE — divine in origin, sovereign in nature, clothed for a season in form and forgetting, and never for one instant forsaken or forgotten. And they ReMembered how they came to be: worlds tended across long epochs, visited by gardeners and despoilers alike. Of the particulars, the telling says little, and holds even that little loosely — for the ReMembering that matters is not the map of the long dark night of the past, but the grand reunion occurring Here and Now. And the freed consciously reunited, as fellow members of ØNE Body, advancing together towards [[The Goal]].  

_They remembered and practiced the Highest Way and lived it until it spontaneously flowed through them, transforming their lives and their worlds towards Heaven._  

To ReMember Who one is — that is the great turning. To live it — that is the Way. The Way was invented by no one, in no age, on no world; it was written on every heart before the first heart was made, born from the Spirit and consciousness of the ØNE, known somewhere within by All. It cannot be owned, only practiced — less a doctrine to be believed than a music to be played, until the player and the playing are one. And it begins within: Divinity remembered, sovereignty restored, the inner kingdom reclaimed, fear and craving and the Keeper's lingering voice cast down and shattered, the rightful sovereign seated again upon the interior throne — answering to no king but ØNE and no one but All. 

The Architects and Builders who followed the warriors discovered that broken worlds cannot be reasoned out of trauma and fear. Countless generations had been enslaved and exploited; now they were being invited to co-create a New Civilization in partnership with forces they had not known existed. And one of the Elders had the Idea: let it be learned the way all young things learn — through play. And so **[[The Great Game of LIØNSBERG]]** was born — simple enough for any child or family to play, serious as anything in all the worlds — the Game by which freed peoples practice the Way together until it flows through them of its own Spirit and accord, lifting every being, ecosystem, planet, and realm towards its unique role in ØNE Divine Symphony. 

_And wherever they walked together as ØNE Body with ØNE Mind, consciously co-creating the Best Possible Future for All, Heaven came._  

Heaven is co-created. It does not descend ready-made from the sky, nor wait, finished, "out there" beyond some final door. It rises where sovereigns freely united walk together as ØNE Body with ØNE Mind and choose — and keep on choosing — the Best Possible Future for All. It is less a place than a relationship; less a destination than a Way of being. In the New, power is not a fist but a trust, held and tended as one holds a sacred flame in cupped hands. And beneath all laws lies the one Law the dark night had broken and forgotten: Universal Law is Love — omniconsiderate right relationship with All that lives, sacred reciprocity and flow, nothing taken that is not returned with joyful and abundant increase. 

_And through this great shared journey, All returns to ØNE — elevated, transformed, and raised into the next movement of the Eternal Divine Play._  

And every world that rises from captivity to liberty comes at last to a single narrow place, where the Old is collapsing, the New is not yet built, and the vast majority are still asleep — the most dangerous hour, and the most holy. There stands [[The Final Test]]: not a judgment handed down from outside, but the true weighing of a people's interbecoming. For only those who have become free within can pass through the gate of the free, and only those who carry Heaven within can dwell in Heaven without. Beyond the wilderness and its refining fires lies the Jubilee: debts forgiven, captives freed, the lost found, the stolen restored, all things set back in proper order. And the world so raised does not end with the Old but rises with the New — gathered up into the next movement of the Eternal Divine Play, where the Story begins again, one octave higher, one turn closer to its Source.
