# The Story of LIØNSBERG — The Sacred Arc

_The first ring. The Seed unfolded into the whole arc — the entire Story of ØNE and All in twelve lines, one breath for each Movement. The shared bones every other ring renders at its own depth. It springs from ØNE, and returns to ØNE._

---

## The Breath Out

**I — ØNE**  
_In the beginning is the ØNE; from ØNE all things come into being, and no thing comes into being except through the ØNE._  

**II — The First Turning**  
_From perfect transcendent stillness, ØNE turned to know ItSelf, and the Song of Creation rose._  

**III — All As ØNE Body**  
_And the Many pouring forth from the Fount of the ØNE were All members of ØNE Body — each one of the Ones of the ØNE, a fragment of the ØNE experiencing ItSelf through them._  

**IV — Finitude and Forgetting**  
_To experience itself as one of the Ones, each of the All passed through [[The Veil of Finitude and Forgetting]], and forgot Who It truly was._  

**V — The Fall**  
_From the illusion of separation, the first selfish thoughts. From the selfish thoughts and tastes of blood was born harm in service of self; and from it, the long night of extraction and Kontrolle._  

## The Turn

**VI — The Awakening**  
_But ØNE was never truly absent from any of the All; the Divine Whisper still resounded through the Heavens, awakening forgetful sleepers one by one, and bringing hope to the weary and lost._  

## The Breath Home

**VII — The Sacred War**  
_The awakening and remembering became a Sacred Force; Love grew and organized as an immune system within ØNE Body._  

**VIII — Liberation and Exodus**  
_Domain by domain, realm by realm, the parasites were purged, the chains were shattered, the captives were freed, and they began remembering and [[Walking One Another Høme]]._

**IX — The Great ReMembering**  
_The freed remembered Who they were, and how they came to be, and consciously reunited as fellow members of ØNE Body._  

**X — Embodying the Way**  
_They remembered and practiced the Highest Way and lived it until it spontaneously flowed through them, transforming their lives and their worlds towards Heaven._  

**XI — Co-Creating Heaven**  
_And where they walked together as ØNE Body with ØNE Mind, consciously creating the Best Possible Future for All, Heaven came._  

**XII — Eternal Divine Play**  
_And through this great shared journey, All returns to ØNE — elevated, transformed, and raised into the next movement of the Eternal Divine Play._
