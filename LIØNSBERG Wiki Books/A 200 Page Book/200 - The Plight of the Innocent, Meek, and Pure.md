# 200 - The Plight of the Innocent, Meek, and Pure


$^1$ The most pure and naive among the Creatures and Civilizations    
$^2$ Were tragically the most innocent and vulnerable within the cosmic order  
$^3$ Upholding the Universal Law and having tasted neither blood nor harm  

$^4$ One by one they fell and were enslaved  
$^5$ As the pitiless Force of Destruction descended from the skies   
$^6$ Swallowing them whole before they knew what had struck them   

$^7$ Planet after planet and realm after realm  
$^8$ Was enslaved and exploited  
$^9$ And then abandoned and left for dead  
$^10$ Once all the Life Force had been drained from it  

$^11$ Ever farther the Dark Fleet pressed out into the Cosmic Order  
$^12$ A mighty Dark Empire always on the march  
$^13$ Expanding its Shadow in search of the next innocent and helpless victim
