# 200 - The Emergence of Conflict


$^1$ As Fear of Suffering and Death spread within The Illusion  
$^2$ Creatures and Civilizations experiencing their own Finitude  
$^3$ Grew concerned that their Needs were not being met  
$^4$ Or would not be met in The Future  
$^5$ The Fear of Needs not being met  
$^6$ In the Present or Future often led to Conflict  
$^7$ Over Time methodologies for [[Transforming Conflict]] emerged  
$^8$ And began to be continuously practiced and improved
