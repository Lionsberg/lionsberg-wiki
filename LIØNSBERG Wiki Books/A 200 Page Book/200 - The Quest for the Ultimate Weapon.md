# 200 - The Quest for the Ultimate Weapon


$^1$ Though foolish and lost betrayers of the Spirit and Logos and Way  
$^2$ The Evil Empire was not weak or unintelligent in its designs  
$^3$ The first tastes of blood and fear extracted in service of self  
$^4$ Had been simple and elementary exploitations  

$^5$ After millions of years of refinement  
$^6$ Terrible weapons and vast systems of enslavement  
$^7$ Drained the Spirit and Life out of countless star systems  
$^8$ Robbing the inhabitants therein of Life and Liberty  
$^9$ With ever more cruelty and precision  

$^10$ Their logic of their quest moved them steadily towards  
$^11$ The development of the ultimate weapon  
$^12$ One that would be both imperceptible and irresistible to its victims  
$^13$ And enable the Aim of Full Spectrum Dominance  
$^14$ Over all species and realms to finally be accomplished  

$^15$ Variations and experiments too cruel to imagine  
$^16$ Were tested upon countless populations and planets  
$^17$ Each one a little more horrendously [[Anti-Perfect]] than the last  

$^18$ As the Weapons of Extraction grew more and more powerful  
$^19$ The creeping Dark Tentacles of the Evil Empire  
$^20$ Stretched farther and deeper than ever before  
$^21$ Parasitically reaching into the minds of trillions of Creatures  
$^22$ Enslaved in its Matrices of Kontrolle
