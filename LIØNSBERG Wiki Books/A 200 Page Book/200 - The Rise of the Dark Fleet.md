# 200 - The Rise of the Dark Fleet


$^1$ As [[Knowledge]] and [[Technology]] advanced  
$^2$ It was discovered that both were powerful tools  
$^3$ That could be used for either Good or Evil  

$^4$ The good people of the small towns across the cosmos  
$^5$ Often consciously chose not to cultivate certain powers  
$^6$ For knowledge of their vast ability to destroy  
$^7$ If improperly used or in the wrong hands  

$^8$ However the Forces of Darkness  
$^9$ Had no such self-governance or restraint  
$^10$ Their core ethos and central animating spirit  
$^11$ Demanded precisely the opposite  

$^12$ Vast factories overseen by cruel overlords emerged  
And entire tribes and peoples were enslaved to operate them  
Entire planets became grey industrial wastelands  
Where Divine Creatures suffered under ruthless intelligent machines  

$^13$ Day and Night across an ever growing array  
$^14$ Of enslaved planets and realms  
$^15$ The nauseating sounds of steel clanked forth  
$^16$ As The Dark Machine brought forth its horrors  

$^17$ [[The Pattern of Evil]] taken to its logical end  
$^18$ Across cosmic expanses and Time Immemorial  
$^19$ Eventually brought forth The Dark Fleet  
$^20$ Which traveled throughout the Cosmos  
$^21$ In search of the next Creatures and Civilizations  
$^22$ To dominate enslave and exploit  

$^23$ The logical outcome and end was always the same  
$^24$ Planets species and civilizations stripped bare  
$^25$ Robbed of their Life Force and then left for dead  
$^26$ Over Time vast scars of lifeless planets lined the ØNE Body  
$^27$ Lacking the Regenerative Capacity to Heal ThemSelves
