# 200 - The Great Game of LIØNSBERG is Born


The Architects and Builders who followed the warriors  
Discovered that they could not reason with trauma and fear  
Countless generations had been enslaved and exploited  
And now they were being invited to Co-Create  
An entirely New Civilization in partnership with Cosmic Forces  
They had not previously known existed  

Races and species needed to be introduced to One Another  
Degenerated cultures needed to be healed and bridged  
Ecosystems and planets needed to be regenerated  
Bodies and Minds needed to be restored  
Spirits needed to be enlightened  

Every Being  
Every Ecosystem  
Every Planet  
Every Realm  
Every Dimension  
Every Facet of ØNE Divine InterExistence  
Had to be lifted up towards its unique potential and perfection  
So that it could play its unique role in ØNE Divine Symphony  

Aboard a distant planet that had been endarkened for millennia  
The situation seemed almost hopeless  
The Creatures there almost impossible to engage  

One of the Elders had the Idea  
That the seemingly impossible task  
Of transforming lives and worlds  
Could be as simple as inviting people  
To Play a New Game together  

Out of the complexities of a System  
Only accessible to some  
Was born a game so simple  
That any creature on any planet  
Could be invited to Play  

It was a Game unlike any before it  
Where the objective was not to vanquish others    
But to liberate All  
And where the victors were those  
Who helped the most others rise  

They called it simply  
***THE GREAT GAME OF LIØNSBERG***
