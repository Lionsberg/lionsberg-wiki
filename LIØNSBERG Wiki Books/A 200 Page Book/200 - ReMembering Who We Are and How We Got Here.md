# 200 - ReMembering Who We Are and How We Got Here


Now that We are standing Together  
Beyond The Boundary of the Known  
In the safety and integrity of the admission  
That We Do Not Know  
We can begin to reveal  
The approximate Story of Earth  

As trillions of galaxies unfurled  
Like flowers in the cosmic expanse  
From the Logos of the ØNE  
Eventually a small galaxy  
Known to English speaking Earthians    
As the Milky Way emerged  

On the remote edges of one of its spiraling arms  
A star known to Earthians as the Sun  
Was born  

Within that star system planets formed  
Too cold or too hot and apparently lifeless at first  

Earth was not created in six days by the ØNE  
Nor did it magically evolve over billions of years  

At just the right Time  
As it transitioned into the "Goldilocks Zone"  
Of this star system  
It was identified by multiple species  
Of Cosmic Gardeners  
And consciously terraformed into a Living Laboratory  

Over Time Immemorial hundreds of species  
Of advanced beings came and went from Earth  
Bringing genetics  
Taking genetics  
Modifying genetics  
Cultivating and growing  
Experimenting and extracting  

And as She developed and flourished  
She became a prized member of the Cosmic Community  
Renowned for her beauty and abundance of Life   

While some of her visitors recognized her  
As the living and conscious being that she is  
Others sought to exploit and kontrolle her  

The memories of nearly every ancient community on Earth  
Recall the times when strange beings in strange ships  
Cultivated and warred over the Earth  

Neither "Life", "Civilization", nor "Humanity"  
Naturally evolved or was directly created on Earth  
All were engineered and shaped  
By a diversity of off-planet space-faring Creatures  
With a diversity of methods and motives  

Just as today humans travel in spaceships  
To the moon mars and beyond   
So too is Earth visited and explored  
By an array of advanced species and civilizations  

Including those who genetically engineered humanity  
As humanity experiences itself today  

Like the rest of Life and Civilization  
Humanity did not originate on Earth  
The Human Template is far more ancient  
And has been localized and adapted to a variety of planets  

The localization and adaption occurs  
By advanced genetic engineering through which  
The "essence" of various genetic "templates"  
Is "mixed" with other locally evolving life  
To produce something "good" or "useful"  
That can survive in that local planetary environment  

If We imagine this as an emergent process  
Through which Life is intelligently guided  
In its ongoing development towards The Goal  
Across billions of star systems and planets   
It becomes easier to archetypically grasp  
The Story of Earth  

For the Story of Earth  
While in its particulars unique  
Is generally shared in common  
With billions of other planets  
Just as Your Story  
While in its particulars unique  
Is generally shared in common  
With billions of other people on Earth  

Especially when viewed from afar  
It is only by stepping back and up  
In Consciousness to view The Story  
From as close to the Perspective of the ØNE  
As We can that We can accurately see Reality  

Consciousness has to abstract ItSelf out  
Of its local context and ReMember Who It Is  
In order to see clearly  

We are not Our Body  
We are not Our Mind  
We are not Our Emotions  
We are a Divine Aspect of the ØNE  
Experiencing ItSelf within an illusion of Separation  

Once We consciously recognize that  
By Awakening while still Living  
Our Consciousness can "surf"  
Between bodies and minds  
Levels and localities  
To begin experiencing ItSelf  
_Not only as One of the Ones_  
_But also as ØNE moving_  
_Among multiple lives and experiences_  

Once that process becomes Consciously Experienced  
One can Perceive far more clearly by Consciously Exploring  
The multiple perspectives it can access within ItSelf  
While weaving those perspectives  
Into Its Own Highest Order Functional Unity  
And thereby accumulating the Quantity and Quality  
Of Wisdom, Understanding, and Knowledge  
It can access at an exponentially accelerating rate  

Because All Consciousness is Ultimately Interconnected  
There is no functional upper limit but ØNE  
To any One of the Ones of the ØNE  

The only functional limitation is the Size of Being  
Of any apparently individuated aggregation  
Of Spirit and Consciousness  
And Energetic Life Force  
Localized to any Time and Space
