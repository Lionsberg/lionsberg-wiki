# 200 - Extracting LifeForce From the Enslaved


The Dark Empires had discovered  
That the more pure and naive their victims  
And the greater the fear and suffering inflicted  
The more powerful the energy extracted  

Sacrifices were demanded  
Victims were hunted and trafficked  
Elaborate rituals and ceremonies were designed  
With the specific aim of bringing the most innocent beings  
To the point of peak anguish and suffering  
Before cutting out their hearts  
Drinking their blood  
And consuming their flesh   

Cosmic breeding grounds and trafficking lines were forged  
To ensure a constant supply of the purest victims  
To feed the Darkest Forces at the top of the Evil Empire  

For the innocent who had not personally experienced it  
The suffering and horror were unimaginable  

Trillions of Divine Beings  
Were born into Alien Kontrolle Matrices  
Entirely unaware that pain and suffering  
Were being intentionally inflicted upon them  
To feed the Dark Forces
