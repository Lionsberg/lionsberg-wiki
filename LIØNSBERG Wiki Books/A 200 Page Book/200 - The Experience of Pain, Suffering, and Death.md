# 200 - The Experience of Pain, Suffering, and Death


$^1$ One of the most difficult experiences of [[Manifest Finitude]]  
$^2$ Was the experience of [[Pain]] and [[Suffering]]  

$^3$ Manifestation out of Transcendent Eternity  
$^4$ And into Finite Realities  
$^5$ Meant that Creatures by design  
$^6$ Could experience Pain and Death  

$^7$ This was experientially necessary  
$^8$ So that Each of the All could differentiate  
$^9$ What was Healthy and Good  
$^10$ And caused Life to Joyfully Flourish in Harmony  
$^11$ From what caused it to wither and die
