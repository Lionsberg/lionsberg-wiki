# 200 - Why Our True Story Is Ultimately Ineffable


However the ancient inscriptions and sacred stories  
Carry strikingly similar Memories across vastly disparate cultures   
While modern stories cast as fiction are veiled containers of Truth  

The War did not start in this local galaxy  
And battles have been fought over countless star systems  
So the long tail of history fades into Darkness  

The names of billions of planets  
And the stories of billions of tribes and species   
Written in millions of foreign languages  
Can never be known or grasped by any One  
Nor would all the libraries of Earth contain them  

Only ØNE knows All  

But All can access and know the ØNE  
And from that Inner Knowing  
The Arc and Essence of All can be glimpsed  

EveryWhere EveryWhen and EveryOne  
Are always accessible via the non-local consciousness  
The permeates every particle of Creation
