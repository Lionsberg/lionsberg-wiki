# Everything Is A Process

Just as [[Everything Is A Project]], so too is everything a [[Process]].

A Process is ordered becoming: a sequence of actions or transformations that, carried out, produces [[Throughput]] toward an aim. The convention asks us to see every piece of recurring work this way, so it can be written down, handed on, improved, and woven into the work of the whole.

## Circle, Project, Process

Every piece of work can be seen three ways at once.

- **As a [[Circle]]:** the people who hold it, gathered in trust, carrying a [[Field of Agreements]].
- **As a Project:** an arc with a beginning, an aim and an end, aligned to the shared Vision through [[Everything Is A Project]].
- **As a Process:** the repeating motion inside it, the steps that happen each time the work is done.

The Circle is the container. The Project gives it direction and a finish line. The Process is how the living work moves, round after round. A [[Cell]] meeting weekly, a [[Quest]] unfolding over a season, an onboarding session run again for each new person: each is all three.

## Why

- **It can be handed on.** A process written down outlives the person who first did it, and lets a newcomer step into a [[Roles|Role]] without starting from nothing.
- **It can improve.** Every run is a chance to learn. Feeding what was learned back into the design is [[Prototype Control]] at the scale of daily work.
- **It can flow.** Seen as a process, a stoppage shows itself, and the next handoff can be made clean ([[Flow]]).
- **It can join the whole.** Processes feed one another. Drawn together in a [[Process Chart]], beside the [[Role Chart]] and the [[Function Chart]], they show how a Cell or a Domain actually works, and where a break sits.

## How

Start small: the next time a piece of work repeats, write down what happened, in order. Then shape it with the Process Template:

- [[Template For Process - Instructions]]
- [[Template For Process - Blank]]
- [[Template For Process - Example]]

The template asks for a purpose, a scope, the Roles responsible, the inputs, the steps, the outputs, how the process is measured, and how it changes over time. The [[LIØNSBERG Process List]] gathers the processes of the whole, beginning with [[The Way]].

A process serves the Goal. When a process stops serving the people and the aim it was made for, it is changed.

## The great Process

Beneath every small process runs [[The Process]]: the continuous improvement of the whole, and the crossing from the old to the new. Each process we write either serves it or slows it; that is the measure of every one.
