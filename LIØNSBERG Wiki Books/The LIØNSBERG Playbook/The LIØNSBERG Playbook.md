# The LIØNSBERG Playbook

_The Living Guide of The Great Game. The field book for the operational movements the Body actually performs._

## How to Use This Book

A **Play** is a small piece of choreography for a complete operational moment in the life of a Circle — a thing a Circle *does*: gathers, decides, mends, harvests, passes the flame. Each Play is **its own continuously improving card**: run it as written until your Circle finds a better way — then improve the card through [[The LIØNSBERG Workshop|the Workshop]], so all Circles rise together. 

**Together, the Plays form the operational pattern language of The Great Game.** Each card names its moment, its moves, its instruments, and its related Plays — a living network, not a list. Within the master Plays, the recurring movements — Check In, Make Commitments, Harvest and Close — are **proto-Patterns**: the same elements recur across many Plays, and as they prove themselves they will be articulated into [[The LIØNSBERG Pattern Language]] as its operational Patterns. Behind every Play stands the deeper timeless Pattern; in front of it stand the instruments of [[The LIØNSBERG Toolkit]] — and the use of the Tools teaches the Wise Right embodiment of Pattern and Play alike, for that is how people actually learn: by doing the thing with a good instrument in hand, until the Pattern lives in the body. The Pattern is the why beneath. The Play is the way through. The Tool is the thing in hand. Embodiment is the point of all three. 

**Each layer is named as it is used in life.** Patterns are *spoken*, so they carry bare names that live in sentences — [[Field of Agreements]], [[Pass The Flame]]. Plays are *called*, so they carry the call — *Play — Decide by Consent*. Tools are *picked up*, so they carry their craft in the name — *Template For…*, *…Tracker*, *…Guide*. Where a bare name and a Play share words, the bare name is the timeless Pattern; the Play is its runnable choreography. 

Every Play answers a need in [[General Needs|the General Needs register]]. Two rhythms hold them all: **[[Play — Run the Weekly Turn|the Weekly Turn]]** and **[[Play — Turn the Season|the Seasonal Turning]]** — the heartbeat and the breath. If you learn nothing else, learn those two. 

*The first Plays follow — the ones we anticipate any Circle will need in its opening season. First-season vintage, held as current best understanding: the Circles that play them will improve them, and the language will grow.*

## Where This Volume Sits — Lexicon · Pattern Language · Playbook

The three operational Volumes of the Codex each carry a distinct content role. The same concept may appear in all three — held differently in each.

- **[[The LIØNSBERG Lexicon|Lexicon]]** defines _**WHAT**_ — the canonical definition of every term in the LIØNSBERG vocabulary. One entry per term. Etymology, function, distinction from distorted uses, linkages.
- **[[The LIØNSBERG Pattern Language|Pattern Language]]** articulates _**HOW**_ — the operational wisdom of establishing and maintaining the concept in lived practice. Context · Forces · Solution · Scale · Examples · Related Patterns. The grammar of action.
- **Playbook** composes _**WHEN**_ — the riffs that invoke Patterns in sequence for common operational needs. Context · Sequence · Improvisation · Outcome. The field music-book; the choreography.

**Example: [[Selectively Permeable Membrane]]** — the **Lexicon** defines what a Selectively Permeable Membrane _is_; the **Pattern Language** articulates _how_ it is established and maintained; **this Playbook** composes the Plays that invoke it ([[Play — Establish Selectively Permeable Membranes]], [[Play — Forge Your Field Of Agreements]], [[Play — Connect With Another Circle]]) when the operational moment calls for it.

The three Volumes are interexistent. Each holds its facing canonically; the others reference. The cross-linking is rich; the wisdom is held once.

## Plays Are Riffs; Patterns Are The Notes

**Plays are compositions of Patterns.** Each Play in this Playbook is a brief score for a recurring operational need: how to run a meeting, how to make a proposal, how to make a decision, how to convene a Circle, how to onboard a new member, how to release a member gracefully, how to choose a Quest, how to pass the Flame.

A Play does not duplicate the wisdom of its underlying Patterns — that wisdom lives canonically in **[[The LIØNSBERG Pattern Language]]**. A Play **invokes** Patterns in sequence or weave, with brief choreographic notes about transitions, timing, and improvisation. The Play is the riff. The Patterns are the notes. The Pattern Language is the source of the music.

When a Body opens a Play, the Play sends them to the canonical Patterns for the deeper wisdom of HOW to perform each move. The Play orchestrates; the Pattern Cards deliver.

Every Pattern lives in **one** canonical place; every Play arranges them.

```
   Each Play: "Play — Title.md" filename — Play — Create Or Join A Circle.md, Play — Run A Meeting.md
   Play composes Patterns for a specific operational need
```

A Play's form:

- **Name** — the operational moment (Run a Meeting, Form a Circle, Pass the Flame, Make a Decision)
- **Context** — when this riff fires; what situation calls for it
- **The Sequence** — Patterns invoked in order or weave, with brief timing/transition notes
- **Improvisation Notes** — common variations; when to adapt
- **Outcome** — what the riff aims for
- **Related Plays** — adjacent compositions

The actual wisdom of HOW to perform each Pattern lives in the Pattern Card. The Play is the choreography.

## How To Use This Chart

**Authoring a new Pattern Card.** Open the **Reverse Facing** in **[[The LIØNSBERG Pattern Language]]** to see every Play that invokes the Pattern you are authoring. Each Pattern Card opens to many Plays; the Pattern must hold its wisdom in a way that each Play can compose from it.

**Composing a new Play.** Add a new **invokes** block in the Plays section above. List the Patterns the Play needs. Each Pattern reference sends the player to the canonical Pattern Card for the deeper wisdom of HOW to perform that move; the Play arranges them into a riff for the operational context.

**Refining a Pattern.** When a Pattern's articulation deepens, all Plays that invoke it benefit automatically — because each Play references the canonical Pattern, not a duplicate.

**Refining a Play.** When the operational riff of a Play sharpens, the Play's composition (the Patterns it invokes) may change. The forward facing here updates; the reverse facing in the Pattern Language updates in sync.

**Discovering a missing Pattern.** If a Play needs a Pattern that doesn't yet exist, the gap surfaces in the Play's invocation list. The Pattern becomes the next authoring target.

**Discovering a missing Play.** If a common operational need has no Play, the gap surfaces in lived practice. The Play becomes the next composition target.

## The Disciplines That Govern The Playbook

- **No duplication.** Wisdom lives in the Pattern Card. Plays reference; never restate.
- **Plays are compositions.** A Play is a brief score, not a manual.
- **Maximum reuse.** Frequently-invoked Patterns hold the most weight in the lived Body. Pattern Cards for high-reuse Patterns deserve the most careful articulation; when one of them deepens, every Play it serves deepens with it.
- **Continuously improving.** The Chart above is a living artifact. Every Play and Pattern matures; the Chart matures with them.
- **Scale-aware.** Each Pattern declares the [[Levels of Scale]] it operates at; Plays invoke Patterns at appropriate scales for the operational context.
- **Cross-cutting axes** ([[Guild|Guilds]], [[Tribe|Tribes]], [[Species]], [[Voluntary Association|Voluntary Associations]]) inform when and how Plays apply.
- **Maps in service of Spirit.** The Plays in this Playbook are tools, not idols. **[[Spirit Precedes Pattern]].** When the Wise Right Thing in this moment, in this Body, in this place contradicts the Play as written — do the Wise Right Thing. The Play is the servant. The Spirit is the work.
