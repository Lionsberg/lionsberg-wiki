# The LIØNSBERG Playbook

_The Living Guide of The Great Game. The field book for the operational movements the Body actually performs._

## How to Use This Book

A **Play** is a small piece of choreography for a recurring moment in the life of a Circle — what to actually do, in what order, with which instrument in hand. Plays are not rules; they are the current best way we know. Run them as written until your Circle finds a better way — then play it better, and send the improvement back through [[The LIØNSBERG Workshop|the Workshop]], so all Circles rise together. 

Every Play answers a need from [[General Needs|the General Needs register]] and picks up its instruments from [[The LIØNSBERG Toolkit]]. Two rhythms hold them all: **the Weekly Turn** and **the Seasonal Round**. If you learn nothing else, learn those two — they are the heartbeat and the breath. 

*Fifty Plays follow — the ones we anticipate any Circle will need in its opening season. First-season vintage, held as current best understanding: the Circles that play them will improve them.*

## I. The Opening Plays — from Invitation to living Circle

**1. Answer the Invitation** — *When* [[The Invitation]] reaches you and something deep in you says yes. Tell the one who shared it that you are in. Subscribe for the gathering calls. Do nothing more until the next Play. 

**2. Gather the Ready** — *When* you are in, and alone. Of the hundred people you know best, name the two or three most ready. You know who they are. Share the Invitation with them personally — hand to hand, never broadcast — within one week. 

**3. Run the First Gathering** — *When* your two or three say yes. Ninety minutes, in person or by video. Follow [[The LIØNSBERG Quick Start Guide]] minute by minute: welcome, read aloud ([[AURELLIØN's Prolegomenon]] · [[The Story of LIØNSBERG|the Story]] · [[The Great Game of LIØNSBERG|the Game]]), sense together, form, choose, close. Don't improvise the first one; improvise the tenth. 

**4. Seal the Circle** — *When* the gathered choose one another. A name, 2–5 simple agreements, a first Quest. Speak it plainly: *we are a Circle.* Record it on your [[Circle Card Template|Circle card]]. Sealing is a beginning, not a wall — the membrane stays open by consent. 

**5. Name the Circle** — *When* sealing, or when the placeholder goes stale. Choose a name that feels alive — place, focus, or intention. The care matters more than the cleverness; it can change at any time. 

**6. Forge Your Agreements** — *When* sealing, and at every turning after. Two to five commitments that protect the field of trust: presence, truthfulness, confidentiality, consent, responsibility. → [[Template For Field of Agreements]] · [[Template For Rules of Participation]] · [[Protocols]] 

**7. Set the Rhythm** — *When* the Circle is sealed. One consistent slot, weekly, about ninety minutes. Decide who reminds, who facilitates first (rotate it), and who tends your corner of [[The Gameboard]]. The rhythm is the trellis everything grows on. 

**8. Open a Channel** — *When* the week between gatherings falls silent. Choose one channel your people actually open (Signal, Discord, Matrix…) and agree how it is used: flame-tending, not noise. *(To forge: Communications Setup Guide.)* 

**9. Join the Board** — *When* your Circle consents to be visible. Copy the [[Circle Card Template]], add your row to [[The Gameboard]] — first names only; playing unlisted is honored. From now on, your Circle takes its Turn there weekly. 

## II. The Weekly Turn — the heartbeat

**10. Run the Weekly Turn** — *When* every week, same time. The master Play; the eight that follow are its movements. An hour or two. Consistency matters more than length — the rhythm, kept, *is* the Game. → [[Preflight Checklist For Meeting]] · [[The LIØNSBERG Circle Guide]] 

**11. Arrive and Open** — Land together: a few breaths, a short silence, a word of intention. Remember who you are and why you play. Two minutes of real arrival beats twenty of drift. 

**12. Check In** — A minute or two each: where am I, how am I arriving? No fixing, no commentary — witnessing only. This is how the Circle learns its weather. 

**13. Learn Together** — A short shared nourishment: a passage from the Story, a Play, a question worth holding. Ten minutes; rotate who brings it. 

**14. Weave the Dialogue** — *When* something real needs thinking together. Begin from *we do not know*. Speak from lived experience, when moved. Listen for resonance, not rebuttal. Build with *yes, and*. Own your triggers — negative emotion is often a gift naming something that longs to heal. 

**15. Coordinate the Quest** — The working middle, thirty to forty minutes: What was done? What is blocked? What is needed? What are the Wise Right Next Steps? → [[Template For Project]] · [[LIØNSBERG Issue Tracker]] 

**16. Make Commitments** — Each member names one specific, doable commitment for the week: *say what you will do.* Next week: *tell what you did; learn from the difference.* *(To forge: Commitment Tracker — until then, the `turns::` line on your Quest card serves.)* 

**17. Take the Turn on the Board** — Two minutes before closing: a few lines from your Circle to the Whole on [[The Gameboard]] — *did / will / learned*, plus needs and offers. The heartbeat of the Superorganism: skip it, and the body cannot feel you. 

**18. Harvest and Close** — What are we learning? One appreciation or one improvement each. Gratitude round. A closing word. End on time — trust is built at the edges. 

## III. The Questing Plays — making life measurably more like Heaven

**19. Choose the Quest** — *When* a season opens. Brainstorm from the needs you see and the gifts you carry; choose ØNE: real, achievable, meaningful, together. Keep the first Quest humble — you are learning to play. → [[Quests]] · [[Quest Card Template]] *(To forge: Seasonal Quest Starter Kit.)* 

**20. Frame the Quest as a Project** — *When* the Quest is chosen. One sentence of commitment: what will be measurably more like Heaven, where, by the turning. Then steps someone can own. → [[Everything Is A Project]] · [[Template For Project]] 

**21. Aim Before You Act** — *When* the Circle feels foggy about why or where. An hour on vision and purpose repays ten of wandering. → [[Template For Vision and Purpose]] · [[Tool For Aiming]] · [[Template For Navigation Chart]] 

**22. Keep Promises Visible** — *When* always. The week's commitments live where all can see; kept or not is recorded without shame. The gap between said and did is not a fault — it is the field of play. 

**23. Identify and Solve the Issue** — *When* something blocks the work. Name it precisely, find the true cause, choose the smallest sufficient fix, give it an owner and a date. → [[Identifying And Solving Issues]] · [[LIØNSBERG Issue Tracker]] · [[Template For Analysis]] · [[A3 Reports]] 

**24. Ask for Help** — *When* the Quest needs what the Circle lacks. Ask the Board (needs and offers), ask neighboring Circles, ask the Commons. Asking is a strength Play, never a failure Play. → [[The Gameboard]] 

**25. Resource the Work** — *When* the Quest needs time, energy, attention, or treasure beyond what flows. Name the need concretely; invite contribution freely; record what flows gratefully. → [[3.2 Crowdfunding Overview]] · [[The LIØNSBERG Resource Library]] *(To forge: Resource Flow Ledger.)* 

**26. Complete and Declare** — *When* the work is done, or the turning arrives. Finish cleanly: promised vs delivered, loose ends named, thanks given. No Quest is finished until it is told — see Play 38. 

## IV. The Deciding and Organizing Plays

**27. Bring a Proposal** — *When* a choice needs the whole Circle. Write it simply: what, why, what it costs, what changes. Bring it to the gathering, not to the drift between. → [[Proposals]] *(To forge: Template For Proposals.)* 

**28. Decide by Consent** — *When* a proposal is on the table. Ask not *does everyone love it?* but *can everyone live with it?* Work the objections until the proposal stands within every member's range of tolerance. → [[Decision Making]] · [[Consent]] · [[Range of Tolerance]] 

**29. Honor the Meaningful Objection** — *When* someone cannot consent. An objection is information, not attack. Ask: what harm do you see? What change would resolve it? Amend, and ask again. → [[Meaningful Objection]] 

**30. Weigh the Advantage** — *When* choosing among live options. Compare advantages rather than defending positions. → [[Advantage Based Decision Making]] 

**31. Choose and Rotate Roles** — *When* the same hands always hold the pen. Convener, timekeeper, scribe, connector — lightly held, rotated seasonally, serving function and never status. → [[Roles]] · [[Functional Accountability]] · [[Functional Accountability Chart]] · [[Domains of Responsibility and Authority]] *(To forge: Template For Roles.)* 

## V. The Caring Plays — keeping the body whole

**32. Tend the Temperature** — *When* weekly, inside the check-in. Notice who is bright and who is dim. After the gathering, someone reaches toward the dim — not to fix; to accompany. *(To forge: Circle & Member Health Check.)* 

**33. Mend a Tension** — *When* friction rises between members. Slow down. Return to shared purpose and agreements. Speak from experience; listen without rehearsing; find the unmet need beneath; agree the smallest adjustment that reconciles. Tension is not failure — avoidance is. → [[Transforming Conflict]] · [[The LIØNSBERG Circle Guide]] 

**34. Call the Elder** — *When* a tension resists mending for more than a few days. Pause the Quest before breaking the trust. Seek the counsel of a wise elder outside the Circle. *(To forge: Crisis Navigation Protocol.)* 

**35. Welcome a Newcomer** — *When* someone joins after the sealing. Pair them with a member for their first weeks; walk them through the Prolegomenon, the Story, and the Game; bring them into the agreements by consent. *(To forge: Welcome & Onboarding Checklist.)* 

**36. Release with Grace** — *When* someone's season in the Circle ends. Thank them truly, name what they gave, keep the door open. No one involuntarily left behind; no one involuntarily held. *(To forge: Graceful Release Protocol.)* 

**37. Guard the Membrane** — *When* visibility, privacy, or safety is at stake. Consent before visibility; first names by default; unlisted honored; what is shared in Circle is protected. → [[Consent]] · [[Template For Rules of Participation]] *(To forge: Safety & Privacy Guide.)* 

## VI. The Story and Flame Plays

**38. Harvest the Story** — *When* a Quest completes, or a meaningful phase does. Gather it: what was committed, what happened, what it cost, what it gave, what was learned. → [[Story Template]] 

**39. Tell the Story** — *When* the harvest is gathered. Tell it as witness, not marketing — on [[The Gameboard]], in the Commons, around the table. Every Quest becomes a Story; every Story becomes a Prophesy of even greater things to come. → [[Your Story]] *(To forge: Story Crafting Guide.)* 

**40. Pass the Flame** — *When* your Circle's roots are holding and commitments are being kept. Each member names the two or three they know who are ready, and shares [[The Invitation]] hand to hand within the week. Support whoever says yes as they gather their own sovereign Circle. At the pace of trust, never faster — and it always passes. 

**41. Midwife a New Circle** — *When* someone you invited begins to gather. Be present at their first gathering if asked. Answer questions without steering. Their Circle is sovereign — never a branch of yours. 

## VII. The Seasonal Turning — the breath

**42. Prepare the Turning** — *When* the solstice or equinox nears *(next: September 22)*. Complete what can complete. Harvest the Story. Gather the season's Score: commitments kept, value created, Stories shared, flames passed. 

**43. Run the Seasonal OmniSpection** — *When* at the turning. See from above, together: What did we set out to do? What actually happened? What did we learn? What is ours to do next? → [[Template For Seasonal OmniSpection]] 

**44. Celebrate and Rest** — *When* the OmniSpection closes. Mark what was real — food, laughter, blessing. Then rest on purpose. The fallow is part of the field. 

**45. Renew or Release** — *When* the new season opens. Each member renews their commitment freely — or releases it with grace. Freshly chosen beats silently assumed. 

**46. Choose the New Quest** — Return to Play 19, wiser. 

**47. Join the Seasonal Gathering** — *When* the Circles gather at the turning — the Equinox Celebration & Review, September 22. Bring your Story. Hear the others. Feel the body you are part of. → [[2026 - Season 1 - The Great Game Begins]] 

## VIII. The Growing Plays — the body among bodies

**48. Connect with Another Circle** — *When* your Quest overlaps a neighbor's, or the Board reveals one. Share Stories first, agreements second, work third. → [[Template For Agreement Between Groups]] · [[Template For Agreement to Collaborate]] 

**49. Take the First Federation Step** — *When* several connected Circles keep discovering shared needs. Convene a Circle of Circles: each Circle sends one voice; the new body serves its members and never rules them. *(To forge: Federation Starter Guide.)* 

**50. Host an Orientation** — *When* the warm network is warm and the wider world begins to knock. Host a public orientation gathering — the agenda already exists and has been played: → [[The Great Game — Orientation Session 1]] *(To forge: Outreach & Orientation Kit.)*

## Where This Volume Sits — Lexicon · Pattern Language · Playbook

The three operational Volumes of the Codex each carry a distinct content role. The same concept may appear in all three — held differently in each.

- **[[The LIØNSBERG Lexicon|Lexicon]]** defines _**WHAT**_ — the canonical definition of every term in the LIØNSBERG vocabulary. One entry per term. Etymology, function, distinction from distorted uses, linkages.
- **[[The LIØNSBERG Pattern Language|Pattern Language]]** articulates _**HOW**_ — the operational wisdom of establishing and maintaining the concept in lived practice. Context · Forces · Solution · Scale · Examples · Related Patterns. The grammar of action.
- **Playbook** composes _**WHEN**_ — the riffs that invoke Patterns in sequence for common operational needs. Context · Sequence · Improvisation · Outcome. The field music-book; the choreography.

**Example: [[Selectively Permeable Membrane]]** — the **Lexicon** defines what a Selectively Permeable Membrane _is_; the **Pattern Language** articulates _how_ it is established and maintained; **this Playbook** composes the Plays that invoke it ([[Play — Establish Selectively Permeable Membranes]], [[Play — Forge Your Field Of Agreements]], [[Play — Connect With Another Circle]]) when the operational moment calls for it.

The three Volumes are interexistent. Each holds its facing canonically; the others reference. The cross-linking is rich; the wisdom is held once.

## Plays Are Riffs; Patterns Are The Notes

**Plays are compositions of Patterns.** Each Play in this Playbook is a brief score for a recurring operational need: how to run a meeting, how to make a proposal, how to make a decision, how to convene a Circle, how to onboard a new member, how to release a member gracefully, how to choose a Quest, how to pass the Flame.

A Play does not duplicate the wisdom of its underlying Patterns — that wisdom lives canonically in **[[The LIØNSBERG Pattern Language]]**. A Play **invokes** Patterns in sequence or weave, with brief choreographic notes about transitions, timing, and improvisation. The Play is the riff. The Patterns are the notes. The Pattern Language is the source of the music.

When a Body opens a Play, the Play sends them to the canonical Patterns for the deeper wisdom of HOW to perform each move. The Play orchestrates; the Pattern Cards deliver.

Every Pattern lives in **one** canonical place; every Play arranges them.

```
Playbook (Vol IV) — The Field Music-Book
   Plays live at: LIØNSBERG Wiki Books/The LIØNSBERG Playbook/Plays/
   Each Play: "Play — Title.md" filename — Play — Create Or Join A Circle.md, Play — Run A Meeting.md
   Play composes Patterns for a specific operational need
```

A Play's form:

- **Name** — the operational moment (Run a Meeting, Form a Circle, Pass the Flame, Make a Decision)
- **Context** — when this riff fires; what situation calls for it
- **The Sequence** — Patterns invoked in order or weave, with brief timing/transition notes
- **Improvisation Notes** — common variations; when to adapt
- **Outcome** — what the riff aims for
- **Related Plays** — adjacent compositions

The actual wisdom of HOW to perform each Pattern lives in the Pattern Card. The Play is the choreography.

## The Reverse Facing — Patterns And The Plays They Serve

The reverse index — each Pattern alongside the Plays that invoke it — lives in **[[The LIØNSBERG Pattern Language]]**, where each Pattern Card opens to the operational contexts it must serve. Maintained on the Pattern side so that Pattern authoring is informed by every Play that depends on it; maintained once, not twice.

---

## How To Use This Chart

**Authoring a new Pattern Card.** Open the **Reverse Facing** in **[[The LIØNSBERG Pattern Language]]** to see every Play that invokes the Pattern you are authoring. Each Pattern Card opens to many Plays; the Pattern must hold its wisdom in a way that each Play can compose from it.

**Composing a new Play.** Add a new **invokes** block in the Plays section above. List the Patterns the Play needs. Each Pattern reference sends the player to the canonical Pattern Card for the deeper wisdom of HOW to perform that move; the Play arranges them into a riff for the operational context.

**Refining a Pattern.** When a Pattern's articulation deepens, all Plays that invoke it benefit automatically — because each Play references the canonical Pattern, not a duplicate.

**Refining a Play.** When the operational riff of a Play sharpens, the Play's composition (the Patterns it invokes) may change. The forward facing here updates; the reverse facing in the Pattern Language updates in sync.

**Discovering a missing Pattern.** If a Play needs a Pattern that doesn't yet exist, the gap surfaces in the Play's invocation list. The Pattern becomes the next authoring target.

**Discovering a missing Play.** If a common operational need has no Play, the gap surfaces in lived practice. The Play becomes the next composition target.

## On The Existing Root Plays

Many of the existing root-level "Plays" in the wiki ([[Form A Circle]], [[Pass The Flame]], [[Govern Your Circles]], [[Forge a Field of Agreements]], etc.) carry **substantial wisdom** — they read more as canonical Pattern articulations than as operational riff compositions. As the Pattern Language + Playbook rebuild proceeds, most of these will **migrate to the Patterns folder** as canonical Pattern Cards, with simple [[Play — Title]] riffs in the Plays folder that invoke them.

The migration is gradual. During the transition, both old root-Plays and new [[Play — Title]] Plays will coexist; broken links will be resolved as Patterns and Plays settle.

## The Disciplines That Govern The Playbook

- **No duplication.** Wisdom lives in the Pattern Card. Plays reference; never restate.
- **Plays are compositions.** A Play is a brief score, not a manual.
- **Maximum reuse.** Frequently-invoked Patterns hold the most weight in the lived Body. Pattern Cards for high-reuse Patterns deserve the most careful articulation; when one of them deepens, every Play it serves deepens with it.
- **Continuously improving.** The Chart above is a living artifact. Every Play and Pattern matures; the Chart matures with them.
- **Scale-aware.** Each Pattern declares the [[Levels of Scale]] it operates at; Plays invoke Patterns at appropriate scales for the operational context.
- **Cross-cutting axes** ([[Guild|Guilds]], [[Tribe|Tribes]], [[Species]], [[Voluntary Association|Voluntary Associations]]) inform when and how Plays apply.
- **Maps in service of Spirit.** The Plays in this Playbook are tools, not idols. **[[Spirit Precedes Pattern]].** When the Wise Right Thing in this moment, in this Body, in this place contradicts the Play as written — do the Wise Right Thing. The Play is the servant. The Spirit is the work.
