# Play — Turn the Season

*The Seasonal Turning · a card of [[The LIØNSBERG Playbook]]*

*When* the solstice or equinox arrives *(next: September 22)*. The season's breath, in five movements: 

**Prepare the Turning** — *When* the solstice or equinox nears *(next: September 22)*. Complete what can complete. Harvest the Story. Gather the season's Score: commitments kept, value created, Stories shared, flames passed.

**Run the Seasonal OmniSpection** — *When* at the turning. See from above, together: What did we set out to do? What actually happened? What did we learn? What is ours to do next? → [[Template For Seasonal OmniSpection]]

**Celebrate and Rest** — *When* the OmniSpection closes. Mark what was real — food, laughter, blessing. Then rest on purpose. The fallow is part of the field.

**Renew or Release** — *When* the new season opens. Each member renews their commitment freely — or releases it with grace. Freshly chosen beats silently assumed.

**Choose the New Quest** — Return to Play 19, wiser.

*These movements are proto-Patterns: the same elements recur across many Plays — the First Gathering, the Weekly Turn, the Turning. As they prove themselves in play, they will be articulated into [[The LIØNSBERG Pattern Language]] as its operational Patterns.*
