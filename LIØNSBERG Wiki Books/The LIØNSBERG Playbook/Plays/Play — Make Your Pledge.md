# Play — Make Your Pledge

*The Opening Plays — from Invitation to living Circle · a card of [[The LIØNSBERG Playbook]]*

*When* you are ready to step from resonance into commitment. Make [[The Pledge]] — the first Commitment, on which all others stand: your weekly TEA for the season, your reciprocal resource contribution of any amount, and the gifts you bring. Update it any time; renew it at every turning. Then take your stand on [[The Gameboard]] — openly, by name, among all who stand: copy the [[Player Card Template]], or send word through [[Reaching Us]]. Until the pledge channel opens, speak your Pledge at your gathering and record it on your card. *(To forge: Pledge Form.)*
