# 36. The Instantiation Hubs and the Commons

Picture one [[Community]] of ten thousand souls. Season by season it discerns its own priorities, challenges, needs, and opportunities — and its fifty [[Cell|Cells]], trained, equipped, connected, and empowered, [[Stack Up And Align]] their [[Seasonal Quests]] to meet the needs, solve the problems, and accomplish the goals at hand. Now picture a hundred thousand such places. The whole planetary design is visible in that one town.

**The common needs call forth the commons.** Every locality's Cells face the same fundamental needs — spaces to gather, learn, heal, make, grow, store, celebrate; and the living expertise to run them. Most are unaffordable to any Cell or family in isolation, and absurd to duplicate fifty times in one town. The answer is [[Fractal Commonsing]]: the basic capacities of a flourishing life held in locally stewarded [[Commons]] with equal access for every member of the community — as locally appropriate, wise, right, and good.

**The commons federate into the Hub Network.** The [[LIØNSBERG Instantiation Hubs]] are that pattern built out at every scale: roughly one hundred thousand interlinked Local Hubs anchoring localities of ~100,000, supporting a million Community Hubs, supported by 150–200 Bioregional and 12–24 Georegional Hubs housing the heaviest shared capabilities — the full network, addressing every one of [[The 12 Pillars of LIØNSBERG]] for every family and community on Earth, buildable for approximately **$10–20 trillion** depending on how much existing infrastructure is repurposed. If a functional federated system serving all aspects of society can serve roughly one hundred thousand people ([[The Prophesy of Mondragon]]), then moving [[10 Billion People In 10 Years]] means co-creating roughly one hundred thousand of them — and that is precisely what the network does.

**The Commons learns as one.** Above the fractal commons stands the highest-order [[The Commons|LIØNSBERG Commons]] — aggregating the leading wisdom, solutions, technologies, templates, and patterns from every hub and locality, reconciling the best of them into [[The Prototype]], and offering it all onward as an interoperable [[Kit of Parts]]. Any locality that adopts and adapts any part joins the whole network's learning; every new instantiation begins where all previous ones arrived. And sovereignty holds all the way down: each individual, Cell, and locality [[Consciously Choose|consciously chooses]] what it shares, with which levels of the whole. ([[Data, Information, Knowledge, and Wisdom Sovereignty]])

**Hubs are called forth, never imposed.** Instantiation follows invitation: hubs are localized and resourced **in response to the self-organization and request of the localities themselves**, engaged in their own [[Local Quests]] — proposals flowing through [[The Prototype]]'s submittal process and the [[Mission Control]] guidance system, never assignments flowing down. Existing infrastructure leads the way: libraries transformed into community knowledge hubs, schools into hubs of lifelong project-based learning.

**The work organizes as Ecclesia Led Joint Ventures.** Around each of [[The 12 Pillars of LIØNSBERG]] — the core functions the failing institutions of the [[Old World]] can no longer fulfill — [[Ecclesia Led Joint Ventures]] form: continuously improving the quality, and continuously decreasing the cost, of every core function of society, fractally, by [[Subsidiarity]], through the hub network. Leading solutions are packaged as interoperable [[Soft Franchises]] — trained, coached, and mentored into local operation, learning across the whole array — and human and AI [[Solutions Matchmakers]] connect every Cell and community to the right tools just in time, with peer coaching and mentoring available to every person on the Quest.

**The resourcing is already designed.** The network is financed by the [[Swords to Ploughshares]] reallocation — the phased redirection of a fraction of what humanity already spends on the machinery of death — carried operationally in [[33. Resourcing the Transformation]].
