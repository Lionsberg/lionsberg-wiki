# 32. The Calling of the Ecclesia

Chapter 17 names [[The Grand Challenge]]: *how do we Move the Whole Population* [[FROM the Old TO the New]]? The 2026 seasons of play answered one part of that question the hard way: an invitation to *join* gathers watchers. Those who arrived waited for a center to act — and this Movement, by design, has no center. What was missing was the **calling**: no one had been asked, by name, by people who know them, to *lead*.

The answer is the **[[LIØNSBERG Ecclesia]]** — in the original Greek sense of *ekklēsia*: those *called out* of their homes into the public square to govern and to do the work of the city. Not an audience. A working assembly of the people, called out by the people.

## The two doors

**The open door — everyone.** Every person on Earth is invited to take up their voice: to resonate, to contribute any amount, and thereby to hold a **dynamic voice in governance** and a **dynamic stake in value**. No one is turned away, and [[18. No One Involuntarily Left Behind|No One Involuntarily Left Behind]]. This door leads, in time, to [[New Citizenship]] for all.

**The narrow door — those called out to lead.** The first act of every voice is to **nominate**: out of the hundred people you know and trust most, the two or three you would most trust to help lead your tribe and community through [[The Collapse-Ascension Nexus]].

The nomination laws are simple and load-bearing:

- You cannot nominate yourself — and beware anyone campaigning to be nominated.
- Not immediate family or romantic partners.
- Not celebrities, politicians, or figures you do not personally know.
- Nominate people you have personally watched navigate hardship faithfully — preferably for three years or more.
- If you cannot think of three people you trust to help lead your community through crisis, **the work is to go find them.**
- Your votes may be delegated, in whole or in part, to someone whose judgment of people you trust more than your own.

When a person is surfaced by three trusted nominations, they receive **The Invitation** — delivered in person by those who nominated them, the Flame passing hand to hand. Those who accept enter the Ecclesia as fellows, and every fellow walks the same five moves as every player of [[The Great Game of LIØNSBERG]]:

**A [[Superorganism]] is emerging. [[Accept The Invitation]]. [[Form A Cell]]. [[Choose A Quest]]. [[Share Your Stories]]. [[Pass The Flame]].**

**The calling persists.** Given the trauma and noise of the [[Old World]], no one is written off for not answering: a person may need to be invited as many as twenty times — by different voices, in different seasons — before they truly hear, attend, and respond. The Movement prepares for this, and keeps inviting.

## What the calling creates

- **The First Three Percent, found by the whole.** [[The First Three Percent]] are not self-appointed; they are surfaced by the trust of the 97% — roughly [[The 300]] in every community of 10,000, identified by their own neighbors.
- **The trust graph.** The web of nominations is a transparent map of trust across the awakening population — and, when the grid falters, a living contact-tree: every member knows their three and is known by theirs.
- **The inverted grammar.** No one nominated to lead through collapse believes someone else will do the work. Being chosen *as the worker* is the content of the message. The calling is the cure for center-dependence — the viral mechanism and the vaccine in one.

## The graph is guarded

The trust graph is the Movement's treasure — and, in hostile hands, it would be a targeting map. The [[Selectively Permeable Membrane]] therefore applies to the Ecclesia's own data as first-order law, **before the first thousand names exist**: a **public tier** (counts, Cells, Quests, Stories — the visible field); a **recognized tier** (what Cells share Cell-to-Cell by mutual consent); and a **private tier** — the person-graph itself, held locally by those it names, exportable and deletable by them, never pooled at any center. Disclosure defaults tighten with the jurisdiction's danger; and beneath every digital layer runs the analog contact-tree — each member knowing their three and known by theirs — which no seizure can reach.

## The calling keeps an immune system

Any mechanism that confers standing will be gamed; the calling guards itself. Nominations are weighted by believability — the demonstrated record of the nominator — so organized capture buys little; campaigning for nomination disqualifies; and because trust networks mirror those who start them, the Ecclesia consciously widens its reach beyond the founders' own kind — across tongues, lands, traditions, and generations — until the called-out look like Earth. Votes may be delegated to those whose judgment of character one trusts more than one's own.

## From calling to governance

The Ecclesia governs itself from the first cells outward, by structural triggers rather than dates ([[Temporal Markers]] serve the work; the work does not serve the markers):

1. The first **55–144 Co-Creators** stand ([[LIØNSBERG Cycles of Growth]], ~Cycle 10).
2. They nominate **5 to 10 respected representatives** to seed the [[System Wide Master Planning Group]] and [[System Wide Master Planning Process]].
3. As the next cycles complete (~200–1,000 Co-Creators), the first [[LIØNSBERG General Assembly]] convenes, refines, and collectively ratifies the [[10 Year Grand Strategy]] and this Strategy and Plan.
4. The [[New Vision and Plan]] is broadcast at planetary scale — presenting humanity with a living alternative to the failing [[Existing Order]].

From that point forward, the *how* belongs — as it always must — to the called-out themselves.
