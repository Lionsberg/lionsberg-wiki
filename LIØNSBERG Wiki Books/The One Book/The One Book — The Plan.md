# Movement V · The Plan

*Where are we? Why are we here? Who calls, and what are we building? You have walked four Parts to stand at the only question left that matters: **how do we get there — from here, together, in time?***

This is the Plan. Read it knowing what it is not: it is not a program awaiting an administrator, and it is not a promise that someone will do this for you. There is no them. The Plan is a description of how *we* move — and its deepest secret is given away at the start: **you are in it.**

## Not an organization — an organism

Every empire that ever set out to change the world built a headquarters, and every headquarters was captured, corrupted, or killed. So the Plan builds none. It grows an organism: sovereign [[Cell|Cells]] of three to thirteen souls, each carrying the complete code, each able to sustain itself, do real work, and plant the next Cell — federating into a [[Superorganism of Cells]] with **nothing at the center to attack**. Though ninety-nine percent were lost, any surviving Cell could regenerate the whole. You cannot bomb it, sue it, buy it, or behead it. It exists everywhere and nowhere at once — and it is already growing.

## The Calling

Movements that broadcast gather watchers; the Plan **calls**. Every person on Earth is invited to take up their voice — and the first act of every voice is to name the two or three people they would most trust to lead their own community through the crossing. Those surfaced by the trust of the people who know them best receive The Invitation, delivered by the hands that named them. They are [[The First Three Percent]] — roughly 250 million leaders, called out not by any center but by the 97% who then resource them: this is the [[LIØNSBERG Ecclesia]], the working assembly of the people, called out by the people. The web of nominations is itself a treasure — a living map of trust that doubles, when grids fail, as the contact-tree by which every member still knows their three — and it is guarded like a treasure: held in tiers, the deepest layer belonging only to those it names.

Every fellow, and every player, walks the same five moves: **[[Accept The Invitation]] · [[Form A Cell]] · [[Choose A Quest]] · [[Share Your Stories]] · [[Pass The Flame]].** Growth is measured the way life measures it — the Fibonacci spiral of the [[LIØNSBERG Cycles of Growth]], where only what has matured replicates.

## The stages — every number in its season

The Plan states its economics without illusion. **The seed cycles are funded entirely by the Movement's own people** — contribution, the first tenth tithed to the [[Commons]] of whatever table you sit at, and the whole pool dividing across the fellows at every new and full moon, so wealth never pools and no more than two weeks can ever be captured or lost. As Cells multiply, their own seasonal [[Seasonal Quests|Quest]] flows become the engine — fifty million Cells directing even modest sums is provision at the scale of nations, flowing peer to peer through [[Centers of Distribution]] without ever passing a center. And only after undeniable planetary presence come the great harvests: [[Swords to Ploughshares]] — the phased redirection of what humanity already spends on the machinery of death, more than enough to build the [[LIØNSBERG Instantiation Hubs]] serving every community on Earth. **No stage's plan depends on a later stage's pool** — which is why the Movement cannot be bought, and why its legitimacy is beyond dispute.

## The system that carries it

Every locality gains its commons of shared capacity — the hubs, growing from libraries and schools before a single stone is laid — provisioned by [[Fractal Commonsing]] and learning as one body: every solution submitted to [[The Prototype]], reconciled into the [[Kit of Parts]], offered back to all, so no community ever starts from nothing. A [[Mission Control]] function serves the whole through the crossing — stewarding the Prototype's learning and keeping a two-way guidance line with the arrayed Ecclesia — equipping always, commanding never, and distributing its own stewardship away by design, so that no single life is ever again a single point of failure. The [[38. The Shadow Work|shadow is faced without flinching]]; the [[42. The Great Stewardship|living world is stewarded, not owned]]; and everything is planned at every scale by the people of that scale, woven — never overruled — into one interoperating fabric.

## Time, and the truth

The [[10 Year Grand Strategy]] began in Spring 2024; a [[20 Year Generational Contingency]] stands behind it; and over both stands [[The Window]] — the interval in which the leap remains possible at all, which no one can calculate and which may close at an unknown time. So the Plan does two things few plans dare: **it states its bets** — the calling compounds; formation holds the called; the economics ignite from below; visibility converts — each instrumented, each with a named threshold at which the Plan itself adapts; and **it keeps [[34. The Record|the Record]]** — the gauge that cannot lie, where every season's progress and every variance is written plainly, lateness consumed knowingly, never hidden and never despaired over. A Plan honest about uncertainty is not weaker; it is the only kind worthy of what hangs in the balance.

## Where the Plan ends

Every person who joins walks three years of healing and formation into full [[New Citizenship]]; the youth begin at fifteen and rise at eighteen; and when the crossing is complete comes [[The Greatest Celebration and Jubilee In History]] — debts reset, captives freed, ancestral lands restored, the [[New Era]] begun. That is the whole road, from this evening to the feast.
