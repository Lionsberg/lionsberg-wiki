# 2. Executive Summary

The LIØNSBERG Strategy and Plan centers around [[The Great Refounding]] of our civilization: the progressive invitation and reorientation of humanity into a [[New Covenant]] of omniconsiderate [[Right Relationship]] with Life, Society, the Cosmos, and the Generative Source of [[All]].

The basic strategy is to move the entire human population, during the course of the [[10 Year Grand Strategy]], out of [[The Caves]] of the [[Old World]] — from the omnicidal [[Old Paradigm]] and [[Old Pattern Language]] into a fully regenerative [[New Paradigm]] and [[New Pattern Language]] — while co-creating the infrastructure, pathways, and support that make [[The Quest]] [[FROM the Old TO the New]] navigable for [[All Humanity]], with no one [[Involuntarily Left Behind]].

The Strategy and Plan includes:
1. **A New Story** — to reunite the narrative structure of human consciousness ([[The Story of LIØNSBERG]] · [[22. The Story - The Quest Towards The Goal of Greatest Worth|ch. 22]])
2. **The Calling of the Ecclesia** — everyone on Earth invited to take up their voice; the first act of every voice the calling out of [[The First Three Percent]] by the trust of their own people ([[32. The Calling of the Ecclesia|ch. 32]])
3. **A New Covenant** — inviting all humanity back into Right Relationship with the Creator and All Creation
4. **A New Commons** — gathering up [[Our Collective Inheritance]] of wisdom, knowledge, resources, solutions, and technologies ([[36. The Instantiation Hubs and the Commons|ch. 36]])
5. **A New Earth Operating System** — connecting and empowering the diverse array of individuals, [[Cell|Cells]], and localities as unique parts of ØNE Body ([[35. The Anatomy of the Body|ch. 35]])
6. **A New Platform** — making The Movement visible to itself, with equal access to The Commons for all ([[The Platform]])
7. **A New Infrastructure Network** — the [[LIØNSBERG Instantiation Hubs]], optimally decentralizing and relocalizing every pillar of life and society ([[36. The Instantiation Hubs and the Commons|ch. 36]])
8. **The Historic Cultural Moment** — reaching the hearts and minds of billions, in a thousand tongues ([[41. The Historic Cultural Moment|ch. 41]])
9. **Connecting and empowering The First Three Percent** — ~250 million leaders in ~50 million Cells, the milestone marked for 2026, its variance carried honestly in [[34. The Record]] — creating the distributed leadership required in every domain and locality
10. **Inviting all humanity** — equal access and [[New Citizenship]] for All, no one involuntarily left behind ([[18. No One Involuntarily Left Behind|ch. 18]])
11. **Three years of healing, reorientation, and formation** for every person who joins ([[The 3 Year Lag]] · [[8. Timing|ch. 8]])
12. **Transforming our weapons into tools of life** — [[Swords to Ploughshares]], repurposing our militaries and [[High Science Technology]] from mutual destruction to co-creation ([[38. The Shadow Work|ch. 38]])
13. **[[The Greatest Celebration and Jubilee In History]]** — to celebrate, reset the debts, liberate the captives, restore the ancestral lands, and inaugurate the [[New Era]]

**Timing**: Following [[The 7 Year Window of Preparatory Work and Learning]], [[The Transformation]] proceeds through the [[10 Year Grand Strategy]] that began March 1, 2024, backed by the carefully managed [[20 Year Generational Contingency]] — all held within [[The Window]], which may close at an unknown time ([[8. Timing|ch. 8]] · [[23. How Much Time Do We Have?|ch. 23]]).

**Investment**: The entire program of action is accomplished by redirecting *already-flowing* resource, energy, and attention away from mutual destruction and [[Waste]] — **in strict stages** ([[43. The Bets and the Stages|ch. 43]]): the early cycles funded entirely from the bottom up by the Movement's own people (contribution, tithe, and seasonal [[Seasonal Quests|Quest]] flows — which is why it cannot be bought); philanthropic redirection as the fruit becomes visible; and only after undeniable planetary presence, the great harvests — [[Swords to Ploughshares]] and the redirection of governmental waste. No stage's plan depends on a later stage's pool.

**Expected Returns**: Technically beyond any discount rate's reach — following the Jubilee, every Citizen of Earth holds an equal stake in the most valuable co-operative body ever created, with a [[Standard Of Living]] and [[Quality of Life]] superior to any enjoyed in the past. The treasury that carries the flows is governed by unbending laws: radical transparency, anti-pooling distribution at every new and full moon, compliance in every jurisdiction touched, and a center that equips but never operates ([[33. Resourcing the Transformation|ch. 33]]).

***Welcome to the adventure and opportunity of a lifetime.***
