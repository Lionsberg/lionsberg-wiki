# Boredom
Boredom is the reading of engagement starved — capacity present, but unmet by challenge or meaning.
**Felt Sense.** Restless flatness. Time thickening, attention sliding off everything it touches; the body fidgeting for a task worth its strength. An ache not of emptiness but of unused fullness.
**What It Signals.** Boredom reports a gap between what the being can do and what the moment asks: the instrument is tuned, and nothing worthy is being played. It is a reading about *aliveness*, not entertainment. Where boredom recurs, the gradient is naming a place gone under-alive — a place your gifts are not being spent.
**Its Gradient.** [[Apathy]] ← Boredom → [[Restlessness]] → [[Interest]] → [[Motivation]] → [[Engagement]]. Its misfit cousin is [[Discomfort]]; its hardened form drifts toward [[Resignation]].
**Its Gift.** A compass — and a guardian. Boredom marks where life has gone under-alive, pointing as surely as [[Joy]] points, only from the other side. And it guards the gift of attention from waste — the finite fuel a Sovereign carries toward [[The Goal]].
**Its Distortion.** Numbed with stimulation — the feed, the scroll, the endless snack of novelty — until the signal underneath, *this is not your path*, is never read. The [[Old World]] sells anesthetics for boredom by the billion, because a being who reads it would change their life.
**The Move.** Do not feed it noise. Sit with it long enough to hear the question it carries: *what would be worthy of me here?* Then either bring fuller capacity to the task at hand — depth is an exit — or follow the compass toward work that fits your actual size.
