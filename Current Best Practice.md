# Current Best Practice

A Current Best Practice is a recommended way of doing something, held humbly: the finest way we presently know, offered for use, and revised the moment lived experience reveals a better one.

It completes a family of three, each the enactable, shareable, or graspable rendering of something greater than itself:

- **[[Current Best Understanding]]** — what we presently *grasp*; it falls short of the Reality it perceives.
- **[[Current Best Articulation]]** — what we have presently *rendered into form*; it falls short of the Understanding it expresses.
- **Current Best Practice** — how we presently *act*; it falls short of the Ideal Practice it reaches toward.

At the top of every holarchy of practice stands an ineffable referent ([[Essences, Renderings, And Ineffable Referents]]): the perfect Way of doing the thing, which — like the [[Meta]] level of every concept and category — lies beyond definition, beyond our [[Current Best Understanding]], and further beyond anything we can articulate or codify. We do not pretend to possess it. What we hold instead is a *recommendation at the top of the holarchy*: the Current Best Practice, which every practitioner then adapts to their own domain, just as every [[Instantiation]] adapts [[The Prototype]] to local reality.

This is [[The Creative Cascade]] applied to practices. The ineffable Way descends through our best conception of it into a recommended practice, which is adapted in a thousand local domains — and the learnings of every adaptation flow back up, refining the recommendation for all who come after. A practice that cannot learn from its practitioners is not a practice; it is a dogma.

**This is why we say "good practice," never "best practice."** To call a practice *best* is to claim the top of the holarchy — to declare the search finished and the Ideal possessed, which no one can truthfully do. A *good* practice claims only what can be honestly claimed: that it serves, that it is the current best we know, and that it expects to be surpassed. The humility is not decoration; it is what keeps the practice alive and the [[Way]] open.

## The Discipline

- Hold every practice as a recommendation, never a law — binding only until a better way proves truer.
- Adapt honestly to the local domain; the adaptation is not deviance, it is the practice working.
- Send the learnings home, so the recommendation improves for [[All]].
- Never let a practice harden into identity or dogma; the moment "how we do things" becomes untouchable, it has become an idol ([[The Creative Cascade]]'s discipline).
- Honor the gap between the practice and the ineffable Way it serves.
