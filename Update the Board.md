# Update the Board

*The one weekly door — how Players and Circles keep the Game, and the Superorganism, visible to itself.*

---

Once a week, at the close of your gathering, your Circle's board-tender takes two minutes here. That is the whole discipline. Through this one door flow all the updates the Board lives on: 

- **A Circle's Weekly Turn** — the five lines: *DID (against last week's WILL, honestly) · WILL · LEARNED · NEEDS · OFFERS* — plus commitments kept (x of y). → [[Template For Weekly Turn Post]] 
- **A Player's update** — a new or renewed [[The Pledge|Pledge]], gifts, a change of state. 
- **A sealing** — your new Circle, taking its place. → [[Play — Seal the Circle]] 
- **A new Quest chosen** · **a Story harvested**. 

**How, this week:** the update form is being established — it will live right here, one tap from [[The Gameboard|the Board]]. Until it opens: post your Turn to the `#turns` channel of [[The Communication Platform]], or send it through any door on [[Reaching Us]], and the stewards will tend it onto the Board. 

**What happens to what you send:** it becomes the record — your Quest card's `turns::` line, your cards in The Commons, the tiles on the Board. The needs you name are seen by the whole body; the offers you name answer someone else's need. This is how one body of many Circles knows itself, plans itself, and moves as one.
