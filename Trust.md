# Trust

Trust, both organizational and individual, is required for Integrated Delivery and co-creation.  Trust must be a common thread running through the entire [[Program of Action]] and will provide the foundation for [[Collaboration]].  Trust is essentially a decision each person on the project makes every day to trust the other participants. 

Trust requires that we count on the inner sense of responsibility, integrity, and capability, having faith that others will choose to act in a trustworthy manner, while simultaneously recognizing the possibility that they may choose to betray the faith and trust we have placed in them. 

Since each participant is asked to focus on project goals and continuous [[Flow of Work]] through the entire Program, each must be empowered by their groups to interweave the short term goals of the group with the long term goals of the project, the overarching Program, and the [[LIØNSBERG System]] as a Whole. 

Trust is realized and progressively grown in different ways for different people. One primary way Trust is built is through making and fulfilling commitments, in the context of clearly defined relationships and culture guided by the Spirit of Truth and Love.  [[Commitments]] form the basis of communication between team members.  Team members must request clear and realistic commitments, strive to keep their own commitments, and timely notify team members if a commitment cannot be met.  **_An Integrated Delivery System becomes_** **_a network of commitments that depends upon all participants keeping their commitments (i.e., being trustworthy and reliable)_**. Because policing of individual activities wastes time and effort, team members must rely upon others to honor commitments or to immediately notify the team when confidence is lost in the ability to deliver as promised.

## As a Felt Reading

**Felt Sense.** The guard standing down in another's presence — weight allowed onto the bond, like a body leaning back into a rope it believes will hold. **What It Signals.** Trust is the felt reading of a bond's load-bearing capacity: the wager, renewed daily as this page says, that the other will choose faithfulness — made with open eyes about the possibility of betrayal. It is what makes every network of [[Commitments]] possible: the [[Superorganism]]'s connective tissue, grown one kept promise at a time. **Its Gradient.** Suspicion ← wariness … Trust → confidence → [[Faith]] — and across the band, its rupture: [[Betrayal]], and the long regrowth after. **Its Gift & Distortion.** The gift: everything cooperative runs on it — trust is the cheapest infrastructure and the most expensive to rebuild; policing is the tax paid where it is absent. Distorted, it becomes naivety (trust unread against evidence) or its inversion — blanket distrust, which starves every bond to protect against one. **The Move.** Extend trust in right-sized increments; keep your own promises as the first practice ([[Move Four]]); notify early when one cannot be kept; and when trust is broken, read [[Betrayal]] honestly before choosing, as a Sovereign, whether and how to rebuild ([[Emotion]]).
