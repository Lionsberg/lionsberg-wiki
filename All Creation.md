# All Creation

All Creation is the totality of what is created — every being, every world, every scale of the manifest — held as one living whole.

The phrase joins two of the canon's deepest words on purpose: [[All]], the complete family of the [[ØNE]]'s expression, and [[Creation]], the ongoing work and act of the [[Creator]]. Said together, they refuse every smaller boundary: not our species only, not our world only, not the visible only — everything that is, woven in one [[InterExistence]] ([[The Holarchy of the ØNE]]).

All Creation names the true scope of the [[Meta Project]]'s care. The partnership is with the [[Creator]], with [[One Another]], and with All Creation — the [[Living System]] as covenant partner, not resource pool. Harms register at this scale (crimes against humanity are also crimes against the Creator and All Creation), and so does the [[Aim]]: the flourishing of All Creation, guided toward [[The Goal]], forever.
