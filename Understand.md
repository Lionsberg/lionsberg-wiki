# Understand

To [[Understand]] is to move beyond holding information *about* a thing into grasping the thing in its context — how it works, why it is as it is, what it is for, and how it relates to the whole. In the ancient ordering, [[Knowledge]] is superordinate to information and subordinate to [[Understanding]]; and understanding, in turn, ripens toward [[Wisdom]].

Our ability to understand is one of the three limits on every [[Map of Reality]] — alongside our ability to [[Perceive]] and to [[Describe]]. Understanding cannot be rushed or merely read into being: it deepens through lived engagement, through [[Mastery]], and through the humility to keep updating the map when Reality discloses what the map had missed.

*Make your ear attentive to [[Wisdom]]. Incline your heart to [[Understanding]].*
