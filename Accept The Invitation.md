# Accept The Invitation

A [[Superorganism]] is emerging — and you are being invited into it.

**Accept The Invitation** is the first of the five moves of [[The Great Game of LIØNSBERG]]:

**Accept The Invitation. [[Form A Cell]]. [[Choose A Quest]]. Share Your Stories. [[Pass The Flame]].**

[[The Invitation]] may reach you as a link passed from a friend, a word spoken at a kitchen table, or a nomination — two or three people who know you, who have watched you navigate hardship faithfully, naming you as one they would trust to help lead their community through [[The Collapse-Ascension Nexus]].

However it arrives, accepting it means the same thing:

- **You stop waiting.** No one is coming from the center to do this for you. There is no center. There is the Pattern, the Seed, and you.
- **You say yes to the work** — not to a membership, an audience, or a program, but to real work: co-creating [[Heaven On Earth]], beginning exactly where you stand.
- **You take up your voice** — a dynamic voice in the governance of what we build together, and a dynamic stake in its value, with the greater responsibility both carry: to steward and to serve.
- **You commit to the moves.** Acceptance completes itself in action — a [[Cell]] formed, a [[Quest]] chosen, Stories shared, the Flame passed. An invitation accepted but never acted on was never truly accepted.

Read [[The Invitation]]. Read the [[Golden Seed]]. Sit with it. And when the yes rises —

Begin.
