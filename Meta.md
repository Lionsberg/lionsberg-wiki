# Meta

**Meta** points toward the ultimately ineffable top of the [[Total Nested Holarchy]] of a category — the eternal, universal, ultimate, self-referential, highest order, all encompassing, most transcendent level of an idea, where the category rejoins [[ØNE]].

Two things must be held together about every Meta term:

**The referent is ineffable.** The Meta level of any concept or category lies beyond the [[Boundary of Knowing]] — beyond our [[Current Best Understanding]], and further still beyond our [[Current Best Articulation]] ([[Essences, Renderings, And Ineffable Referents]]). This is why Meta terms are purposefully left abstract: to concretize them would be to commit the [[Patterns of Failure|Pattern of Failure]] of [[Concretizing The Absolute]] — mistaking our rendering for the Reality and building an idol where a window was meant to be.

**The conception is a rendering.** When we say the Meta X is "the highest universal overarching and uniting X of Xs, that by definition contains within itself and orders all other possible Xs," we are describing our best *conception* — the finest fingerpost we can raise. The fingerpost is refined forever; what it points toward does not move.

For example, we could speak of:

The [[Meta Story]]: the highest universal overarching and uniting "Story of stories", that by definition contains within itself and orders all other possible stories.

The [[Meta Intention]]: the highest universal overarching and uniting "Intention of intentions", that by definition contains within itself and orders all other possible intentions.

The [[Meta Goal]]: the highest universal overarching and uniting "Goal of goals", that by definition contains within itself and orders all other possible goals.

The [[Meta Project]]: the highest universal overarching and uniting "project of projects", that by definition contains within itself and orders all other possible projects.

The [[Meta Game]]: the highest universal overarching and uniting "Game of games", that by definition contains within itself and orders all other possible games.

It is critical to note that *by definition*, the intention, object, or goal of the [[Meta Project]] or [[Meta Game]] could not be anything other than the [[Meta Goal]].

This is because [[All]] are fundamentally and functionally unified by [[One Thing]]. Every Meta term is the top of its own holarchy — and every such top is the same summit approached up a different face of the same mountain. At the Meta level, all categories reunify: the Meta Story, the Meta Goal, the Meta Intention, the Meta Game are, ultimately, [[ØNE]].
