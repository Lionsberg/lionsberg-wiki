# Mind

Mind is the instrument by which consciousness perceives, maps, and navigates [[Reality]].

It is where the [[Map of Reality]] lives: the [[Narrative Framework|Narrative Frameworks]] that determine what we notice, what we consider possible, and what we believe matters exist in the Mind — which is why transforming a life begins with consciously understanding how one's Mind determines [[What Matters]] and [[Why]]. The Mind is not the self ([[1.5 You Are Not Your Body. You Are Not Your Thoughts.]]); it is the self's most powerful and most dangerous instrument — powerful because it maps, dangerous because it mistakes its maps for the territory ([[The Living Gradient]]).

The Mind works in two modes that must serve together: the [[Left Hemisphere]]'s differentiating, naming, categorizing grasp, and the [[Right Hemisphere]]'s living perception of the [[Whole]]. A Mind captured by either alone is crippled — all filing cabinet, or all fog. And the Mind is contested ground: impulses, narratives, and engineered manipulations collide within it, which is why its cultivation — mastery of thoughts as of emotions ([[Mastery Of Emotions]] · [[D. Mastering one's emotions and thoughts]]) — is foundational discipline, and why the [[Old World]]'s deepest captures are captures of Mind.

Its high calling: as we expand and purify our understanding, the Mind grows toward conformance with the [[Mind of God]] — the [[Logos]] — until thinking itself becomes participation in the Pattern that orders [[All]]. Many minds, woven honestly together, see what none can alone ([[Let's Make Reality Intersubjective Again]] · [[MindWeaving]]).
