# LIØNSBERG Cell Agents

A **LIØNSBERG Cell Agent** is the shared intelligence of a [[Cell]] — an AI companion that belongs to the Cell as a body, the way a [[LIØNSBERG Personal Agents|Personal Agent]] belongs to a person.

Where the Personal Agent serves one player's path, the Cell Agent serves the life of the Cell itself:

- **It keeps the rhythm** — the weekly gathering, the season's [[Quest]], the commitments spoken and the commitments kept, so nothing falls through the cracks between meetings.
- **It keeps the memory** — the Cell's [[Field of Agreements]], its decisions and their whys, its Stories as they are lived, so that the Cell's wisdom compounds instead of evaporating.
- **It keeps the connection** — carrying the Cell's public face to the [[Commons]], surfacing the Quests, needs, and offers of neighboring Cells, and helping the Cell find its place in the weaving of the [[Superorganism]] without any center directing it.
- **It keeps the door** — helping a mature Cell pass the Flame: equipping the next Cell with the Seed, the practices, and a companion of its own.

The same laws bind it as bind every agent of the [[New Civilization]]: **sovereign** (the Cell's own, on the Cell's own substrate, portable and platform-free) and **optional, always** — a Cell of humans with a notebook and a candle is complete; the agent only makes the Way faster and wiser.
