# Depression
Depression is the gray flatline where feeling itself dims — less an emotion than emotion's power outage.
**Felt Sense.** Not sadness but the absence of signal. Colors mute, food loses taste, love reads as fact instead of warmth. Time thickens; the smallest act costs everything. Where [[Grief]] comes in waves, depression is the sea gone still and gray — feeling pressed down, as the word says.
**What It Signals.** Often, depression is weather that froze: [[Grief]] unwept, anger unspoken, overwhelm unrelieved — a feeling too large or too forbidden, put on ice so life could continue. The flatline is the cost of the freezing. But not always: clinical depression is real, has real bodily roots, and is no failure of character or practice. When the gray persists, deepens, or darkens toward not wanting to be here, it deserves skilled care — medicine, therapy, both. Seeking that help is [[Sovereignty]] in action, not its surrender.
**Its Gradient.** The frozen form of the whole sadness band — [[Sorrow]], [[Grief]], and [[Despair]] can each freeze into it. Its near kin are [[Hopelessness]] and [[Misery]].
**Its Gift.** Hidden inside the shutdown is a forced stop: depression halts a life that had become unlivable and refuses to restart it unchanged. Something — a pace, a role, a silence — is being flagged for facing.
**Its Distortion.** Depression's own voice is the distortion: it narrates the flatline as truth — *this gray is what the world really is.* It is not. It is the reading of a dammed instrument, not of Reality.
**The Move.** Do not fight it alone, and do not only practice at it — get real help when help is due. Then, gently, thaw: motion before motivation, one small warm thing daily, one person told the truth. Ask the stopped life the stop's question: *what was I not facing?* What thaws first is usually what was frozen first.
Depression is also one pole of the energetic spectrum of [[Enthusiasm]] — mania inappropriately motivating toward delusional stories, depression inappropriately demotivating from beneficial, actionable ones ([[1.14 The Poles Of Mania And Depression]]). Discernment of the [[Wise Right]] way forward requires returning the system to a clear, balanced point of energy and consciousness — which is transformation of the inner and outer [[Context]], never mere willpower.
