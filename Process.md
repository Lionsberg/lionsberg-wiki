# Process

A Process is ordered becoming — a sequence of actions or transformations that, executed, produces [[Throughput]] toward an aim.

In the deepest register, process is what Reality is made of: not static things but livings — flowing, exchanging, becoming ([[The Living Gradient]]). A "thing" is a slow process; a being is a process that maintains itself; [[Creation]] is the Meta-process, still running. To see the world as process is to see it truly.

In the delivery register, Process is a precise level of the work: the [[Program]] holds the whole; [[Project|Projects]] deliver its parts; Processes are the repeatable sequences of commitments and actions inside them that actually produce [[Value]] ([[Goal Delivery System]]). Well-designed processes carry three marks: they **flow** without stoppage ([[Flow]]), they **learn** — every execution feeding improvement back into the design ([[Prototype Control]]), and they remain **servants** — the moment a process is followed for its own sake against its purpose, it has become the [[Old World]]'s paperwork.

And there is [[The Process]] — the great one: the continuous improvement of the whole, the crossing [[FROM the Old TO the New]], the [[Sacred Ascent]] of beings and civilizations. Every small process either serves it or obstructs it; that is the test of every procedure ever written.
