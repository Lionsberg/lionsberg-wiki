# Vibration

Vibration is the foundational oscillation from which manifestation arises — what a living process *is doing*, beneath every appearance of stillness.

Nothing in the universe is static. What we perceive as solid things are patterns of movement stable enough to name: standing waves in the one field of [[ØNE]] [[Living System]]. Matter vibrates; life pulses; consciousness attends in waves; communities breathe in rhythms of gathering and dispersing. To say *everything vibrates* is not metaphor draped over physics — it is the plainest description of a Reality that is process all the way down ([[The Living Gradient]]).

Every vibration has a quality — its [[Frequency]] — and every meeting of vibrations is either resonant, dissonant, or somewhere on the gradient between ([[Resonance]]). This is why presence matters, why places carry atmosphere, why a room changes when a person enters it: living processes read and affect one another's vibration constantly, beneath language ([[Attunement]]).
