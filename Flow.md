# Flow

Flow is the unobstructed movement of life through a system — and the state of a being or work moving with it.

Flow moves at every scale, and all the scales are one pattern:

**The Divine Flow** — the great current of [[InterExistence]] itself, the movement of [[Life]], [[Time]], and [[Energy]] through [[All]] things from and toward [[ØNE]]. To live well is not to dam or race this current but to join it — the oldest teaching of the [[Way]] ([[1.13 Harmony, Equilibrium, Right Relationship, Flow, Oneness]]).

**The Sovereign's flow** — each being holds a daily, allocatable flow of [[Time]], [[Attention]], and [[Energy]]: the true currency of a life, and of every [[Commitments|Commitment]] made to others (a Circle pledging a tenth of its available flow is pledging its life-current, not an abstraction).

**The flow of [[Work]] and [[Value]]** — in the delivery system, Flow is the design criterion: [[Work]] planned to move without stoppage, [[Waste]] removed because it obstructs, [[Value]] streaming to where it serves ([[Flow of Work]] · [[Value Stream Mapping]]). A Program's health is read in its flow the way a body's is read in its circulation.

**The flow state** — the felt signature of joining any of these: the being fully given to the work, self-consciousness gone, action and awareness merged. Effortlessness is not the absence of effort but effort in perfect [[Attunement]].

Obstruction anywhere — a dammed emotion ([[Emotion]]), a hoarded resource, a bottlenecked Program, a refused calling — produces the same result at every scale: stagnation behind the dam, drought beyond it. The move is always the same: find the obstruction, and restore the flow.
