# Irony
Irony is the wry reading of the gap between what is said or expected and what is.
**Felt Sense.** Double-vision, felt. One eyebrow rises while the other stays put; a dry half-smile; the small internal *click* of two layers snapping into view at once — the surface and the actual. Cooler than laughter, warmer than judgment: amusement with a fleck of ache in it.
**What It Signals.** Irony reports a gap — between word and deed, expectation and outcome, the [[Map of Reality]] and the territory it claimed to cover — seen from just enough altitude that the gap becomes legible rather than merely painful. It is a threshold reading of the second order: not the map breaking, but the self watching maps break, its own included. The fire station burns; the preacher of patience snaps; the plan to simplify complicates. Irony is what noticing feels like.
**Its Gradient.** The reflective end of the map-strain band: [[Confusion]] → [[Cognitive Dissonance]] → [[Paradox]] → irony. Kept warm, it neighbors [[Amusement]] and [[Humor]]; run cold, it slides toward [[Sarcasm]], [[Cynicism]], and [[Contempt]].
**Its Gift.** Humility and humor about all maps. Irony vaccinates against self-seriousness: whoever has truly felt it can no longer mistake their map for the territory, or their pronouncements for guarantees. It is the laughter that keeps [[Truth]]-seeking honest.
**Its Distortion.** Irony as permanent armor — the refusal ever to mean anything, every sincerity pre-mocked so nothing can wound. The [[Old World]]'s late hours mistook this reflex for intelligence; it was anesthesia. Detachment that never lands is not seeing double — it is declining to see at all.
**The Move.** Enjoy the click, then cash it in. Ask: *what does this gap teach about my map?* Let irony do its one hour of work — humbling, lightening — and then commit anyway. The Sovereign sees the gap and still means what they say.
