# Belonging

The value of belonging represents a sense of connection and belonging to a larger community or group. It involves feeling accepted, valued, and supported by others, and can be achieved through a sense of shared identity, shared values, and shared experiences.

## As a Felt Reading

**Felt Sense.** Settling. The shoulders drop, the guard stands down, the body stops auditioning; warmth in the chest of being *among one's own*. **What It Signals.** Belonging reports that the bond between self and circle is live and load-bearing — accepted, valued, woven in ([[Connection]] sustained into membership). It is a cell's confirmation that it has found its tissue in the [[Superorganism]]. **Its Gradient.** [[Invisibility]] ← [[Loneliness]] ← [[Disconnection]] … [[Connection]] → Belonging → [[Communion]]. **Its Gift & Distortion.** The ground from which sovereign risk becomes possible — we dare most where we belong. Distorted, it becomes belonging purchased by self-erasure (fitting in, which is belonging's counterfeit), or the closed circle that belongs *against* others. True Belonging never costs the self, and never requires an enemy ([[Right Relationship]]). **The Move.** Contribute — belonging deepens through giving, not auditioning; and widen the circle: every [[Circle]] belongs to larger circles, out to [[All]] ([[Emotion]]).
