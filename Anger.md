# Anger
Anger is the reading that something precious is being violated or blocked.
**Felt Sense.** Heat rising. The jaw sets, the chest expands, blood moves to the hands; the body straightens and turns *toward* — energy gathering to push back, break through, set right. Where fear contracts, anger mobilizes.
**What It Signals.** Anger reports a crossing: a [[Boundary]] breached, a value trampled, life obstructed, someone or something we love being harmed. It is the energy of [[Justice]] arriving as feeling — the [[Living System]]'s way of saying *this must not stand*. Its presence is evidence of care: we burn only where something matters.
**Its Gradient.** One band of heat: [[Annoyance]] → [[Irritation]] → [[Frustration]] → Anger → [[Fury]]. Slow-cooked, it becomes [[Resentment]]; curdled into verdicts on beings, it turns toward [[Contempt]], [[Disgust]], [[Hate]].
**Its Gift.** Strength and clarity in the same instant: the energy to protect what is precious, and a precise pointer to which boundary or value was crossed. Read well, anger is a map of what we stand for.
**Its Distortion.** Enthroned, it becomes aggression — the fire turned on beings instead of harms. Refused, it becomes the false peace of anger swallowed: compliance on the surface, [[Resentment]] fermenting beneath. Both dam the flow; neither restores what was crossed.
**The Move.** Feel the heat without discharging it. Read it: *what precious thing was violated? What boundary needs restating?* Then act as a [[Sovereign]]: name the crossing plainly, repair or defend what needs it, and let the fire spend itself in [[Right Relationship]] rather than against a person.
