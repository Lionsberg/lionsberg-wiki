# Planet

A Planet is a world-scale body of the [[Holarchy]] — the largest living whole a species inhabits directly, and one holon among the cosmos's many.

In the nested scales of the [[The Holarchy of the ØNE|Holarchy]] — Sovereign, [[Circle]], [[Community]], [[Bioregion]], Planet, [[Cosmos]] — the Planet is the integrating body of all life it carries: one [[Living System]], one atmosphere, one water cycle, one fate shared by every being aboard. Earth is not humanity's property or platform; it is the [[Superorganism]] we are cells of, the garden entrusted to our [[Stewardship]], and — in the [[Meta Project]]'s frame — the worksite: [[Worksite Earth]], where the [[New World]] is being built within the living body of the old one.

Planetary scale is where the [[Old World]]'s fragmentation fails most visibly: no nation can steward an atmosphere, no market a biosphere. The challenges of this scale — regeneration, climate, the [[Commons]] of land and sea and sky — are the forcing function that calls humanity into [[Higher Order Functional Unity]]: coordination worthy of a planet, without the tyranny of a planetary throne ([[Holarchy]] · [[Interoperability]]).

And the Planet is not the ceiling. Worlds themselves are holons within wider bodies ([[Beyond Earth]]); a species that learns to be whole on its own world becomes, for the first time, a trustworthy neighbor among worlds.
