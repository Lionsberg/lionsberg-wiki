# Disappointment
Disappointment is the reading of the gap between what was hoped and what arrived.
**Felt Sense.** A drop. The lift of expectation suddenly unsupported — a small sag in the chest, a deflation, the "oh" that escapes before any words. Brief or lingering, it always has the shape of a fall from a height we had already climbed in imagination.
**What It Signals.** Disappointment reports that the [[Map of Reality]] ran ahead of reality: we had pre-lived a future, and the present declined to match it. It is expectation grieving in miniature — a small hoped-for world, ending.
**Its Gradient.** Disappointment → [[Sadness]] → [[Sorrow]] → [[Grief]] → [[Anguish]] → [[Despair]]. Repeated and unmetabolized, it compounds into [[Discouragement]]; aimed at a person who let us down, it shades into [[Hurt]].
**Its Gift.** Disappointment is a precision instrument for desire. The sting marks exactly what we truly wanted — often more honestly than we had admitted before the gap opened. Read well, it clarifies [[Hope]]: *this* is what I was reaching for.
**Its Distortion.** Refused, it hardens into cynicism — the pre-emptive lowering of all hopes so nothing can ever fall. Enthroned, it becomes chronic grievance: the world perpetually guilty of not being our imagination.
**The Move.** Feel the drop without rushing to shrug it off. Then ask the gift's question: *what does this sting say I wanted?* Keep the want; release the script. Hope aimed again is disappointment completed.
