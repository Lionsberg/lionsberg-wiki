# Total Nested Holarchy

The Total Nested Holarchy is the complete nested structure of any category of Reality — every instance of the category, at every scale, ordered as wholes within wholes ([[Holarchy]]), with the category's [[Meta]] level at its ultimately ineffable top and its fullest diversity of manifestation within.

The [[LIØNSBERG System]] renders several such structures explicitly, each the same architecture applied to a different aspect of the whole:

- [[Total Nested Holarchy of Goals]] — what we intend to realize, at every scale
- [[Total Nested Holarchy of Ideals]] — what perfection looks like, at every scale
- [[Total Nested Holarchy of Values]] — what matters, at every scale
- [[Total Nested Holarchy of Issues]] — where actuality falls short, at every scale
- [[Total Nested Holarchy of Needs]] — what must be met, at every scale
- [[Total Nested Holarchy of Loves]] — what the heart is given to, at every scale
- [[Total Nested Holarchy of Sovereignties]] — who wholly is, at every scale

These are not seven separate structures. They are seven facings of the one Total Nested Holarchy of the ØNE ([[The Holarchy of the ØNE]]) — the single nested architecture of existence, viewed through goals, ideals, values, issues, needs, loves, and sovereignties in turn. Each facing shares the same shape: [[ØNE]] at the top, [[All]] within, and at the center of every [[Categories|Category]] along the way, an [[Ideal]].

Every rendering of a Total Nested Holarchy — including each of the pages above — is a [[Current Best Articulation]] of a structure that is ultimately more continuous, more alive, and more unified than any rendering can show ([[Essences, Renderings, And Ineffable Referents]]).

*Note on the word: earlier articulations said "Total Nested Hierarchy." The structure was never a hierarchy of command; it was always wholes within wholes. The language was corrected to Holarchy in August 2026 — an example of [[Current Best Articulation]] improving while the [[Current Best Understanding]] it renders holds steady.*

Within every Total Nested Holarchy, membership and value are read as gradients, not boxes — every embodied instance standing somewhere on the continuous field around the [[Ideal]] at the center of its Category ([[The Living Gradient]]).
