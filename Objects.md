# Objects

[[Objects]] are the physical or metaphysical [[Things]] we perceive as separate from our [[Self]] — the discrete forms we encounter as we explore the wonders of our universe. From the moment we incarnate into form, the perception of objects is how a finite being begins to make contact with what is: this tree, this star, this idea, this other.

Within [[Ultimate Reality]], the existence of discrete objects is a product of the limited [[Perceive|perception]] of the observer — an emergent appearance arising from more fundamental underlying physics and metaphysics. In the deepest view, all objects are aspects of ØNE, and their separateness is provisional and perceptual.

And yet the perception, identification, and [[Categorization]] of objects remains necessary. It is how information is gathered, issues are identified and solved, and progress is made towards [[The Goal]]. The wisdom is to hold both truths at once: to work skillfully with objects, while remembering that the divisions between them are drawn by the [[Map of Reality|map]], not by the ultimate underlying territory.
