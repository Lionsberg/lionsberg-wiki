# In Community

[[In Community]] names the condition under which the deepest human work must be done: together, not alone.

Each individual is inherently finite — limited in what they can [[Perceive]], [[Describe]], and [[Understand]] from their single vantage. For this reason, accurate [[OmniSpection]], trustworthy [[SenseMaking]], and the continuous correction of our shared [[Map of Reality]] can only be done In Community, through [[MindWeaving]]. The same holds for the Walk itself: the Way is not walked in isolation but as fellow members of ØNE Body — in the [[Circle]], in [[Community]], in the weekly rhythm, in the mutual holding of promises and stories.

To do a thing In Community is not merely to do it near others. It is to do it *with* them — vantages woven, gifts joined, burdens shared — so that the result carries a wholeness no individual could reach, and each participant is strengthened rather than spent.
