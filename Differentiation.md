# Differentiation

[[Differentiation]] is the act of distinguishing this from that — the perceptual and cognitive move by which a finite being separates the seamless field of [[Reality]] into discernible [[Objects]], qualities, and relationships. It is the first move of map-making: before anything can be named, categorized, or understood, it must first be told apart.

Differentiation is necessary and good — without it there is no [[Perceive|perception]], no language, no action, no [[Understanding]]. But it is a tool of the map, not a property of the territory. What has been differentiated remains, in [[Ultimate Reality]], undivided. The mature mind differentiates cleanly *and* remembers the unity beneath its distinctions — dividing to understand, without forgetting that it was the mind that drew the lines.

Together with [[Categorization]], differentiation is the process through which each being's subjective [[Map of Reality]] comes into being.
