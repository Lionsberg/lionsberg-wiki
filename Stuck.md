# Stuck
Stuck is the reading of no-movement — will present, path absent; neither flowing nor resting.
**Felt Sense.** Idling pressure. Energy with nowhere to go: pacing, circling the same thought, opening the same door onto the same wall. The body wants to push and finds nothing to push against. Not tired — thwarted.
**What It Signals.** Stuck reports a mismatch between the will and the map: desire intact, gradient unreadable. The engine runs; the wheels have lost the road. Crucially, it is not the reading of low capacity ([[Overwhelm]]) or surrendered will ([[Resignation]]) — the wanting is alive, which is exactly why the stillness aches.
**Its Gradient.** [[Hesitation]] → [[Friction]] → Stuck → [[Resignation]] (if it hardens). Its restless cousin is [[Boredom]]; its way out often opens through [[Discomfort]].
**Its Gift.** Stuck usually reports that the *frame* is wrong, not the will: the next move lives outside the current map. The wall you keep hitting is the edge of a picture, not the edge of the world. Stuck is the summons to re-see — an invitation to [[Attunement]] before further effort.
**Its Distortion.** Refused, it becomes grinding — harder pushes at the same wall, mistaking force for progress. Enthroned, it becomes the comfortable lament: forever "blocked," never re-framing, because a wall can excuse what a road would demand.
**The Move.** Change altitude, change scale, or change the question. Zoom out to [[Purpose]]; shrink the scale; ask *what am I assuming?* — aloud, to a [[Circle]] if you have one. Then make the smallest true movement in any honest direction. Motion breaks the seal; the road appears under moving feet.
