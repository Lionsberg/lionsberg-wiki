# Total Nested Holarchy of Ideals

At the absolute top of the Total Nested Holarchy of Ideals is [[ØNE]] — the Ultimate [[Ideal]] that orders all other Ideals within Itself. Within It is [[All]]. In between are the categorical structures we use to [[Perceive]] and [[Navigate]] [[Reality]] — and at the center of every [[Categories|Category]], at every level, stands an Ideal.

Reality is inherently One. To function within it, we differentiate the highest-order unity into Categories, arrayed across a total nested holarchy of abstraction — and each Category is defined by the Ideal at its center, with a wide diversity of manifestation arrayed around it. The relative [[Value]] of any individuated aspect of [[Creation]] depends on its depth in the holarchy, its [[Conformance To The Ideal]] of the Categories it inhabits, and its [[Relative Potential]] — each read as a gradient, never a box ([[The Living Gradient]]).

This holarchy, processed through a [[Narrative Framework]], is essentially the [[Map]] we use to perceive and navigate the universe. It is the companion of the [[Total Nested Holarchy of Goals]]: the Holarchy of Ideals holds what perfection looks like at every scale; the Holarchy of Goals holds what we intend to realize at every scale.

Because every Ideal below [[ØNE]] is a rendering made by minds ([[Essences, Renderings, And Ineffable Referents]]), the holarchy is held as [[Current Best Understanding]]: a north star at every altitude, never a totalitarian judge at any ([[Ideal]]). How an Ideal at the center of a category becomes embodied reality is traced in [[The Creative Cascade]].
