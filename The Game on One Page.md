# The Game on One Page

*The whole of [[The Great Game of LIØNSBERG]] on a single page — print it, pin it, pass it.*

*Players: everyone who chooses · Circles of 3 to 13 · a turn each week · a round each season · playing time: as long as you love · ages: all*

---

**The Game** — All of us, together, making every place we touch a little more like Heaven. Co-Creating Heaven, right here and now, with no one involuntarily left behind.

**The Moment** — One world is dying, and another is being born — not two events, but one. We are alive inside that nexus, and the new arrives the only way living things do: seed by seed, Circle by Circle.

**The Goal** — ØNE. Creator's Intent. Heaven. *Here, now, everywhere, for everyone, forever.*

**The Compass** — Before every move, one question: ***Does this make life a little more like Heaven?*** If it does — make the move. If it does not — pause, turn, and choose a better one.

**The Five Moves**

1. **Form a Cell** — three to thirteen souls, gathered at the pace of trust; a name, a few simple agreements, a first Quest.
2. **Choose a Quest** — one tangible act of goodwill for the season ahead: real, achievable, meaningful, together.
3. **Learn and Grow** — do the work, week by week: *say what you will do; do what you said; learn from the difference.*
4. **Share Your Stories** — every Quest becomes a Story; every Story becomes a Prophesy of even greater things to come.
5. **Pass The Flame** — each Player shares the Invitation with the two or three they know are ready. New sovereign Circles form. The fruit carries the seed.

**The Turns** — Each week, a turn: gather, check in, keep the commitments, plan the next steps, take your Turn on the Board, close in gratitude. Each season, at the solstice or equinox, the round completes: OmniSpection then a new Quest, and the wheel turns again. The clock runs at two speeds: this week's turn, and the ten thousand generations to come.

**The Board** — The Commons where the whole living Game is visible: **Who We Are · How We Are Questing · Where We Are Going. Why.** Every Circle tends its own path of the Sacred Garden; the Vision stays in the middle and grows.

**The Superorganism** — A thousand sovereign Circles, one living body: a single conscious being, operating a single system, producing measurable Throughput of a single Goal — planetary and beyond. The weekly Turn is its heartbeat; the Board its senses; the Stories its blood.

**The Score** — Time, Energy, and Attention invested · Commitments kept · Value created · Stories shared · Flames passed.

**How We Win** — Together, or not at all. *None of us, until All of us.* And the victory is not waiting at the end: every commitment kept, every hungry person fed, every wound mended, every child who learns the world can be trusted — we are winning with every move.

**Begin** — Tell someone. Share [[The Invitation]]. Gather. Play.
