# The Communication Platform

*Where meaning and energy flow between the gatherings — the LIØNSBERG Server.*

---

[[The Invitation]] promises: *"Communications systems enable meaning and energy to flow, as shared sensemaking and discernment continually reveal the Way."* This page holds the present form of that promise. 

**The LIØNSBERG Server** — presently on Discord, being established this season; in time, to be replaced by our own. Discord is borrowed land: useful, familiar, immediate — and never the record of record. The Board and The Commons — plain text, in the open git record — remain the Game's memory; the Server is its living conversation. 

## The Shape (posited)

- **Arrival** — welcome, questions, and the standing **Help Button**: press it, and one of Us will find you. 
- **The Pulse** — `#turns`: every Circle's weekly Turn ([[Template For Weekly Turn Post]]), where needs meet offers in the open. 
- **The Stories** — harvests told as they happen; the finest tended into The Commons. 
- **The Commons Floor** — needs and offers, coordination between Circles, shared sensemaking. 
- **The Workshop Bench** — questions and improvements to the plans: *perhaps you could help improve them.* 
- **Circle Rooms** — optional rooms for Circles who want their own channel here. 

## The Membrane

The Server link travels through the doors — your inviter, the Substack, the orientations ([[Reaching Us]]) — by invitation, not broadcast. And **platform-optional, always**: everything essential can be done by email through Reaching Us. The Server makes the body livelier; it is never a gate.
