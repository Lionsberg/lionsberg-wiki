# Dread
Dread is fear of the certain-but-not-yet — the weight of what is coming.
**Felt Sense.** Heaviness, not flutter. The limbs slow, the stomach sinks, time thickens. Where fear spikes, dread settles — a stone carried toward an appointed hour: the results to be read, the conversation that must be had, the loss already on the calendar.
**What It Signals.** Dread reports that something hard is not merely possible but expected — the mind has run the map and found the path passing through pain. It differs from [[Fear]] in its tense: fear says *might*; dread says *will*. It often carries a second message: something that must be faced is still being postponed, and the postponement itself has begun to cost.
**Its Gradient.** High on the band: [[Worry]] → [[Anxiety]] → [[Fear]] → Dread → [[Terror]]. Its vaguer kin is [[Foreboding]], which senses an approach without naming it.
**Its Gift.** Lead time. Dread gives the soul notice — to ready what can be readied, say what must be said, and stop pretending the hour will not come.
**Its Distortion.** Enthroned, dread pre-lives the pain daily, paying the price of the hard hour every morning before it arrives. Refused, it fuels the long procrastinations that make the dreaded thing worse — and adds shame to the bill.
**The Move.** Ask first: *is this truly certain, or has fear forged the certainty?* If the hour is avoidable, act now. If it is not, prepare — then return to today, for the dreaded hour will need you rested, and today is not that hour. Walk toward it: dread shrinks with approach and grows with delay.
