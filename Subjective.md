# Subjective

[[Subjective]] names the inescapable condition of every finite perceiver: all experience, description, and understanding occurs *from a vantage* — through the particular faculties, history, and [[Mastery]] of the one perceiving. The [[Map of Reality]] that each of Us holds is subjective and limited by our ability to [[Perceive]], [[Describe]], and [[Understand]].

Subjectivity is not a flaw to be eliminated; it is a truth to be owned. The rendering that admits its vantage is more honest — and therefore more trustworthy — than the rendering that claims to come from nowhere. Faithfulness grows along two roads: deepening [[Mastery]] of the thing perceived, and weaving one's finite view together with the views of others, in community, so that what no single vantage can see becomes visible to the whole.
