# Prototype

A Prototype is a first or typical model of a solution or system, from which subsequent instances are developed and instantiated. 

In the [[LIØNSBERG System]], Prototype has a specific meaning relative to the mechanics of the [[LIØNSBERG Integrated Delivery System]]. 

In [[The Creative Cascade]], the Prototype is the fourth rung: the first integrated embodiment of an [[Archetype]], still "in the cloud" — maintained as a [[Kit of Parts]] and continuously improved by the learnings of every [[Instantiation]]. See [[The Prototype]] for the New-World-scale Prototype itself.
