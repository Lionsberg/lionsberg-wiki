## A Co-Creative Superorganism Emerges

In response to this profound crisis, opportunity, and need, some kind of **higher order functional unity** must now emerge among **Us**. Something actually capable of meeting the needs, solving the problems, and accomplishing the goals at hand across millions of local communities.

Some kind of planetary scale **Superorganism**, composed of **Us**, must be born.

And it must grow to become strong, capable, and competent enough to move mountains, create worlds, build civilizations, and regenerate local communities.

And so it is.

> ***Superorganism**: The new creation that emerges through our consciously united intention, energy, and action. A living Being we must care for, nurture, and protect as it learns, plays, and grows.*

## You Are Invited to Play

As unlikely as it may seem, it is entirely possible for ***that*** to emerge through **Us**.

It just takes some commitment. Some passion. Some purpose. Some play.

Along with some structure, rhythms, roles, and rules.

**Play** is precisely how young superorganisms learn and grow.

And so **[[The Great Game of LIØNSBERG]]** was born.

## The Goal of The Game

The purpose and goal of the game is simple: **Co-Creating [[Heaven On Earth]]**. This means reimagining, redesigning, rebuilding, and regenerating our lives, our culture, and our world. *With no one involuntarily left behind.*

## The Game Is Simple

1. Resonant individuals form sovereign **[[Circle|Circles]]**.
2. Circles choose meaningful **[[Quest|Quests]]**.
3. Every Quest becomes a **Story**.
4. Every Story becomes a **Prophesy** of even greater things to come.
5. We all work together to **[[Pass The Flame]]** to the next awakening souls.

Through every **Circle**, **Season**, **Quest**, **Story**, and **Play**, the emerging superorganism grows stronger.

In **The Commons**, a shared gameboard shows Us **Who We Are**, **Where We Are Going**, **What We Are Doing**, and **Why**.

Communications systems enable **meaning** and **energy** to flow, as shared sensemaking and discernment continually reveal the Way.

## Courage, Commitment, and Simple Next Steps

Those who feel the resonance and would like to play are invited to take three simple actions:

1. **Respond to the one who shared this with you** — let them know you are in.
2. **Subscribe to [the Substack](https://cocreatingheaven.substack.com/)** (free of course) to receive the next communications, including invitations to the orientation gatherings.
3. Of the 100 people you know best: **pass this invitation forward to the two or three in your network most resonant and ready to play** — within one week.

The minimum commitment to play is 4–5 hours a week through the current season — each season turning at the solstice or equinox — and a reciprocal weekly resource contribution of any amount.

From these simple steps **The Great Game begins**.

The elders say we have only a short season remaining to **unite and empower [[The First Three Percent]]**.
