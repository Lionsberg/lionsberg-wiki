# The Platform

**The Platform** is the digital backbone of the [[New Civilization]]: the continuously improving interface, resting on a continuously improving interoperable technology stack, through which [[The Movement]] becomes **visible to itself**.

Its work is threefold:

- **Visibility.** [[Self-Governing, Self-Sustaining, and Self-Replicating]] people, [[Cell|Cells]], and [[Quest|Quests]] made visible to one another — so that anyone can find their kin, every Cell can find its neighbors, and the whole Body can see how it is doing at any moment.
- **Provision.** Equal access, for every person who accepts [[The Invitation]], to the pathway of lifelong learning, wellbeing, and development, and to the tools, solutions, services, and support of [[The Commons]] — delivered just in time, in response to the Quest at hand.
- **Flow.** The connective tissue for [[Ecosystemic Flows]] — offers and requests, resources and stories, commitments made and kept — moving between the diverse parts of [[The Body]] without passing through any controlling center.

Its laws are the laws of everything in the New: **interoperable** (each functional system replaceable as technology transforms — data architecture and human culture are the constants); **decentralized** (no master store; the whole world downloadable and regenerable by any [[Cell]]); and **never required** — the Platform makes the Way faster and wiser, but the Game is played complete with paper, voice, and sky. If the grid goes down, the Movement does not.
