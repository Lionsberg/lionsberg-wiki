# Compassion

The value of compassion represents a sense of empathy and understanding towards others, and involves feeling a sense of connection and shared humanity with others. It involves actively caring for and supporting others, and can be demonstrated through actions such as kindness, generosity, and support.

## As a Felt Reading

**Felt Sense.** The heart turning toward — softening and mobilizing at once; another's pain landing in one's own chest *with* the impulse to help. **What It Signals.** Compassion is [[Empathy]] completed into care: the reading that another's suffering is real, that they are kin, and that something can be offered. It is the interfeeling of the [[Superorganism]] tending its own body. **Its Gradient.** [[Sympathy]] → [[Empathy]] → Compassion → fierce compassion ([[Protective]]) — deepening from feeling-for, through feeling-with, into moved-to-act. **Its Gift & Distortion.** The gift is [[Love]] made responsive — suffering met instead of passed by. Distorted, it becomes pity (which looks down), rescue that disempowers a sovereign being, or empathic flooding that drowns the helper. Compassion at full health includes the self among its objects. **The Move.** Feel with, without merging; ask what serves their [[Potential]], not our comfort; act within one's real capacity; and let the [[Circle]] carry what one heart cannot ([[Emotion]]).
