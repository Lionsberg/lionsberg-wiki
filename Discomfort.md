# Discomfort
Discomfort is the general reading of misfit — something, somewhere, does not sit right.
**Felt Sense.** A low unease without an address: shifting in the chair, a faint wrongness in the stomach or the schedule or the sentence just spoken. The body registering friction before the mind can say between what and what. Vague — and vague on purpose.
**What It Signals.** Discomfort reports misfit between the being and its situation — body, arrangement, or conscience — before the misfit has a name. It is the vaguest of all readings, and therefore the most valuable early one: the tremor that, read now, never has to become the earthquake of [[Stress]], [[Overwhelm]], or betrayal of self. It asks only: *look closer — where?*
**Its Gradient.** [[Ease]] ← Discomfort → [[Unease]] → [[Distress]] → [[Pain]]. It stands across from [[Boredom]] and upstream of [[Motivation]] — misfit is often the first pull toward better fit.
**Its Gift.** The growth-edge marker. Most development lives just outside comfort: the word not yet spoken, the skill not yet held, the conversation not yet had. Discomfort draws the map of where growing is currently possible — [[Courage]] is the willingness to walk it.
**Its Distortion.** Treated *always* as an error to eliminate — every friction medicated, optimized, or avoided — rather than *sometimes* an edge to walk toward. A life engineered for zero discomfort is a life sealed against its own becoming — and a whole economy sells exactly that seal as the good life.
**The Move.** Pause and localize: body, situation, or conscience? Then discern — a misfit to *fix*, or an edge to *walk*? [[Attunement]] tells them apart. Fix the first promptly; walk the second gladly.
