# Activity

An **Activity** is one bounded unit of [[Work]] in a [[Network of Commitments]]: a piece of doing that an Actor commits to and keeps, and that moves [[Throughput]] toward [[The Goal]]. It is the smallest committed step in the [[LIØNSBERG Integrated Delivery System]].

An Activity is a [[Commitments|Commitment]] before it is a task: it is completed only when someone makes and keeps a commitment to do it ([[Making And Securing Commitments]]). If it helps Us All realize The Goal, it produces [[Value]]; if it serves nothing above it, it is [[Waste]].

## What every Activity carries

- **Its card.** Every Activity has one [[Work Card]], which is how it is seen, sorted and kept.
- **Its links.** At least one [[Predecessor]] and at least one [[Successor]]. An Activity with no links is not yet part of the plan.
- **Its size.** A duration of no more than **five days of ten-hour work: fifty hours.** Anything larger is broken into several linked Activities. Small Activities keep the network honest: progress shows, handoffs are visible, and a slip is caught in days rather than weeks.
- **Its owner.** The person or [[Cell]] whose word it is, and who hands it on ready and acceptable ([[Handoff Points]]).
- **What it serves.** A [[Milestones|Milestone]], a [[Quest]], or a larger Activity above it.
- **Its status.** Where it stands, and once known, when it actually started and finished.

## Where Activities come from

The [[Work Breakdown Structure]] breaks the work down from [[The 12 Pillars of LIØNSBERG]] to the Activities that do it. [[Pull Planning]] orders them backward from the Milestones, and the linked whole gives the [[Critical Path]]. The Activities of one branch of the breakdown, carried in order, make a [[Work Stream]]. A Quest runs no longer than ninety days; an Activity is its smallest committed step. A Milestone is an Activity of zero duration: a marker, not a stretch of work.
