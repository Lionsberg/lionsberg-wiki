# Story Template

*Copy this file to tell a harvested Quest's Story: `The Commons/Stories/<Story Title>.md`.*

A Story is free-form — told in your Circle's own voice. What was committed. What actually happened. What it cost, and what it gave. What was learned. Not marketing: witness. *Every Quest becomes a Story; every Story becomes a Prophesy of even greater things to come.*

```
# <Story Title>
circle:: [[<Circle>]]
quest:: [[<Quest>]]
season:: <e.g. 2026 · Season 1>

<the Story itself>

**The flame passes to:** <who this Story is being passed to>
```
