# Quest Card Template

*Copy this file to create a Quest card: `The Commons/Quests/<Quest Name>.md`. One card per Quest, tended weekly by its Circle.*

```
# <Quest Name>
circle:: [[<Circle>]]
season:: <e.g. 2026 · Season 1>
commitment:: <the one-sentence tangible act of goodwill — what will be
             measurably more like Heaven, where, by the next turning>
state:: chosen | in play | harvested
turns:: <the weekly Turn — did / will / learned (+ needs / offers), one line per week>
story:: [[<Story>]] (when harvested)
```

*Taking the Turn each week is the heartbeat of the Superorganism — a few minutes of tending that keeps [[The Gameboard]] alive and honest.*
