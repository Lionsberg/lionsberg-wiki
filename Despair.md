# Despair
Despair is the far-band reading that no path remains — pain reporting truly, possibility reported falsely.
**Felt Sense.** The bottom. Weight without waves, dark without doors. The body wants the floor; the eyes stop scanning the horizon, because the horizon has closed. Even wanting feels pointless, which is despair's signature: the reaching function itself goes dark.
**What It Signals.** Despair reports two things at once, and only one is reliable. About the pain, it is honest: the load has exceeded what this being can currently carry alone. About the world, it overreaches: *no path exists* is a claim about all futures, made by an instrument built to read the present. Despair is real weather and a false forecast.
**Its Gradient.** [[Disappointment]] → [[Sadness]] → [[Sorrow]] → [[Grief]] → [[Anguish]] → Despair. Settled into prediction it becomes [[Hopelessness]]; sustained and saturated, [[Misery]].
**Its Gift.** Despair strips the situation to bedrock. It ends pretending, ends performing, ends the exhausted strategies — and in that terrible clearing, it makes the being maximally honest about needing help. That honesty is the door it cannot see.
**Its Distortion.** Despair's danger is not excess but isolation: alone, its false forecast goes unchallenged and begins to act on the world. Enthroned, it poses as the only clear-eyed view — despair loves to call itself realism.
**The Move.** Not insight — company. The first move is refusing to be alone with it: one call, one [[Circle]], one person in the room. Paths reappear in [[Connection]] before they reappear in thought. If the dark includes thoughts of ending, tell someone now; that is [[Sovereignty]], not weakness.
