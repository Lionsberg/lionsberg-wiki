# Holon

A Holon is something that is simultaneously a whole and a part.

A cell is a whole in its own right — and a part of an organ, which is a whole and a part of a body. An [[Individuals|Individual]] is whole — and part of a [[Group]], which is whole and part of a [[Community]], which is whole and part of the [[Living System]] of Earth, which is whole and part of [[All]] [[Creation]] within [[ØNE]].

Everything real is like this. Wholeness and parthood are not opposites to choose between; they are the two faces of every living thing. A holon that forgets it is a part becomes a cancer; a holon that forgets it is a whole becomes a slave. Health, at every scale, is both at once: full sovereign integrity *and* full participation in the larger wholes.

This is why the [[LIØNSBERG System]] is holofractal — the same pattern repeating at every scale ([[Holofractal Omnifederation]]), from the Individual to the whole. Each [[Instantiation]] of [[The Prototype]] is sovereign, a whole in its own right, and simultaneously a part of the learning, co-creating body of [[All]]. Each Part in the [[Kit of Parts]] is a whole capability and a part of the greater whole. The pattern connects; it never commands.
