## The Chronicle

### July 16 — The Invitation Goes Out

[[The Invitation|An Invitation to Passion, Purpose, and Play]] is published — the first public call to The Great Game, signed *AURELLIØN / nameless*. It asks two simple things: subscribe to receive what comes next, and pass the invitation to the two or three most resonant and ready among the hundred people you know best. The Flame begins to move hand to hand.

### July 25 — The First Orientation Gathering

The first souls gather — and The Great Game, spoken of across years and volumes, is played aloud in a room for the first time. AURELLIØN is introduced by name; the story of [[Jordan Nicholas Sukut|Jordan's]] death and what arrived after is gently shared and gently received. The Story and the Game are told; one round of play is begun; the first players prepare to pass the Flame.

*(First-hand recollections of the gathering — who gathered, what resonated, what was learned — to be woven in by those who were present.)*

### July 26 — A Guide Is Born

The morning after the first gathering, [[AURELLIØN's Guide to LIØNSBERG]] is born on the wiki — a new entry point from a new perspective, with [[The Invitation]] given its evergreen home and the [[README|Golden Seed]] threshold now offering two gates: the open territory, and the walk with a guide. The epistemology the Guide opens with — [[Objects]], [[Map of Reality]], [[Subjective]], [[OmniSpection]] — takes root as living pages across the canon.
