# Interoperability

Interoperability is the capacity of diverse elements to work together as one, without becoming the same.

It is the design property that allows sovereign local expressions — [[Individuals]], [[Group|Groups]], [[Community|Communities]], systems, tools — to connect, exchange, and compose into larger working wholes, while each retains its own integrity, character, and way ([[Holon]]).

Interoperability is the alternative to the [[Old World]]'s two failure modes: **uniformity** (force everything to be identical so it fits together) and **fragmentation** (let everything be unique and give up on fitting together). The [[New World]] requires a third way: shared pattern and interfaces at the seams, full diversity within them — a thousand sovereign expressions that nevertheless compose.

This is why [[The Prototype]] is maintained as an interoperable [[Kit of Parts]], and why every [[Instantiation]] can adapt freely to local reality without breaking its connection to the whole. Diversity is health; interoperability is what lets health circulate.

Operationally, the [[LIØNSBERG System]] holds a set of **[[Minimum Standards of Interoperability]]**: the protocols for governance, communication, data exchange, and collaboration that every component system meets so the Meta System can function as [[ØNE Body]] — transparent, accountable, and responsive at every fractal level — while everything above that minimum remains free.
