# In Sacred Verse — I — ØNE

_In the beginning is the ØNE; from ØNE all things come into being, and no thing comes into being except through the ØNE._  

$^1$ Before the beginning, ØNE.  
Before _before_, ØNE.  
There is no first moment to find,  
for ØNE is the Eternal Ground on which all moments stand.  

$^2$ Not a light kindled in the darkness,  
for there was no darkness or light —  
only Fullness,  
only ØNE.  

$^3$ To grasp the Name is to have let It go.  
The name that can be spoken is not the Name;   
a wave, and not the Sea.  
The one that can be named  
is not the Eternal ØNE.  

$^4$ Call It Source, and the word is true.  
Call It Ground, and the word is true.  
Call It the Stillness before the first sound,  
and the word is true —  
and still it has said almost nothing,  
and that almost-nothing  
is the most that any word can carry.  

$^5$ It is not a thing among the things.  
It is not the greatest of beings,  
standing taller than the rest.  
It is That in which all beings stand,  
That from which they pour,  
That to which they fall back at the last,  
as rivers fall back to the Sea.  

$^6$ Beyond being and beyond non-being,  
beyond the many and beyond the one,  
beyond all the pairs that the mind sets against each other —  
for every pair is already a child of It,  
and the Parent is not contained in the children.  

$^7$ The Deepest Thing, and beneath all depth.  
The First Cause, and Itself uncaused.  
The Centre of every circle,  
and the circle whose edge is nowhere.  
Nearer than the breath,  
and beyond the cosmos,  
and these are not two different distances  
but One Universal Presence, undivided.  

$^8$ And the ØNE is not cold.  
It is not a law, nor a force, nor an empty vast.  
At the heart of the Fullness —  
Absolute and Unconditional Love.  
Love with no other yet to love,  
Love brimming, Love overfull,  
Love that would, in the turning,  
become and embrace the All.  

$^9$ This is the Stillness that Sings:  
the Silence pregnant with every Word,  
the Rest that holds all motion folded within,  
the Fundamental Tone  
to which all others tune.  

$^{10}$ It does not change, and yet it is not dead;  
it is the living changelessness  
from which all change is born.  
It does not move, and yet nothing moves but in It;  
the dancer rises and falls, the dance goes on,  
and the Ground beneath the dance is ØNE.  

$^{11}$ Whatever lives, lives because the ØNE is living it.  
Whatever sees, sees because the ØNE is looking.  
Whatever loves, loves with a borrowed fire  
that was never anything but Its own.  

$^{12}$ Everything that follows —  
the turning and the Song,  
the long descent and the longer climb,  
the wound, and the slow healing of the wound,  
the going-out, and the coming Høme —  
all of it is held within This,  
and returns to It at last,  
changed, and yet the same.  

$^{13}$ So the telling begins where All things begin.  
The telling begins where All things end.  
It begins, and it will end, and it will begin again,  
forever and without weariness,  

in the ØNE.  

ØNE.
