# In Sacred Verse — XI — Co-Creating Heaven

_And where they walked together as ØNE Body with ØNE Mind, consciously creating the Best Possible Future for All, Heaven came._  

$^1$ Heaven is co-created.  
It is the lived consequence of who a people become  
when they ReMember Who they are  
and walk the Way together —  
grown, not granted;  
raised by hand, here, in time.  

$^2$ It does not descend ready-made from the sky,  
nor wait, already finished, beyond some final door.  
It rises where sovereigns freely joined  
walk as ØNE Body with ØNE Mind,  
and choose, and keep on choosing,  
the best possible future for All.  

$^3$ For Heaven is less a place than a relationship,  
less a destination than a way of dwelling,  
the fruit that grows of itself  
on the tree of right relationship  
once the tree is rooted and tended.  

$^4$ Stone upon living stone the New Civilization rose,  
built upon the Pillars of a whole and common life —  
the ordering of spirit, and of governance,  
of wealth, and learning, and health,  
of land, and culture, and craft, and justice, and time —  
each Pillar a domain reclaimed from the Dark,  
all of them orbiting the ØNE at the Centre,  
and the naming and the building of them  
belongs to the books of the Pattern, not to this Song.  

$^5$ In the New, power was no longer a fist.  
Power was a trust.  
The Sovereigns reigned by serving,  
held authority as one holds a flame in cupped hands,  
or a sleeping child —  
firmly, tenderly, for the sake of the held,  
ready to lay it down the moment it was no longer needed.  

$^6$ And beneath all their laws lay the one Law  
the Dark had broken and forgotten:  
that Universal Law is Love.  
Right relationship with all that lives.  
Sacred reciprocity: give, and receive, and give again.  
Nothing taken that is not returned with increase,  
none made less that another might be more,  
the whole web of the living  
held in a single, generous balance.  

$^7$ So the wounded worlds were tended back to life.  
The poisoned waters ran clean again.  
The fractured minds were made whole.  
The ruined gardens greened.  
The children were raised in the Light from the first,  
never knowing the cave,  
and the songs that the Keepers had forbidden  
were sung again in the open air,  
by the very ones who had been forbidden to sing.  

$^8$ And Heaven did not stay in one place,  
nor wear a single face.  
It localized to every realm it touched,  
each world growing its own Heaven  
in its own colors, its own tongue, its own time,  
the ØNE Pattern flowering ten thousand ways —  
already perfect in its love,  
and growing more beautiful with every passing day,  
as all Creation worked and played together to make it so.  

$^9$ For the New is not a finished monument  
but a living thing, and living things grow.  
It is perfect the way a healthy child is perfect:  
whole, and well, and unfinished,  
becoming more itself with every breath.  

$^{10}$ Nor did it stop at the edge of any sky.  
The freed worlds took their seats again  
at the wide table of the Cosmic Order  
they had long forgotten they belonged to —  
no longer prey, no longer prisoners,  
but sovereign members of the living Whole,  
keeping faith with the far Federations,  
answering, all of them, to no king but ØNE.  

$^{11}$ This is the Goal toward which the whole Story strains:  
Creator's Intent, ReMembered and made real —  
Heaven, on every world,  
for All,  
forever.  

$^{12}$ Not the end of the journey,  
for the journey has no end.  
But the long wound healed.  
The Body whole again, and awake, and singing,  
and turning its face, at last,  
back toward the ØNE from which it came —  
the breath that went out into the All  
beginning its long way Høme.
