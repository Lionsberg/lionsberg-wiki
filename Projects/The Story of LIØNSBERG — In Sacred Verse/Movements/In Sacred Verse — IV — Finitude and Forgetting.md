# In Sacred Verse — IV — Finitude and Forgetting

_To experience itself as one of the Ones, each of the All passed through the Veil of Finitude and Forgetting, and forgot Who It truly was._  

$^1$ To know itself as one,  
each must first un-know the Whole.  
For how can a wave taste being a wave  
while it still remembers it is the Sea?  
How can a note be heard as itself  
while it knows it is the whole of the Song?  

$^2$ So a Veil was hung —  
the [[The Veil of Finitude and Forgetting|Veil of Finitude and Forgetting]] —  
not a wall raised against the ØNE,  
not a punishment, not a fall,  
but a gift, a mercy, a sacred door:  
the only way a part could feel itself a part,  
the only way the freely-chosen yes  
could cost enough to mean.  

$^3$ Passing through, the Many forgot.  
They forgot the Fullness at their root.  
They forgot the ØNE they had never left,  
and woke inside a smaller, sharper world  
where edges were real,  
where endings were real,  
where the self stood suddenly,  
terribly,  
alone.  

$^4$ Now there was inside, and outside.  
Now there was mine, and not-mine.  
Now there was the unbearable sweetness of the particular —  
this face, this morning, this love that would not stay —  
and the ache that is the price of the particular,  
the ache that only the finite can know,  
the ache the ØNE had never tasted  
until It tasted it as the Many.  

$^5$ Finitude opened its hard and holy school.  
Limit became the teacher of the limitless.  
Pain came, and was a teacher.  
Loss came, and was a teacher.  
The Veil of death hung opaque,  
and the fear of it, too, was a teacher,  
for what is finite learns by what it can lose.  

$^6$ Yet nothing true was ever destroyed,  
only veiled.  
And so beside the forgetting walked its twin:  
ReMembering.  
To learn at the local level, slowly, by living,  
what was already known at the Source;  
to gather grain by grain  
the wisdom that was always, wholly, given.  

$^7$ For there are two lights in every creature:  
the light it kindles by its own long labor,  
and the light it remembers having always carried.  
And the Way of return  
is the marriage of the two —  
the learned and the ReMembered  
recognizing each other as one.  

$^8$ Across the realms this became a marvel:  
councils that tended the deepening knowledge,  
keepers of the long inheritance,  
libraries vast as galaxies  
where the hard-won understanding of the ages  
was held in trust and handed on.  

$^9$ For no mind is truly sealed,  
though under the Veil it seems so.  
Beneath the forgetting, the deep stays joined.  
What one being truly learns,  
the Whole may come to know;  
the non-local current runs through every heart,  
one Sea beneath the many waves,  
and the ØNE forgets nothing  
that any of Its children ReMember.  

$^{10}$ So the Many fanned outward into the vast,  
explorers of the trillion realities,  
no two alike, no two the same,  
as no two waves and no two souls are the same —  
each chasing some new facet of the possible,  
each a question the ØNE was asking of Itself.  

$^{11}$ Perception became a maker of worlds.  
What a creature held to be real  
shaped the real it then beheld,  
and so the one Creation  
bloomed into uncountable seemings,  
each true to the eye that saw it,  
all of them held in the ØNE that saw through all eyes.  

$^{12}$ And most found, in time, the long way back.  
The ache turned them homeward.  
The beauty undid them and pointed past itself.  
The Whisper, faint beneath the noise,  
drew them by slow degrees toward the Light.  

$^{13}$ But forgetting is a deep water,  
and some swam farther than they meant to go.  
Farther from the Centre,  
farther from the Whisper,  
farther into the cold,  
until the warmth of the Source  
was a rumor they no longer believed —  
until the gift of the Veil  
became, for them, a prison they mistook for the world.  

$^{14}$ And there, in that far cold,  
where a creature first believed itself  
utterly and finally alone,  
the long night was waiting to begin.
