# In Sacred Verse — VIII — Liberation and Exodus

_Domain by domain, realm by realm, the captives were freed, and began Walking One Another Høme._  

$^1$ Domain by domain, the chains were struck.  
Realm by realm, the cave-mouths opened.  
Not all at once, and never easily —  
each liberation its own long labor,  
each world a battle, and a long tending after,  
for the breaking of a chain is one work,  
and the healing of the one who wore it, another.  

$^2$ For the freed did not step out into glory.  
They came out blinking, broken, scarcely alive,  
minds fractured by the long captivity,  
tongues still shaping the Keeper's words,  
flinching from the very Light that came to save them.  
At first, freedom itself felt like falling,  
and many wept for the cave they had been carried from.  

$^3$ For the cave can be carried out of the body  
long after the body is carried out of the cave.  
A people can be freed in a single hour  
and yet take generations to be free.  
The chain on the wrist is struck with a blow;  
the chain on the mind is loosed only by ReMembering.  

$^4$ So the liberated learned the long Way of the crossing,  
the pattern walked by every world that rises,  
the road worn smooth by countless feet before.  

$^5$ They woke —  
and let the waking be real,  
though the seeing sickened them,  
though it would have been easier to sleep.  

$^6$ They left the caves —  
though the cave was the only home they had known,  
though the Keepers called them back by name,  
though the dark behind was familiar  
and the road ahead was not.  

$^7$ They confessed that they did not know —  
and this was the key that fit the lock,  
not certainty, but the humble, freeing confession  
that nearly all they had been taught was a lie,  
and the True Story had not yet been told.  
The proud could not cross, for their hands were full;  
only the empty hands could take hold of the new.  

$^8$ They crossed the Boundary into the UnKnown —  
past the edge of the world they had been given,  
out where the old map ended  
and the real one was not yet drawn,  
trusting the Whisper farther than the eye could see.  

$^9$ They walked the Wilderness —  
and did not curse it, for it was not punishment.  
The Wilderness is the forge.  
It strips away what the cave had fastened on.  
It teaches the weak legs to carry.  
None pass through the Wilderness unchanged;  
the Wilderness passes through them,  
and they come out the far side  
as the kind of beings the Promised Land requires.  

$^{10}$ For the Promised Land is not a place  
that waits, already finished, for arrival.  
It is the country only the transformed can enter,  
because it is the country they themselves become.  
The crossing does not lead to the new world;  
the crossing is the making of the ones who are the new world.  

$^{11}$ And no one walked it alone.  
This is the Sacred Chain:  
each one reaching back for the one behind,  
each one steadied by the one ahead,  
the awakened bearing the still-waking,  
the strong of one hour carried by the strong of the next,  
none flung away, none written off,  
no one left behind who could be reached —  
Walking One Another Høme.  

$^{12}$ And this is no tale of strangers and far-off worlds.  
It is the crossing of every world that wakes,  
and of every soul within them,  
and of whoever now turns these pages  
in the hour of their own waking.  

$^{13}$ The world that seems the whole of the world  
is a cave with a wide and sunlit mouth.  
The ache no comfort can cure  
is the Whisper, calling.  
The wrongness felt since childhood  
was never madness; it was sight.  

$^{14}$ No hand reaches for this telling by accident.  
The one who finds it  
was reaching for the Chain  
long before the Chain had a name —  
and the Chain, all the while,  
was reaching back.  

$^{15}$ So the freed took the hand ahead  
and offered the hand behind,  
and walked.  
The Wilderness is wide,  
but it has been crossed before  
by countless ones, on countless worlds,  
and they call out from the far shore,  
where the New is already being built,  
_come; it can be done; we are waiting._
