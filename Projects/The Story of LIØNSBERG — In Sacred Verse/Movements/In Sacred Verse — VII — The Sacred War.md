# In Sacred Verse — VII — The Sacred War

_The awakening and remembering became a Sacred Force; Love grew and organized as an immune system within ØNE Body._  

$^1$ One by one is how it begins.  
But one by one is not how it ends.  
The awakened found one another across the dark,  
recognized the same fire in distant eyes,  
and the Whisper in each  
became a Word between them,  
and the Word between them became a Flame.  

$^2$ The elders of the Light convened a Great Assembly,  
and did not rush to arms.  
First they grew still.  
First they sought the Wisdom and the Guidance of the ØNE,  
that the answer might be the ØNE's,  
and not the proud invention of the wise.  

$^3$ And in the long stillness it became clear,  
clear and heavy as a stone laid on the chest:  
the Dark would not relent of itself.  
The parasite does not loosen its grip for pity.  
The empire does not free the captive out of shame.  
What feeds on suffering  
will not be reasoned out of its hunger.  

$^4$ And it became clear, too,  
that to stand idle was no innocence.  
To leave the weak to be devoured  
and call it peace  
was not Love, but the betrayal of Love.  
Mercy that abandons the bound  
is only comfort wearing mercy's robe.  

$^5$ So Love did the thing the gentle fear to name:  
Love took up arms.  
Not to dominate — the Dark already owned that art.  
Not to repay cruelty in kind,  
for cruelty answered with cruelty  
only crowns the cruel.  
Love took up arms to set the captive free.  

$^6$ This is the first law of the Sacred War,  
and woe to the warrior who forgets it:  
the aim is to end the enslaving,  
not to destroy the enslaver —  
for the captor, too, is a captive,  
bound by the very fear it wields.  
The victory is the prisoner walking free,  
and, wherever it can be won,  
the captor itself reclaimed,  
the lost one welcomed Høme.  

$^7$ For Anti-Good is a sickness in the Body,  
and the answer to a sickness is not more sickness.  
A wound is not healed by a deeper wound.  
The healthy flesh rises around the hurt,  
surrounds it, stops its spread,  
and knits the torn Body back to life —  
and this is the immune response of the Whole.  

$^8$ So the long-awaited turning hardened into form.  
Love became as organized as domination had ever been.  
The good, so long scattered, outmatched, and afraid,  
learned at last to move as ØNE —  
patient, disciplined, coordinated, relentless,  
fierce as any empire that ever marched,  
and bound by a wholly other law.  

$^9$ This is the hardest lesson of the Light:  
that gentleness without strength is swept aside,  
and strength without gentleness becomes the very Dark it fought;  
and only when the two are wed —  
the lion and the lamb in a single heart —  
can a power arise that frees without enslaving.  

$^{10}$ The Force for Good went out across the realms.  
And the Universal War to Restore the True Kingdom  
was joined on a thousand worlds at once —  
not one war, but the ØNE war,  
wearing ten thousand faces,  
fought in the open field and the secret heart alike,  
on the battlefield, in the council, at the cradle.  

$^{11}$ And because such a work  
could not be carried by courage alone,  
nor won by scattered acts of scattered good,  
there arose the need for a Way —  
a System equal to the scale of the task,  
by which a whole holofractal Body  
could think, and move, and build, and heal as ØNE.  

$^{12}$ And in this very hour,  
on a sacred mountain in a country far away,  
a Light was kindled.  
There dwelt an ancient people, gentle and fierce at once,  
winged as the angels, maned as the lions,  
who had ReMembered early and never wholly forgotten,  
and had kept the ØNE at the Centre of all.  
Their Kingdom knew no king but ØNE;  
every citizen was sovereign and free;  
the law was Love, and the strong served the small.  

$^{13}$ It lifted up its lamp.  
It sounded a Call across the dark,  
a single clear note beneath the noise of empires —  
and to that Call  
the awakening worlds began to turn.  

$^{14}$ What that Kingdom was,  
and the Way it carried, and the Game it became,  
the Song will tell in the Movements to come,  
when the captives have crossed,  
and ReMembered,  
and come at last to take up the Way.
