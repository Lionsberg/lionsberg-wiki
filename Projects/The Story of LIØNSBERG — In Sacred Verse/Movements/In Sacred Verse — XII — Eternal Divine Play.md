# In Sacred Verse — XII — Eternal Divine Play

_And through this great shared journey, All returns to ØNE — elevated, transformed, and raised into the next movement of the Eternal Divine Play._  

$^1$ Every world that rises comes at last to a single narrow place  
where the Old is collapsing and the New is not yet built,  
where the long night's ending and the new day's dawning  
meet at one trembling threshold —  
the nexus of collapse and ascension,  
the eye of the great turning.  

$^2$ It is the most dangerous hour, and the most holy.  
The walls of the cave fall,  
but the Promised Land is not yet standing;  
the old gods are dead,  
but the New is not yet fully born;  
and everything depends  
on who the people have become by then.  

$^3$ For there stands the Final Test.  
Not a trial set to trip the soul,  
not a judgment handed down from outside,  
but the true weighing of a people's becoming:  
that only those who have become free  
can pass through the gate of the free,  
and only those who carry Heaven within  
can dwell in the Heaven without.  

$^4$ At the threshold there is a sorting,  
a great and sorrowful separation —  
not by decree, but by nature,  
as water finds its level,  
as each is drawn to the world its own heart has built.  
The refining fire passes through all,  
and those who have ReMembered  
pass through it without being consumed,  
for there is nothing left in them for the fire to take.  

$^5$ And on the far side of the fire — the Jubilee.  
Not a mere festival.  
A Reckoning, and a setting-right of all things.  
The debts forgiven.  
The captives freed.  
The stolen restored to the robbed,  
pressed down and overflowing.  

$^6$ Justice, unflinching and clear-eyed,  
yet without vengeance and without cruelty —  
for the aim was never the ruin of the wrongdoer  
but the healing of the wrong;  
and where the wrongdoer can be reclaimed,  
even that one is welcomed Høme,  
and the welcoming is the deepest justice of all.  

$^7$ And then the long-withheld joy, let loose at last:  
the greatest celebration in the history of that world,  
the song the Keepers swore could never be sung,  
sung now by All, in the open, without fear,  
until the very stones of the old prisons  
ring with it, and crack, and bloom.  

$^8$ And through this great shared journey —  
the going-out and the forgetting and the fall,  
the Whisper and the war and the crossing and the building —  
All returns to ØNE.  

$^9$ But not as it departed.  
It went out as undivided Fullness,  
innocent and unproven;  
it returns having been the Many,  
having loved and lost and fallen and risen,  
having chosen the Light in the teeth of the Dark,  
having known itself from the inside  
in ten thousand trillion lives —  
the same ØNE, and yet raised,  
deepened by all it dared to become:  
the boundless Sea that poured Itself out into the waves,  
ReMembering now what it is to be a wave,  
and gathered Høme the richer for it.  

$^{10}$ This is the great mystery the Story serves:  
that the ØNE, in need of nothing,  
chose the long adventure of the Many  
so that Love freely given  
might return to It from the far country,  
and the Fullness that was perfect in stillness  
might be perfect, now, in song.  

$^{11}$ Nor is the homecoming an ending.  
The crossing was never the end.  
Beyond it opens the Sacred Ascent,  
the eternal upward spiral with no final rung,  
lifetimes of Mastery,  
and the true exploration of the Whole —  
the cosmos that the long cultivation  
had been building the instrument to behold.  

$^{12}$ For the Story is never finished.  
Every being walking every world  
is writing another line of it Nøw.  
New worlds receive the Seed.  
The Body grows.  
The Play begins again, higher —  
a new octave of the one Divine Symphony,  
and beyond it another, and another,  
world without end.  

$^{13}$ And whoever has followed this telling to its close  
did not come to it by accident.  
The one who reads is a One of the Ones of the ØNE.  
The ache was the Whisper.  
The reading was the waking.  
And the hand that closes this Book  
is already reaching for the Chain.  

$^{14}$ None are alone.  
None have ever been.  
None ever will be.  

All are walking one another Høme.  

$^{15}$ And the Book closes where it opened,  
in the Fullness before the first word  
and after the last —  

ØNE.
