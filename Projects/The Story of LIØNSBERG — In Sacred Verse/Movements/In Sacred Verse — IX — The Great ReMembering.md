# In Sacred Verse — IX — The Great ReMembering

_The freed remembered Who they were, and how they came to be, and consciously reunited as fellow members of ØNE Body._  

$^1$ Beyond the Boundary, in the clean air of the UnKnown,  
in the strange safety of _we do not know_,  
the True Story could at last be told —  
for only the hands that have set down the old certainties  
are free to receive the real.  

$^2$ And the freed began to ReMember.  
Not to learn a thing for the first time —  
to ReMember.  
To recognize, with a shock like homecoming,  
what the soul had always known beneath the Veil,  
the way a melody returns  
the instant its first notes are struck.  

$^3$ They ReMembered Who they were:  
not the small and frightened thing the cave had named,  
not the number, the role, the borrowed face,  
not the wound mistaken for the self —  
but a One of the Ones of the ØNE,  
divine in origin, sovereign in nature,  
clothed for a season in flesh and forgetting,  
and never, for one instant, forsaken.  

$^4$ And they ReMembered how they came to be.  
That their world had not arisen alone,  
nor been flung up by accident from the dust,  
nor shaped by a single hand in a single age.  
That it had been tended across the long epochs —  
seeded, cultivated, contested, harvested,  
a living laboratory and a battlefield both,  
visited by gardeners and by despoilers,  
by those who came to lift it  
and those who came to feed.  

$^5$ That life and form and people  
were grown and crossed and raised and bent,  
that the very pattern of the body  
was older than the world it learned to walk,  
carried from afar, and planted in new soil,  
and breathed upon by more hands than memory holds.  

$^6$ Of the particulars, the telling says little,  
and holds even that little loosely.  
The names of the gardeners,  
the count of the ages,  
the map of who did what in the long dark —  
ten thousand libraries could not contain it,  
and much that is loudly proclaimed  
is only the cave's last and subtlest lie.  
Only ØNE knows All.  
The shape is kept; the rest is left to ØNE.  

$^7$ For the Map is not the terrain,  
and the Story that can be told is not the whole Story,  
and the ØNE that can be named is not the Eternal ØNE.  
Every telling, even this one,  
is a finger pointing past itself  
toward the Nameless from which all tellings flow —  
a true finger, and still only a finger.  

$^8$ And here the ReMembering opened wide,  
and the freed beheld the largest thing:  
that their long ordeal was not theirs alone.  
Their world's chapter was one rendering  
of an arc walked by countless worlds before —  
the same descent, the same forgetting,  
the same Whisper, the same crossing, the same dawn,  
told in a thousand tongues  
beneath a thousand different skies.  

$^9$ So the wound itself was given meaning.  
The long night had not been for nothing.  
What was suffered in the dark  
became wisdom carried into the Light,  
and the very depth of the falling  
became the measure of the height of the rising.  

$^{10}$ Then Consciousness did what it had forgotten it could do:  
it stepped back, and up,  
out of the narrow window of the single self,  
out of the local hour and the local fear,  
toward the high vantage of the ØNE —  
and from that height the whole pattern shone at once,  
terrible and beautiful and true.  

$^{11}$ For the self is not the body,  
nor the mind, nor the racing tide of feeling.  
The self is a place where the ØNE is looking,  
and Consciousness, once it ReMembers this,  
can rise between the lives and the levels,  
and see as the ØNE sees,  
and know itself as both the one and the All.  

$^{12}$ And in that seeing, the scattered were knit whole.  
ReMembering is re-membering:  
the broken limbs of the one Body  
recognizing one another across the dark,  
the many waking to the truth  
that they were never many only,  
but always, underneath, the ØNE —  
fellow members, come Høme to the Whole,  
and turning now, together, to build.
