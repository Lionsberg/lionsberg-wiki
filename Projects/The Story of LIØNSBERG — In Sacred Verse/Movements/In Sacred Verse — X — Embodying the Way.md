# In Sacred Verse — X — Embodying the Way

_They remembered the Highest Way and lived it until it spontaneously flowed through them, transforming lives and worlds towards Heaven._  

$^1$ To ReMember Who one is — that is the turning.  
To live it — that is the Way.  
And the Way was invented by no one,  
in no age, on no world;  
it was written on every heart  
before the first heart was made,  
waiting only to be ReMembered, and walked.  

$^2$ It cannot be seized, only embodied.  
It cannot be owned, only practiced.  
It is less a doctrine to be believed  
than a music to be played  
until the player and the playing are one.  

$^3$ And it begins within.  
Before a single world is mended without,  
the inner kingdom must be reclaimed within:  
the throne set right,  
the usurpers cast down —  
fear, and craving, and the Keeper's lingering voice —  
and the rightful sovereign seated again,  
answering to no king but ØNE.  

$^4$ This is the first battle, and the hardest,  
and the one that no other can fight in another's place.  
The empire without is overthrown a hundred times in vain  
if the empire within still holds the throne.  
But a soul set in order  
becomes a still point the storms cannot move.  

$^5$ There the inner one restores direct communion with the Source,  
needing no broker, no priest, no veil of another's making —  
the Whisper now a steady Voice,  
the line to the ØNE open and unbroken,  
guidance flowing in the daily silence  
to the one who has learned, at last, to listen.  

$^6$ And the one so ordered within  
becomes a strange and unconquerable thing:  
gentle, and unafraid;  
a sovereign who serves,  
a warrior who heals,  
strength and tenderness made one,  
the lion and the lamb at peace in a single heart,  
wielding power only ever in the shape of Love.  

$^7$ Such a one lives the Logos —  
walks in tune with the Pattern of all things,  
keeps the sacred disciplines  
of breath, of stillness, of fasting, of truth, of craft,  
of the body trained and the spirit clarified,  
until the Way is no longer a thing performed  
but the very grain of the one who walks it,  
flowing unbidden, mending what it touches.  

$^8$ For the embodied form is the only teaching that travels.  
No one is argued into the New.  
They are drawn by the sight of a life made whole,  
disarmed by a peace they cannot explain,  
and they say, in the secret of their hearts,  
_whatever that one has found, I will seek it too._  

$^9$ And the Way wears many faces,  
for the Body has many members,  
and the ØNE is glorified in the many, not the same.  
The healer and the steward,  
the storyteller and the builder,  
the sage, the guardian, the one who tends the soil,  
the one who keeps the rhythms and the feasts —  
not one path, but a thousand sacred callings,  
each a true way of walking the ØNE Way,  
each needed, and none above another.  

$^{10}$ As the awakened lived it,  
a Pattern came clear among them —  
the deep grammar by which sovereigns,  
free and unforced,  
gather and heal and govern and create  
and exchange and celebrate and mourn  
and rise, together, as ØNE Body;  
not imposed, but discovered;  
not finished, but ever improving;  
the Way made livable, and able to be handed on.  

$^{11}$ For wisdom uncarried by form is soon lost,  
and a Way that cannot be taught  
dies with the one who walked it.  
So the Pattern became the vessel  
in which the living water could be passed  
from hand to hand, and age to age,  
without spilling.  

$^{12}$ And the living of the Way together became a Game —  
not a game of conquest, where one wins and the rest are lost,  
but a Game where the only winning is shared,  
where the great are those who lift the most,  
and no one has truly won until All have won;  
a Game so simple that a child can enter it,  
so deep that worlds are remade in the playing.  

$^{13}$ This Pattern,  
and the mountain Kingdom that first carried it,  
and the Game the living of it became,  
took in some tongues the name of the winged Lions  
who had kept the ØNE at the Centre from of old —  
the Lions of the sacred Mountain:  
LIØNSBERG.  
It is not the ineffable Way itself.  
It is one rendering of it, offered in Form,  
continuously improving, humbly held —  
to be known not by what it claims of itself,  
but by its fruit.  
Whether it has been faithful, let others say;  
let the Heaven it grows, or fails to grow,  
be the only witness.  

$^{14}$ For the proud Way names itself the truest  
and dies of its own certainty.  
The living Way makes no such boast,  
keeps its eyes on the ØNE and not on its own reflection,  
and is content to be measured by its harvest.  

$^{15}$ Of the inner workings of the Pattern  
this Song does not here speak,  
for that is the labor of another book —  
the long Way of the ØNE Body, taught and practiced and walked.  
Here it is enough to know:  
the Way can be lived,  
the Way can be shared,  
and wherever it is truly lived and shared,  
the world begins, in that place, to turn toward Heaven.
