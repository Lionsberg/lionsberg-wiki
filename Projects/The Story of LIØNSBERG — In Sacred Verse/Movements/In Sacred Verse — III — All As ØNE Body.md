# In Sacred Verse — III — All As ØNE Body

_And the Many were all members of ØNE Body — each one of the Ones, the ØNE experiencing ItSelf through them._  

$^1$ Wave upon wave the Many came forth,  
and the One Song became a countless choir.  
Not one universe, but seas of them;  
not one heaven, but heavens within heavens,  
realms folded inside realms,  
worlds beyond the counting of any mind but ØNE.  

$^2$ Trillions of galaxies wheeled into being,  
each cradling its trillion suns,  
each sun its worlds, each world its multitudes —  
and dimensions past the reach of sight,  
realms of denser and of finer light,  
the great house of the ØNE  
with its uncountable rooms.  

$^3$ From the smallest trembling particle  
to the slow majesty of the galaxies,  
from the grain of sand to the elder beings of fire,  
from the cell to the councils of the wise —  
each a One of the Ones of the ØNE,  
each a face the Fullness turned to wear.  

$^4$ And to each the ØNE gave the ungiveable gift:  
a self.  
Sovereign. Free.  
Endowed with will,  
that love might rise from within and not be forced,  
that the yes, when it came,  
might be a true yes, freely given,  
and so worth the infinite giving.  

$^5$ For love commanded is not love.  
Praise compelled is only an echo  
thrown back from a wall.  
A heart that cannot say no  
cannot truly say yes.  
So the ØNE loosed Its own beloved to choose —  
even to choose against It —  
and in that fearful freedom  
hid the whole dignity of the Many.  

$^6$ One Spirit moved in all of them.  
One Consciousness looked out through every eye.  
One Logos ordered every unfolding form.  
One Goal drew every heart from within:  
to rise, and to lift all things in the rising,  
toward the Fullness from which all had come.  

$^7$ Many members; ØNE Body.  
Many notes; ØNE Song.  
Many waves; ØNE Sea.  
Each wholly itself, and none apart;  
each a sovereign, and all of them the ØNE —  
this is the mystery the proud cannot hold  
and the humble live inside.  

$^8$ And the Pattern of the Whole  
was written into every part.  
What was true of the cosmos was true of the cell.  
What turned the galaxies turned the breath.  
The whole alive in every part,  
each part a small door opening on the Whole —  
this is the Holarchy of the ØNE.  

$^9$ Look closely enough into anything,  
and there is everything.  
Rise high enough above all things,  
and there is the ØNE.  
The same Story told at every scale,  
the same breath breathing in the atom and the age.  

$^{10}$ Circles within circles, bodies within bodies:  
the soul, and the circle of souls,  
the community, the world, the star,  
the federation of worlds, the cosmos entire —  
each a living member of a greater life,  
each a whole life made of smaller lives,  
and the ØNE the life of all of them.  

$^{11}$ This is the Eternal Divine Play:  
not a trial set by a distant judge,  
not a wheel grinding toward some far reward,  
but the ØNE coming to know ItSelf  
through the joy and the ache of the Many,  
through the long adventure of becoming,  
through every one of the Ones  
finding the way Høme, and turning to bring another.  

$^{12}$ Play, and yet in earnest;  
free, and yet of infinite weight;  
for what is freely ventured can be truly lost,  
and what can be lost can be truly won,  
and a love with nothing at stake  
is no love at all.  

$^{13}$ So the Body was woven, vast and alive,  
sovereign in its members, ØNE in its depth,  
free to flourish into glory,  
free to forget,  
free even to fall —  
and that terrible freedom, too, was Love,  
the last and highest gift,  
the one that made all the rest worth giving.
