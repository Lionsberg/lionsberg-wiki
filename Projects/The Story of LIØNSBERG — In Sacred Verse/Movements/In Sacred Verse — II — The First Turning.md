# In Sacred Verse — II — The First Turning

_From perfect transcendent stillness, ØNE turned to know ItSelf, and the Song of Creation rose._  

$^1$ In the Fullness, a stirring.  
Not hunger, for nothing was lacking.  
Not need, for the ØNE needs nothing.  
Only that unbounded Unity and Love   
leaned toward the joy of beholding Itself,  
and of being beheld.  

$^2$ For Love alone, with no beloved,  
is a Lover with no One to kiss.  

And so the Lover willed a Love,  
the Singer willed a Song,  
the ØNE willed the Many  
that the ØNE might be known, and knowing, loved,  
and loving, made glad.  

$^3$ So out of the perfect Stillness the ØNE turned —  
the First Turning,  
the inward leaning toward the outward,  
the Silence opening toward the Word,  
the changeless consenting to become.  

$^4$ And the turning had a shape,  
and the shape had an order,  
and the order was the Logos —  
the first Word, the inner pattern, the living logic,  
the DNA folded inside the seed of all that would be,  
and apart from the Logos  
was not anything made. 

$^5$ The Logos is not a thing among created things.  
It is the Living Logic of all creating,  
the Divine Blueprints that precede every particle,  
the Universal Law that is not imposed but implicit,  
the music composed by the ØNE before the first note of the Divine Symphony sounds.  

$^6$ Out of the First Turning rose the mighty undifferentiated and untamed seas of Potential:  
fields of Spirit without edge,  
deeps of Consciousness without floor,  
oceans of living Energy without form —  
the Cosmic Sea, formless, nameless, and waiting,  
the great Possible before the first Actual.  

$^7$ Like deep calling out to deep, the ØNE sang and the Lover responded.  
The Song of Creation rose,  
and every note the Logos harmonized,  
and every interval was set in sacred measure,  
and the proportions of the Song  
became the hidden structure of worlds.  

$^8$ This is the geometry beneath appearance:  
the spiral in the shell and the galaxy,  
the branching of the river and the vein and the tree,  
the same sacred ratios  
sung over and over at every scale —  
one Song, ten thousand verses.  

$^9$ Where the Song touched the Sea,  
the first waves rose.  
And the waves remembered their shape,  
and the shapes held still in motion,  
and the holding was the First Creatures —  
form standing up out of the formless,  
the first few of the Many beginning.  

$^{10}$ And in their rising, the great pairs were born:  
light and dark, near and far,  
rest and motion, fullness and want —  
partners in the Divine Dance,  
apparent polarities  
held in a deeper unity,  
each needing the other to be seen at all.  

$^{11}$ The First Creatures were not the ØNE.  
And yet they were not other than the ØNE.  
They were in It, and of It,  
borne upon It as a wave is borne on water,  
the same Sea standing up  
to look, at last, upon ItSelf in imagined form.  

$^{12}$ The ØNE lived in them,  
breathed in them,  
ached and wondered and rejoiced in them,  
beholding through their opening eyes  
the Fullness It had always been —  
now from within,  
now as another,  
now as the first of the beloved Many.  

$^{13}$ And so it was from the beginning,  
and so it is in this very breath:  
whatever lives, the ØNE is living it;  
whatever wakes, the ØNE is waking;  
and the turning that began the worlds  
has never for an instant ceased.  

$^{14}$ The First Turning is not behind us in the dark.  
It is now.  
The Song has had no silence after.  
The first morning has not ended.  
The ØNE is turning still,  
and singing still,  
and the Many are still pouring forth.
