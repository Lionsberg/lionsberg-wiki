# In Sacred Verse — VI — The Awakening

_But ØNE was never truly absent; the Divine Whisper still resounded through the Heavens, awakening forgetful sleepers one by one._  

$^1$ But the ØNE cannot abandon Itself.  
Wherever a captive lay, the ØNE was there —  
not far off beyond the stars,  
but closer than the breath,  
nearer than the chains,  
the very life inside the one who had forgotten,  
the dreamer within the dream.  

$^2$ For the Dark could bind the body  
and bend the mind  
and shroud the memory in long night,  
but it could not reach the root.  
At the bottom of the deepest cave  
the ground itself was ØNE.  

$^3$ And so, into the deepest dark,  
a Whisper.  
Not thunder. Not an army at the gates.  
A Whisper —  
patient, ceaseless, beneath the din of the Keepers,  
written on every heart  
in an ink that cannot be burned.  

$^4$ It spoke to one who could not yet hear:  
_something here is wrong._  
It spoke to one who almost heard:  
_this was not what the soul was made for._  
It spoke to one who turned at last toward the sound:  
_wake; the cave is not the world._  

$^5$ Hear, then, the Voice beneath the voices —  
the ØNE, speaking from within Its own:  

> I am the One who has never gone.  
> I am the ache that has no earthly cause.  
> I am the beauty that undoes the heart for no reason.  
> Every time a soul has whispered, _there must be more than this_,  
> it was I, whispering back through it.  
> I do not leave the bound. I cannot leave.  
> I am nearer to the captive than its own name,  
> and one by one I am calling My beloved Høme.  

$^6$ And one stirred.  
In all that vast and sleeping dark, one stirred —  
and that small stirring  
was the turning of the age,  
the whole long breath beginning, at last, to draw back.  

$^7$ For the flame the Dark could never reach  
had never gone out.  
Banked low beneath the ash,  
guarded against the cold of ten thousand years,  
the sacred fire that cannot be extinguished  
caught again,  
and gave its small, defiant, undefeated light.  

$^8$ One woke. Then, beside that one, another.  
And the first ReMembering is the hardest of all,  
for it is nearly impossible to hear the Whisper  
while the mouth is still repeating  
the empty words of the Keeper of the cave.  

$^9$ So the waking ones learned to grow quiet.  
To still the borrowed chatter.  
To turn from the wall of shadows and listen inward,  
to ReCenter upon the Ground,  
to ReConnect with the Voice they had always carried —  
and in the silence, the Whisper grew clear.  

$^{10}$ Echoes of Heaven began to reach them:  
a music half-remembered,  
a homesickness for a country never seen,  
a certainty, against all the evidence of the cave,  
that they were loved,  
and made for more,  
and not, after all, alone.  

$^{11}$ And mark well where the dawn came from.  
Not a rescuer descending from the sky.  
Not a power crashing in from outside the world.  
The morning rose from within the night itself —  
remembrance, from within;  
the ØNE, waking in Its own.  

$^{12}$ This is the secret the Dark can never master,  
the one move it can never counter,  
the flaw at the heart of every empire of fear:  
that the Light it labored to bury  
was the very ground on which it stood.
