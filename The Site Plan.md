## The Other Maps (each with its own job — this plan replaces none of them)

- [[The Map of the LIØNSBERG Universe]] — the *essence* map: fingerposts to the heart of the canon, for minds and agents. 
- [[The LIØNSBERG Map of Maps]] — the *outer* map: the fields where others are already at work across the Earth.

## The Tending Rule

When any surface changes, walk its row: touch everything in "keep in step with." When a new surface is born, give it three things — a row in this registry, a door in the Guide's Territory (if it belongs on the paved road), and a check against [[Reaching Us]]. And at every seasonal turning, walk the whole Plan once — the coherence pass that keeps the City rising as one.
