# Instantiation

An Instantiation is the [[Prototype]] embodied on the ground — in a specific place, among specific people, under specific conditions and constraints.

It is the final rung of [[The Creative Cascade]]: the moment the descending work leaves "the cloud" of conception and takes up residence in [[Reality]]. A community instantiates the [[LIØNSBERG System]] in its own domain ([[Instantiation Of The Prototype]]); a family instantiates a pattern of living in its own home; a builder instantiates a design on an actual site, with actual soil, actual weather, and an actual budget.

**Every Instantiation compromises the [[Ideal]] to some degree.** Local conditions demand it — capacity, geography, history, the people actually present. This is not failure, and it is not to be hidden. The deviation between the Ideal and the Instantiation is the most valuable information the whole System receives: *the compromise is the data.*

Instantiation takes three general forms: **building the [[New]] from the ground up**, **transforming and remodeling what already exists**, and **removing and replacing elements of the [[Old]] that are incompatible with the New**. Most real domains involve all three.

For this reason, every Instantiation carries two obligations in exchange for what it receives:

1. **Adapt honestly.** Take from the [[Kit of Parts]] what serves the local domain, in whatever combination and sequence fits — and let the local reality speak, rather than forcing the pattern.
2. **Send the learnings home.** What was compromised, why, and what was learned flows back up to [[The Prototype]], so that every future Instantiation inherits the aggregated wisdom of all prior ones ([[The Creative Cascade]]'s Return Flow). Learning couples to action in the same breath, and lessons attach to the Part where they were discovered ([[Prototype Control]]).

Each Instantiation remains sovereign — a whole in its own right, not a branch office of the Prototype ([[Holon]]): connected, never commanded.
