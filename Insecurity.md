# Insecurity
Insecurity is fear turned inward — the reading that one's own standing, worth, or place is not assured.
**Felt Sense.** A low chronic clench: eyes checking other faces for a verdict, a rehearsed casualness, the quiet exhaustion of constant self-monitoring. Not a spike but a climate — fear running in the background, about the self, under everything.
**What It Signals.** Insecurity reports that belonging feels contingent — that somewhere the system concluded *my place must be earned and can be lost.* It usually points backward, to rooms where love was conditional and a verdict once truly fell. And it points precisely at the places where worth is still outsourced to external judges.
**Its Gradient.** A chronic inward register of the low band — [[Unease]] and [[Anxiety]] aimed at the self. Sharpened by exposure it borders [[Shame]]; met and answered, it opens toward [[Confidence]] and settled [[Belonging]].
**Its Gift.** A map of the outsourced self. Every insecurity marks a place where worth is held in someone else's hands — and therefore exactly where [[Sovereignty]] can be reclaimed.
**Its Distortion.** Refused, it hides behind masks: perfectionism, grandiosity, the endless credential, approval sought like oxygen. Enthroned, it shrinks life to the rooms that feel safe and edits the self for every audience, until no one — ourselves included — has met the original.
**The Move.** Catch the courtroom in session: *whose verdict am I awaiting?* Then repatriate the judgment — worth is given with being, not earned and not rescindable. Practice being seen unedited in one safe [[Circle]]: insecurity heals by exposure to real [[Love]], not by winning the old trial.
