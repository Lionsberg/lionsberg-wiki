# Let's Make Reality Intersubjective Again

A rallying call for the recovery of shared Reality.

Every finite view is [[Subjective]] — a partial rendering from one vantage. Reality becomes *intersubjective* when many honest vantages are woven together: perceptions compared, descriptions tested, maps corrected against one another in good faith, until what no single view can see becomes visible to the whole. This weaving is how communities have always found their footing in what is — and it is precisely what the [[Old World]]'s fragmentation, siloing, and manufactured division have broken. When the weaving fails, each mind is left alone with its own map, and shared action towards any common [[The Goal|Goal]] becomes impossible.

To make Reality intersubjective again is therefore foundational to everything else: it is practiced in the [[Circle]] through dialogue and shared [[SenseMaking]], in the rhythm of [[OmniSpection]], and in every act of humble, honest comparison of maps. Not the collapse of perspectives into one enforced view — the *weaving* of sovereign perspectives into a living, continuously corrected, common understanding.
