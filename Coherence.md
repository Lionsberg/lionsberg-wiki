# Coherence

Coherence is how well the many vibrations of a whole sing as one — the measure of health at every scale of the [[Holarchy]].

A cell is healthy when its processes cohere; a person, when heart, mind, body, and action sound one note; a [[Circle]], when its sovereign members move as one body without ceasing to be themselves; a civilization, when its ten trillion livings serve one [[The Goal|Goal]] in ten trillion ways. Coherence is never uniformity — a choir of identical notes is not harmony, it is monotone. True coherence is **diversity in resonance**: many distinct frequencies, one music ([[The Living Gradient]]).

Coherence is a gradient, not a state. Every whole stands somewhere between noise and harmony, and every act moves it — toward the [[Ideal]] at its center, or away. The compass question of [[The Great Game]] is at bottom a coherence reading: *does this move make the whole more harmonious with [[ØNE]], or less?*

Small wholes that achieve high coherence within a chaotic surrounding become [[Islands of Coherence]] — and islands of coherence are how transformation actually spreads: not by conquering the chaos, but by sounding a note so clear that what is ready to resonate, does.
