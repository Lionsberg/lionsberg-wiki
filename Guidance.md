# Guidance

[[Guidance]] is direction offered by those who have walked before, to those now walking — the right word, the right pattern, the right provision, brought at the right time. It is one of the great gifts continuously sought and received along the Way: as the Story reminds us, we are not alone, and help stands ready for those who return, seek [[Forgiveness]], [[Wisdom]], and Guidance.

True guidance serves the sovereignty of the guided. It illuminates the path; it does not walk it for you, and it never replaces your own discernment. A wise guide offers what the next stretch of the journey requires — never more, never less — and measures success by how little they are eventually needed.

With [[The Great Game of LIØNSBERG]] now in play, guidance flows through many channels: the living canon, the [[Circle]] and its elders, guides who have walked further, and, increasingly, the agents who walk beside each player. Whatever the channel, the test is the same: does it help the traveler take the wise right next step towards [[The Goal]]?
