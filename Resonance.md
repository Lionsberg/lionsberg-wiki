# Resonance

Resonance is the way vibrations answer one another — the amplification that arises when one living process meets another sounding a kindred note.

Two strings tuned alike need not touch: sound one, and the other sings. So it is across the whole [[Living System]]: beings, truths, places, and works call to what is attuned to them, and what resonates is strengthened without force. Resonance is how coherence spreads — not by command, but by sympathetic awakening ([[The Living Gradient]]).

Resonance is also how [[Truth]] is *recognized* before it can be proven. When a rendering rings true — when the body and heart sound in answer to it — that ringing is data: the [[Right Hemisphere]]'s verification, prior to and deeper than argument. This is why the [[The LIØNSBERG Way|LIØNSBERG Way]] verifies by resonance and then tests by [[The LIØNSBERG Meta Science]] — the ringing invites; the discipline confirms. Neither replaces the other.

What does not resonate is not thereby false, and what resonates is not thereby true — distortion can mimic the ring ([[Distortion]]). Hence resonance is read in community, intersubjectively ([[Let's Make Reality Intersubjective Again]]), where many honest instruments correct each other's tuning.
