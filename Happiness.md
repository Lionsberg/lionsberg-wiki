# Happiness

The value of happiness represents a commitment to finding joy and fulfillment in life. It means pursuing activities and relationships that bring joy and fulfillment, and striving to create a sense of well-being and contentment.

## As a Felt Reading

**Felt Sense.** Sustained lightness — the background hum of a life in working order; ease in the body, readiness to smile. **What It Signals.** Happiness is the mid-band of the bright spine: steadier than [[Joy]]'s piercing note, brighter than [[Contentment]]'s quiet floor. It reports that the present shape of life — its relationships, work, and rhythms — is broadly in [[Coherence]] with what we need and love. **Its Gradient.** [[Contentment]] → [[Gladness]] → Happiness → [[Joy]] → [[Delight]] → [[Awe]] → [[Bliss]]. **Its Gift & Distortion.** The gift: a trustworthy weather-report on the life as a whole. The distortion is the [[Old World]]'s central error: happiness *pursued directly* recedes — chased as product, it becomes the pleasure-treadmill. Happiness arrives as a byproduct of meaning, love, and contribution; it cannot be purchased, only grown. **The Move.** Read what is actually working and tend it; invest in the sources (bonds, purpose, health, presence) rather than the symptom; and share it — happiness, like [[Joy]], multiplies by division ([[Emotion]]).
