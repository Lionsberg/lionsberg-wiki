# Universe

The Universe is the total manifest order — [[All]] that has taken form, at every scale, held as one.

Physically, it is everything the sciences survey and infinitely more than they have yet illuminated: the [[Observable Universe]] is the lit porch of a house whose extent remains [[Dark]] ([[Energy]]). Metaphysically, the Universe is not a container of dead objects but one [[Living System]] — the body of manifestation, emanating from, existing in, and returning to the [[ØNE]], every part interexistent with every other ([[The Holarchy of the ØNE]]).

The [[Creator's Intent]] for the Universe is the ultimate context of every smaller intent ([[Intention]]); the [[Meta Authority]] of the Universe transcends every temporal authority; and its daily gift — the income of [[Energy]] from sun and cosmos that powers all [[Life]] — is the original commons, the endowment on which every economy actually runs.

And because no mind holds the Universe whole, we inhabit it through renderings: every cosmology, every physics, every sacred story — and the [[LIØNSBERG Narrative Universe]] itself — is a [[Current Best Articulation]] of the one Universe that exceeds them all ([[Essences, Renderings, And Ineffable Referents]] · [[Map of Reality]]).
