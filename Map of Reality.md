# Map of Reality

Each of Us navigates the territory of [[Reality]] using a [[Map of Reality]] — the working model of what is, assembled from everything we have perceived, described, differentiated, categorized, and understood. Our existing [[Narrative Framework|Narrative Frameworks]] of thought are this map in action: when the map is working, we move confidently through the known; when it fails, we fall into the [[Unknown]] — and discover there was a hole in our map that we fell through.

The fall is not a punishment; it is a disclosure. It forces us to engage in the process of updating our map and story to better match the aspects of Reality revealing themselves to us. Many realizations cannot be grasped in advance at all — they emerge only from the crucible of direct engagement with life, which is why the Way insists: *to know the Way, one must live it.*

Three humilities govern every map:

1. **Every map is [[Subjective]]** — limited by the holder's ability to [[Perceive]], [[Describe]], and [[Understand]], and more or less faithful according to their [[Mastery]] of the territory in question.
2. **The map is never the territory.** Every rendering — including every page of this wiki — is a fingerpost toward what is, not a replacement for it.
3. **No map can be corrected alone.** Recognizing the inherent finitude of each individual, the map is consciously and continuously updated in community — through periodic retrospection and shared sensemaking — so that We can navigate from where We are to where We are going, taking the wise right next steps towards [[The Goal]].

A living map, humbly held and continuously updated together, is one of the most powerful instruments a sovereign being — or a civilization — can carry.
