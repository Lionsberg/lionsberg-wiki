# Perception

Perception is the receiving of Reality — the act by which a being takes in the Territory and begins to render its [[Map of Reality]].

Perception is not passive and it is not neutral. What is received depends on the instrument and its tuning: [[Attention]] chooses where perception aims; [[Values]] shape what registers as signal ([[2.9 How Values Shape Perception]]); the [[Narrative Framework]] already held decides what the incoming even *means*. Two beings stand in one room and perceive two rooms. This is the founding humility of the Quest to Perceive: **perception is not Reality** ([[2.4 Perception Is Not Reality]]) — it is a rendering, instrument-shaped, always partial.

The instrument has two modes that must serve together: the [[Right Hemisphere]]'s open receiving of the [[Whole]], and the [[Left Hemisphere]]'s focused grasp of parts ([[Mind]]). And it has an edge: the [[Boundary of Knowing]], beyond which Reality continues and perception does not — which is why the deepest disciplines of perceiving are not techniques but postures: stillness, openness, the willingness to have the map corrected ([[Attunement]] · [[We Do Not Know]]).

Perception grows. It sharpens with honesty, widens with love, and deepens in community — many honest instruments weaving their partial renderings into a common seeing no single one could achieve ([[Let's Make Reality Intersubjective Again]]). To perceive truly is the first Quest, because everything downstream — judgment, navigation, creation — inherits the quality of the seeing.
