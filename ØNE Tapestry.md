# ØNE Tapestry

The [[ØNE Tapestry]] is the living weave of [[All]] That Is — the whole within which every [[Things|Thing]], every being, every story, and every world is a thread. Nothing exists in isolation: each thread takes its meaning, strength, and beauty from its place in the weave, and the weave ItSelf flows from [[ØNE]], the Source in which all threads are united and ordered.

To perceive Things as separate is the beginning of exploration; to follow each thread back into the Tapestry is the ripening of [[Understanding]]. Every soul that weaves its unique individual and local story back into [[The Great Story]] strengthens the Tapestry — and discovers that it was never a loose thread at all.
