# Link to the Concept, Not the Coordinate

A [[Current Best Practice]] of [[The LIØNSBERG Book of Conventions]]: **in running prose, link an idea to its concept page — never to a numbered section, chapter file, or other positional address.**

Every important concept in the LIØNSBERG Universe deserves one page that holds its center — the generalized, timeless statement of the idea, the way an [[Ideal]] holds the center of its [[Categories|Category]]. That page is the concept's stable address. Around it, the concept is developed in many places: chapters of the [[LIØNSBERG Wiki Books]], sections of the Guides, outside publications, transcripts, and stories. Those are the diverse manifestations around the center.

**The practice:**

1. **Prose links point to concept pages.** When writing anywhere in the wiki, link `[[Instantiation]]`, not `[[2.0 Program Development]]`; link `[[Archetype]]`, not the chapter where archetypes are narrated.
2. **Concept pages carry a *Discussed in* footer.** At the foot of each concept page, list where the concept is developed in depth — book chapters, guide sections, outside works. The concept page is the hub; the discussions are the spokes. A reader lands at the center and descends as deep as they choose.
3. **Positional addresses appear in exactly three places:** book-internal navigation (chapters linking their neighbors and tables of contents), the *Discussed in* footers, and citations of a specific passage where that passage itself is the point.

**Why:** a numbered section is a *coordinate* — an address that depends on position and the voice of one book. Books get revised, chapters renumbered, arguments restructured; every prose link into a coordinate breaks or silently rots when they do. Concept pages are *names* — they survive every reorganization beneath them. And the hub does something no coordinate can: it accumulates coherence. Each time the concept is discussed somewhere new, one footer line makes the new discussion findable from the center — the Return Flow of [[The Creative Cascade]], applied to knowledge itself.

Like every convention, this is held as a [[Current Best Practice]] — a recommendation at the top of the holarchy, refined by the experience of all who write here.
